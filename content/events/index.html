    Automatic Events¶

    Events start on their own every 30 minutes. When one opens, the server announces it and you have 1 minute to type @eventjoin and pick it. @eventjoin also shows your current Event Points.

    Winners earn Event Points, spent at the Event Shop, the Mining Zone tool shop, Hell Raid and the Antiquity Gacha.

    The events¶

    Event How to win Prize
    Bombring Survive 5 rounds of exploding Bombrings, 30 seconds each, in quiz_02. 20 Event Points to every survivor
    Dice Stand in one of 4 boxes. A 4-sided die is thrown and the other boxes are kicked out. Last box standing wins. 20 Event Points
    Poring Catcher Dozens of Porings with fake names spawn. Kill the one named exactly Poring. Kill a wrong one and you are out. 20 Event Points
    Poring Hunter Score the most points before time runs out: Poring +1, Drops +5, Poporing -10. 20 Event Points
    Disguise The Disguise Event NPC in the Quest Room (moc_para01 31, 31) turns into a monster. Be the first to type its name in chat. 4 Event Points per round
    Monster Battle Two monsters fight in poring_w02. Bet 20 Event Points on one at the MVP Bet Girl. 100 Event Points if your monster wins

    Weekend double rates¶

    Every Friday 00:00 to Monday 00:00, Base EXP, Job EXP and drop rates (including cards) are doubled.

    Battle Rumble¶

    A GM-run event at the Novice Magic Tournament Gate (new_1-1). Only Base Level 99 Novices with an empty inventory may enter. You get random rental gear for 30 minutes, then fight to be the last one standing.

    On entry you get one random costume from each of the three lists below, plus a fixed set of gear (Advanced Paradise Shadow Shield #24693, Advanced Paradise Shadow Armor #24694, Advanced Paradise Shadow Shoes #24695, Advanced Paradise Shadow Earring #24696, Advanced Paradise Shadow Pendant #24697, Novice Rod [3] #1639). All of it is rented for 30 minutes.
