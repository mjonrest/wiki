    Druid & Alitea Gear Guide¶

    The Druid line goes Druid → Karnos → Alitea (the 4th-job tier, max Base Lv 275, Job Lv 60). Druids use daggers, one-handed axes, two-handed axes and maces, plus a shield. Every item here is checked against the Miracle item database and NPC files. Click an item to see its stats and every place to get it.

    New Alitea items

    The Alitea weapons and crowns marked New below were added on 2026-11-03. You need the latest client patch to see their names and descriptions.

    The three builds¶

    Build Main skills (Alitea) Damage Scales with Notes
    Werewolf (claw) Alpha Claw, Frenzy Fang, Savage Lunge, Primal Claw, Alpha Phase Physical POW (Savage Lunge and Alpha Claw: +10% per POW) Alpha Claw and Frenzy Fang count as melee (range 3). Savage Lunge (range 7) and Primal Claw (range 5) count as ranged, so stack both Melee and Ranged damage %. Alpha Phase raises Frenzy Fang to 7 hits.
    Wereraptor (feather) Pinion Shot, Quill Spear, Tempest Flap, Apex Phase Physical, ranged CON All range 7, so Ranged damage % is the key bonus. Apex Phase makes Tempest Flap hit 3 times.
    Nature caster Glacial Shard / Stomp / Nova, Chilling Blast (Water); Roaring Piercer, Furious Storm (Wind); Terra Wave, Solid Stomp (Earth); Gravity Hole (Neutral) Magic SPL Truth of Ice / Wind / Earth add SPL scaling. Pick one element and stack that element's magic damage %.

    The quickest way to a working Druid is the Dim Glacier set (Lv 230) plus the Alitea shadow set.

    Set bonuses to know¶

    • Dim Glacier set: Dim Glacier Axe + Armor + Boots + Manteau (physical), or Dim Glacier Mace + Robe + Shoes + Muffler (magic). Adds ATK or MATK per weapon refine, plus P.ATK/POW or S.MATK/SPL at higher enchant grades. The armor pieces drop in the Immortal instance (Ep20), and Pipiring (icas_in 170, 65) enchants them. The new Dim Glacier Nature Axe and Knife count for this set too. Easiest full set to finish.
    • Gaebolg set: Armor + Boots + Manteau adds +10% damage to all races, and Armor + Manteau adds +10% to all elements. Robe + Shoes + Muffler gives the same as magic.
    • Alitea shadow set: each M. Alitea piece paired with a Master Shadow Weapon or Shield gives +2 to all trait stats. A full set refined to a total of +27 per side ignores 50% DEF/MDEF and 20% RES/MRES.

    Progression path¶

    1. Karnos, Lv 160-200: a Vicious Mind Two-Handed Axe or Dagger from Sky Fortress, plus Temporal Circlet (Karnos) [1] #400998 (boosts Sharpen Gust and Chop Chop). Get it from HUGINN2001 in Glast Heim (glast_01 141, 291) for 5 Sealed Temporal Circlets.
    2. Alitea, Lv 200+: farm Shadow Essence at Master Shadow and build the M. Alitea shadow set, then swap two pieces for your build's skill shadows.
    3. Lv 230: the full Dim Glacier set and a Dim Glacier weapon (Axe, Nature Axe, Mace or Nature Knife). Enchant both in Icas.
    4. Lv 240-250: finish Ep21 for Wigner tickets and swap armor, garment and accessories to Gaebolg. Casters buy the Sky Glacial Scepter. Add a Yorscalp crown, or a rune crown for your build.
    5. Lv 250-265: Dimensions Nature Axe / Dagger or Muqaddas Garz, Dimension World shoes from Hotbear Mur, and the Frontier Rune Crown (Alitea) from Bitoli at Lv 265.

    The Druid Stone costume enchants (Malangdo costume enchanter) only boost 1st-job Druid skills, so they barely matter for Alitea.
