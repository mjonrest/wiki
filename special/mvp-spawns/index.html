    MVP Spawns & Respawn Times¶

    Every MVP that spawns on a map by itself, with its respawn time. The respawn timer starts when the MVP dies. A time like 2 h ~ 2 h 10 min means it comes back after 2 hours plus a random extra of up to 10 minutes.

    You can also ask Hera in Prontera whether an MVP is alive, and click any MVP for its drops and stats.

    Search

    Press ++ctrl+f++ to find an MVP or a map on this page.
