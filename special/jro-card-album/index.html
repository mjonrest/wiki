    jRO Cards from Card Albums¶

    Every Old Card Album #616 and Mystical Card Album #12246 you open has a bonus chance to give a jRO card on top of its normal card. The normal card from the album is not affected: you always get it, and the jRO card is an extra.

    When you win one, the whole server sees it: "[Old Card Album] Name obtained … (Rare - 0.50%)!"

    • The Mystical Card Album has twice the chance of the Old Card Album in every tier.
    • Within a tier, every card has the same chance.
    • Only these two albums roll a jRO card. Other card albums, including the second Old Card Album #12817 and the Headgear, Armor, Weapon (and so on) Card Albums, don't.
