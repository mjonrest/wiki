    Episode 19: Issgard, Land of Snow Flowers¶

    Maram has news from the north. The guardians of Issgard have sent an invitation, and Varmundt's airship takes you past the barrier to the Ice Castle. There the Iwins are fighting the snake-like Rgans, who are being experimented on by the Illusion scientists Juncea and Bagot. Disguised as a newborn Rgan, you sneak into the Snake's Nest and uncover what the two are doing.

    • Level: Base Level 200 or higher.
    • Starts at: the Grey Wolf Village, /navi wolfvill 223/83.
    • Main hub: the Ice Castle (icecastle and its interiors icas_in).

    Which script is loaded

    This page follows the combined quest file for Episode 19, which is the one the server loads. The split versions (separate main, misc, daily and side quest files) are switched off in the server config, so anything that exists only in them is not available here.

    Before you start¶

    • Base Level 200. Below that, the Grey Wolf Villager only tells you there are quests for level 200 and up.
    • Episode 18 finished. The story only starts once the last step of the Episode 18 main quest is done.
    • Rgan Transformation Scroll. For most of the episode you have to look like a Rgan. Rgans in the Snake's Nest (and the scouts outside it) throw anyone in human form back to jor_back3. You get your first Rgan Transformation Scroll #101162 from the story (step 14). Each one lasts 20 minutes. More can be bought from Lunch at /navi jor_back2 250/30 for 2 Low-quality Rgan Magic Core #1000707 and 2,000 Zeny each.
    • Rgan cores. The story asks for Low-quality Rgan Magic Core #1000707 three times: 10, 10 and 20. They drop from the Rgans around jor_back3, jor_dun01 and jor_dun02 (Primitive Rgan, Lowest Rgan, Junior Rgan, Intermediate Rgan). Save some extra to buy scrolls.
    • Free weight. Several NPCs refuse to continue if you can't carry the reward, so keep your inventory light.
    • A party. Iwin Patrol, Bagot Laboratory and Confused Snake's Nest all need a party. A one-person party is enough.

    Episode Skip NPC

    The custom Episode Skip NPC in Prontera (/navi prontera 164/163) can mark Episode 19 as complete if you have an Episode 19 Pass Ticket (item 1001263), are a 3rd class or higher, and are under 50% weight. It gives the story's Snow Flowers, reputation and EXP plus a bonus. The catch: it only accepts characters that used the same NPC to skip Episode 18.

    Story path¶

    Chapter 1: The invitation¶

    1. Grey Wolf Villager, Grey Wolf Village, /navi wolfvill 223/83. Maram, Miriam and Suad appear and ask you to take them to Varmundt's Mansion. You can also talk to Maram (/navi wolfvill 220/85) once he has appeared. Quest: Guest from the North Wind 1.
    2. Walk into the main building of Varmundt's Mansion (/navi ba_in01 375/102). The group gathers there. Talk to Smart Ellie at /navi ba_in01 384/114. Quest: Guest from the North Wind 2.
    3. Talk to Crux (/navi ba_in01 386/110). He takes you to Prontera Castle. Walk inside and talk to Crux at /navi prt_cas 320/270. Quest: Guardian's Box and Invitation 1.
    4. Go into the king's room (the door at prt_cas 174/342) and talk to Nihil at /navi prt_cas 331/343. You receive a Guardian Invitation #1000607. Quest: Guardian's Box and Invitation 2.
    5. Go back to Varmundt's Mansion. A scene plays when you reach /navi ba_maison 186/251. Quest: Guardian's Box and Invitation 3.
    6. Talk to Navigator Ginger (/navi ba_in01 30/264) and board the airship.
    7. On the airship, give the invitation to Navigator Ginger (/navi air_if 46/71). Quest: Guardian's Box and Invitation 4.
    8. Talk to Lehar on the airship (/navi air_if 32/63). Quest: Guardian's Box and Invitation 5.
    9. Talk to Navigator Ginger again and choose to get off. You land at jor_tail. Talk to Lehar at /navi jor_tail 221/53. Quest: Into the Ice.

    Note

    From now on Navigator Ginger on the airship and at /navi jor_tail 216/51 flies you between Varmundt's Mansion and Issgard.

    Chapter 2: The Ice Castle¶

    1. Head north through jor_back1 to the Ice Castle (the castle gate is at jor_back1 385/229). Lehar waits along the way at jor_back1 381/232. Inside, talk to Lehar at /navi icas_in 141/216 (castle entrance: icecastle 213/175). Quest: Encounter in the Ice Castle.
    2. Talk to Guardian Leon at /navi icas_in 138/220. You receive 40 Snow Flower #1000608. Leon also starts the Purification Saint side quest. Quests: Meet the Irwin Scouts. and Let's meet Voglinde.
    3. Outside, talk to the Captain of the Eagle Patrol (/navi icecastle 23/115) and sign the papers. Quest: Eagle Patrol.
    4. Make a party (one person is fine) and enter Iwin Patrol through the Captain. During the patrol, talk to The mysterious young man at 1@iwp 260/390, near the fourth checkpoint. Quest: Subject 210426 Encounter.
    5. Go to the Frozen Scale Plains and walk up to /navi jor_back2 250/30. The young man appears again. Talk to him. You receive a Rgan Transformation Scroll #101162 and 2 Snow Flower #1000608. Quests: Rgan Transformation Scroll and "[Standby] Lunch Menu Selection" (the young man, now called Lunch, becomes a daily quest NPC).
    6. Back at the Ice Castle, walk to /navi icecastle 27/126. Horuru appears. Talk to her. Quest: Accompany Reconnaissance Report.
    7. Go to the dining hall inside the castle (door at icas_in 83/233) and talk to Aurelie Petit at /navi icas_in 34/189. Quest: Reiji's Plan.

    Chapter 3: Into the Snake's Nest¶

    Stay transformed

    From here on, use a Rgan Transformation Scroll #101162 before you talk to anyone at the Rgan base or in the Snake's Nest. Without it the Rgans send you back to jor_back3 65/321.

    1. Go to the Rgan base at /navi jor_back3 99/318. Transform, then talk to Horuru. You receive 25 Snow Flower #1000608. Quest: Snake's Nest Infiltration.
    2. Enter the cave and talk to Miriam at /navi jor_dun02 263/170. You receive 25 Snow Flower #1000608.
    3. Follow the Rgans to the upper level (jor_dun02 exit at 18/31 leads to jor_nest). Talk to the Rgan Priest at /navi jor_nest 185/49.
    4. Talk to the Rgan Bishop at /navi jor_nest 196/202.
    5. Talk to the Rgan Bishop at /navi jor_nest 115/187.
    6. Talk to the Rgan Bishop at /navi jor_nest 53/244.
    7. Walk to /navi jor_nest 22/141 and talk to Juncea twice (health check, then her research). She asks for your eggshell.
    8. Go back to Miriam at /navi jor_dun02 263/170. She sends you to the higher-grade hatchery.
    9. Talk to the Rgan Priest at /navi jor_nest 23/58. You receive a Greater Rgan Eggshell #1000606.
    10. Bring the eggshell to Juncea (/navi jor_nest 22/141).
    11. Talk to Bagot next to her (/navi jor_nest 26/142).
    12. Talk to Juncea again. She gives you a Rgan name. Quest: Infiltration Interim Report.
    13. Walk outside to /navi jor_nest 82/163. Chime's robot finds you and Lazy.
    14. Go to the Illusion's airship at /navi jor_nest 24/252 and talk to Lazy.
    15. Go down to the lower level and talk to Rgan Security at /navi jor_dun02 153/239.
    16. Walk to /navi jor_dun02 150/269 and talk to Miriam. You find the humans held there.
    17. Return to the Ice Castle dining hall and talk to Aurelie Petit (/navi icas_in 34/189). You receive 50 Snow Flower #1000608, 119,725,365 Base EXP and 7,000,000 Job EXP. Quests: Aircraft Destruction Operation and Vulnerability Investigation. This unlocks Airship Destruction.

    Chapter 4: Gathering evidence¶

    1. Back in the nest (transformed), talk to Lazy at /navi jor_nest 127/207. He gives you three places to investigate. Quests: Finding Clues - Jungkea's Lab, Finding Clues - Baggot's Lab, Finding Clues - Rgan Guard.

    2. Check all six spots. The order doesn't matter.

      • Juncea's lab: walk in (around jor_nest 43/141), then search the Pile of Papers at /navi jor_nest 21/195 and /navi jor_nest 19/142.
      • Bagot's lab: step on /navi jor_nest 66/260 to be moved to jor_dun03, then search the Pile of Papers at /navi jor_dun03 74/71 and /navi jor_dun03 49/80.
      • Rgan guards: walk to jor_nest 177/176, search the Pile of Papers at /navi jor_nest 181/173 (you get a Ledger #1000845) and listen to the Rgan Guard at /navi jor_nest 173/177.

      Quest when all six are done: Share the clues. 36. Report to Lazy (/navi jor_nest 127/207). The choices in this talk don't change anything. Quest: Whispering Silently. 37. Talk to Juncea at /navi jor_nest 31/140. She wants something sweet. 38. Go to the adventurers' dorm in the Ice Castle (/navi icas_in 245/197). Ask Jaerorong or Comfort for an Ice Cookie #1000846. 39. Give the cookie to Juncea (/navi jor_nest 31/140). 40. Juncea wants more: bring 1 Ice Cookie #1000846 or 10 Low-quality Rgan Magic Core #1000707. 41. Do it again: 1 Ice Cookie #1000846 or 10 Low-quality Rgan Magic Core #1000707. The Iwins only hand out cookies during step 38, so plan on bringing 10 cores each time. 42. Keep talking to Juncea. Show her the Ledger #1000845, then listen to Bagot. Quest: Accumulating Doubts. 43. Go back to the dorm and talk to Lazy (/navi icas_in 245/197), then Miriam (/navi icas_in 249/199). Quest: Intermittent difference. 44. In Juncea's lab (transformed), check the Unnoticed Box at /navi jor_nest 19/190. 45. Take a clean tube from the Empty Test Tube at /navi jor_nest 20/194. You receive a Juncea Test Tube #1000604. 46. Use it on the Unnoticed Box again. You get a Red Liquid Test Tube #1000605. 47. A Rgan stops you at /navi jor_nest 24/185 and sends you to Bagot's laboratory.

    Chapter 5: Bagot's laboratory¶

    1. Step on /navi jor_nest 66/260 to reach jor_dun03 and talk to Juncea at /navi jor_dun03 57/63. She wants to talk to you alone.
    2. With a one-person party, talk to Juncea again to create and enter Bagot Laboratory. Talk to Juncea inside and defeat her transformed form. Then talk to her once more. You are sent back to the Ice Castle (icecastle 27/123).
    3. Talk to Horuru at /navi icecastle 27/126. Quest: Chaotic Snake Nest.
    4. Go to Horuru's house (door at icecastle 62/137) and talk to Horuru at /navi icas_in 247/116. She takes the Red Liquid Test Tube #1000605.
    5. Bring 20 Low-quality Rgan Magic Core #1000707 to Vellgunde (/navi icas_in 244/117). You receive 25 Snow Flower #1000608.
    6. Talk to Juncea (/navi icas_in 243/120).
    7. Talk to Aurelie (/navi icas_in 249/113).

    Chapter 6: The confused nest¶

    Point of no return

    Once you go through the warp below for the search, you can't enter the normal 2nd floor of the Snake's Nest until the operation is finished.

    1. Go to /navi jor_dun01 8/238 and step on the warp. An Iwin scout asks if you're ready. Say yes and you enter the nest under search (jor_que). Talk to the Iwin Recon at /navi jor_que 271/88. You receive 25 Snow Flower #1000608.
    2. Talk to Miriam at /navi jor_que 144/253.
    3. Talk to the Intermediate Rgan at /navi jor_que 131/148.
    4. Walk to /navi jor_que 36/38 and talk to Lehar (jor_que 32/37).
    5. Talk to Lehar again to create and enter Confused Snake's Nest (a party is required). Clear it. When it's over, take the warp portal Aurelie opens. It takes you to jor_back3. Quest: Those Who Vanished.
    6. Report to Aurelie Petit in the dining hall (/navi icas_in 34/189). You receive 50 Snow Flower #1000608. Episode 19 is complete.

    The story gives 242 Snow Flower #1000608 in total. It also gives Episode 19 reputation at steps 11, 17, 18, 33, 55 and 60. Each run of Iwin Patrol pays 2 Snow Flowers and EXP on top of this.

    What the story unlocks

    • After step 12: the Ice Castle dailies. Lunch's Select Menu for Lunch opens after step 14.
    • After step 22: the Snake's Nest dailies.
    • After step 33: Airship Destruction. Enter through the Rope at /navi jor_nest 22/255.
    • After step 60: the remaining dailies and side quests, the daily Simulation Battle through Arolong (/navi jor_nest 66/260), and the start of Episode 20.

    After the story¶

    • Iwin Patrol: daily, through the Captain at /navi icecastle 23/115.
    • Finding an Underground Hideout: Horuru at /navi icecastle 27/126. Bury High-sensitivity Feelers at marked spots in jor_back2 and jor_back3. You need this quest to start Episode 20.
    • Ice Castle dailies: Private Holly (icas_in 33/53), Vellgunde (icas_in 185/63), Moryara (jor_tail 48/241), the Supplier (icecastle 97/177), Guardian Leon (icas_in 42/252), Zoryara (icas_in 241/66), Friederike (icas_in 32/123), Surr (icas_in 27/116) and Tataring (icas_in 27/122).
    • Snake's Nest dailies: Rgan NPCs at jor_nest 257/26, 239/244, 37/75, 235/57 and 34/79.
    • Side quests: Purification Saint (begins with step 11), Frozen Sea (Surr, icas_in 27/116), Preaching Doctrine (jor_nest 17/97), Curious Things (jor_nest 239/239) and In Need of Help (Lehar, icas_in 141/216, after the story).
    • Shops: Hoyoyo (icas_in 175/63) trades Snow Flowers and reputation goods. Maram (icas_in 180/61) sells supplies, Snow armor and Glacier weapons.
