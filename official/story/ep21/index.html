    Episode 21: Age of Heroes¶

    A dimensional rift opens on Scale Island, and Nyar uses it to send you, Lehar and Nillem back to a time before Lasgand gained his power. In the past you join the Gaebolg Resistance in Lunaforma, earn the trust of the Richard Mercenaries, the Walter fleet in Alberta and the Wigner merchants, and sneak into the Jormungandr cult's temple at Gimli. The cult's plan to bring back Jormungandr leads to a final battle. After that, Nyar brings you home to settle things with Lasgand.

    • Level: Base Level 230 or higher.
    • Starts at: Diver Fapa, next to the airship landing in jor_tail, /navi jor_tail 233/41.
    • Main hub: Lunaforma (jor_mbase and its interior mbase_in), with Alberta (jor_albe and its interior jalbe_in) as the second town.

    Before you start¶

    • Episode 20 finished. Diver Fapa only talks about the island once the last Episode 20 step (handing the heart fragment to Voglinde) is done.
    • Base Level 230. Below that, Diver Fapa only tells you the quest is for level 230 and up.
    • A party. Gimli Infiltration, Mysterious Ghost Ship, Final Battle, Secret Altar and Silent Sanctuary all need a party. The party leader creates each one.
    • Getting around. Boats link the maps of the past:
      • Walter Sailor at /navi jor_mbase 343/148 sails to Alberta. The Walter Sailor at /navi jor_albe 201/200 sails back.
      • Walter Sailor at /navi jor_mbase 161/343 sails to the Southern Raised Land (jor_raise2). The Hidden Boat at /navi jor_raise2 100/26 sails back. The north edge of jor_raise2 (118/414) leads to the Northern Raised Land (jor_raise1).
      • Small Boat at /navi jor_mbase 121/26 goes to Scale Island (jor_crk_p). The Small Boat at /navi jor_crk_p 75/57 comes back.
      • The Warper also lists Lunaforma, Alberta (Ep21) and the Raised Land fields.

    Story path¶

    Chapter 1: Through the rift¶

    1. Diver Fapa, /navi jor_tail 233/41. Nillem, Aurelie and Lehar join you. Pick I'll go now. to sail to Scale Island (jor_crk). If you pick I'll go later., talk to him again and choose Go to Scale Island. Quest: Detected Rift on the North.
    2. Talk to Nyar at /navi jor_crk 105/108. Nyar opens a rift to the past and you land on the Scale Island of the past (jor_crk_p). Quest: Rift on Scale Island.
    3. Talk to Nyar at /navi jor_crk_p 105/108. Quest: First Meeting.
    4. Walk to /navi jor_crk_p 100/95. Ivan and the resistance soldiers find you, and you are moved to luna_sf1. Quest: Lunaforma.
    5. Talk to Ivan (/navi luna_sf1 258/151). He takes you to Lunaforma (jor_mbase 38/152).

    Chapter 2: The Gaebolg Resistance¶

    1. Talk to the Resistance Soldier (/navi jor_mbase 54/155). Quest: Gaebolg Resistance.
    2. Talk to Tris (/navi jor_mbase 56/152).
    3. Talk to the Resistance Soldier again.
    4. Talk to Tan (/navi jor_mbase 57/150). He sends you to the Richard Mercenary Group.
    5. Walk to /navi jor_mbase 313/106. Go into the mercenary building (door at jor_mbase 321/115). A short scene plays at mbase_in 289/124. Quest: Richard Mercenaries.
    6. Talk to ??? at /navi mbase_in 292/127 and say you came to see Richard. He sends you to Richard's room.
    7. Knock on Richard's door at /navi mbase_in 113/120. Nobody answers, so try the next door at /navi mbase_in 113/114. A stranger stops you there and takes you back to the office.
    8. Step back into the office (/navi mbase_in 289/124), then fill out the form at the Pile of Documents (/navi mbase_in 299/126).
    9. Talk to Valdaris (/navi mbase_in 302/123).
    10. Talk to Tan (/navi mbase_in 302/122).
    11. Valdaris shows you around Lunaforma. Walk to each spot in order: /navi jor_mbase 316/117, /navi jor_mbase 316/100, /navi jor_mbase 243/120, /navi jor_mbase 145/108 and /navi jor_mbase 135/108.
    12. The door at mbase_in 113/120 now leads into Richard's room. Talk to Valdaris (/navi mbase_in 167/128), then Richard (/navi mbase_in 168/126), then Lee (/navi mbase_in 166/124).
    13. The door at mbase_in 113/114 now opens too. Talk to Lee inside (/navi mbase_in 167/79).
    14. Walk to /navi jor_mbase 163/196. The resistance leaders are waiting.

    Chapter 3: Heine's errands¶

    1. Talk to Tris (/navi jor_mbase 168/200) until she asks you to fetch something for Heine. This takes four talks. Quest: Calculating Profit and Loss. Hunt: 10 Skipskipper in the Southern Raised Land (jor_raise2), and bring 1 Bright Eye #1001641.
    2. Bring the Bright Eye to Tris. Heine now wants you in Alberta.
    3. Sail to Alberta and talk to Nillem (/navi jor_albe 192/209). Quest: Diligent Worker of Alberta.

    4. Talk to Yohan at the port (/navi jor_albe 239/115). Deliver his cargo to both places, in any order:

      • Walter Sailor at the market near the pier, /navi jor_albe 148/235.
      • Walter Sailor in the guild warehouse, /navi jalbe_in 22/63.

      Quest: Small Cargo Transportation. 24. Report to Yohan. When Nillem complains, pick Stop Nillem. If you pick Complain to Yohan. and then I'll take a good rest., he takes the job back and you have to ask him for work and redo the deliveries. Then clean the four Pile of Dirt spots: /navi jor_albe 151/210, /navi jor_albe 211/136, /navi jor_albe 167/139 and /navi jor_albe 190/175. Quest: Thorough Port Cleaning. 25. Report to Yohan. Quest: Walter Officer Visit. 26. Talk to Iana Operta in the guild building (/navi jalbe_in 67/95). Deliver her letters, in any order: - Patissier Edel, /navi jalbe_in 126/54. - Professor Sanguin, /navi jalbe_in 29/28.

      Quest: Unexpected Letter Delivery. 27. Report to Iana Operta. Maristella Walter is there too and suggests heading to Lunaforma. Quest: News From Lunaforma.

    Chapter 4: Ivan's soup kitchen¶

    1. Back in Lunaforma, talk to Ivan (/navi jor_mbase 233/277) and ask about the ghost ship. Quest: Ivan Wigner's Joyful Missionary Life.
    2. Talk to Lehar at the soup kitchen (/navi jor_mbase 214/316) several times. You help serve the soup. When Lehar asks, talk to Ivan (/navi jor_mbase 212/311) and then Lehar again. Quests: Lehar's Fulfilling Relief Efforts (1), (2), The Cause of the Family's Ruin! and Lehar's Fulfilling Relief Efforts (3).
    3. Have a meal with Ivan (/navi jor_mbase 189/282). Quest: Ghostship Witness (1).
    4. Talk to Lyriq (/navi jor_mbase 185/311) twice. Quests: Ghostship Witness (2) and Ivan's Plan.
    5. Talk to Ivan (/navi jor_mbase 233/277). He asks you to take a package to his sister in Alberta. Quest: Errand for Ivan.
    6. Give the package to Lalaila Wigner (/navi jalbe_in 56/138). Quest: Ivan's Regret.
    7. Open the Dusty Box on the bookcase in the office next door (/navi jalbe_in 73/187). Lalaila gives you a letter for Captain Maristella Walter. Quest: The Receiver of the Letter.
    8. Give the letter to Maristella Walter (/navi jalbe_in 68/46). Quest: Ownerless Weapon.
    9. Report to Ivan in Lunaforma (/navi jor_mbase 233/277). Quest: Cult Infiltration.

    Chapter 5: Inside the Gimli temple¶

    1. Go to the Northern Raised Land and step on /navi jor_raise1 323/71. You are moved to luna_sf1. Talk to Ivan (/navi luna_sf1 49/268).
    2. Enter the temple at /navi jor_raise1 152/323 (jor_tmple1). Step on /navi jor_tmple1 186/72 to reach the group's meeting room and talk to Ivan (/navi luna_sf2 113/257). The exit at luna_sf2 105/253 takes you back into the temple.
    3. Ask around the temple, in this order. Each spot moves you into a room in luna_sf2:
      • Step on /navi jor_tmple1 110/190, then talk to the Believer at /navi luna_sf2 45/152.
      • Step on /navi jor_tmple1 186/228, then talk to the Believer at /navi luna_sf2 118/265.
      • Step on /navi jor_tmple1 238/268, then talk to the Believer at /navi luna_sf2 196/257.
    4. Go back to the meeting room (jor_tmple1 186/72) and talk to Ivan (/navi luna_sf2 113/257). The group decides to come back better prepared. Quest: Recruiting Talent for Success.
    5. Talk to Tris in Lunaforma (/navi jor_mbase 143/272).
    6. Take the Small Boat to Scale Island and talk to Tan (/navi jor_crk_p 71/86).
    7. Talk to Ivan (/navi jor_crk_p 69/85).
    8. Back in Lunaforma, talk to Ivan (/navi jor_mbase 166/302). Quest: 2nd Floor Cult Infiltration.
    9. Step on jor_raise1 323/71 again and talk to Tan (/navi luna_sf1 51/267).
    10. Step on /navi jor_tmple1 238/268 and talk to Nadoyo (/navi luna_sf2 187/254).
    11. Talk to the Guard (/navi luna_sf2 198/263) to register. Talk to him again: the party leader picks Finalize the registration. to create Gimli Infiltration, then everyone picks Can we head in now? to enter. At the end you come out at jor_raise1 318/66.
    12. Step on jor_raise1 323/71 and talk to Nadoyo (/navi luna_sf1 48/265).

    Chapter 6: The ghost ship¶

    1. Talk to Tris (/navi jor_mbase 143/272).
    2. In the central building (door at jor_mbase 204/196), the door at mbase_in 94/141 is now open. Talk to Wilhelm (/navi mbase_in 116/186).
    3. Talk to Reinhardt (/navi mbase_in 118/186). Quest: Ghost Ship Report.
    4. Sail to Alberta and talk to Maristella Walter (/navi jalbe_in 68/46). Quest: Mysterious Ghost Ship.
    5. Talk to Maristella Walter at the harbor (/navi jor_albe 190/186). The party leader picks Set coordinates. to create Mysterious Ghost Ship, then pick Investigate the Mysterious Ghost Ship. to enter. At the end you are back at jor_albe 191/182. Quest: Ghost Ship Identity.
    6. Talk to Maristella Walter again. Quest: Message from a Wizard.
    7. Talk to the Wigner Executive (/navi jor_albe 191/183). Quest: Return to Lunaforma.

    Chapter 7: Heine's tablet¶

    1. Talk to Tris in the central building (/navi mbase_in 92/123) twice. Quest: Black-Haired Beast.
    2. Take the tablet to the room on the left (door at mbase_in 68/128) and talk to the Heine Tablet (/navi mbase_in 21/168). Heine asks for 2 Vellelopy #1001643. Velelling in the Northern Raised Land drop them.
    3. Bring them to the Heine Tablet and keep talking to it. After the third talk you receive Heine's Tablet #1001627.
    4. Talk to Tris (/navi mbase_in 92/123). News arrives and everyone runs outside.
    5. Talk to Nadoyo outside the central building (/navi jor_mbase 201/192). He saw a giant serpent. Quest: Gathering Eyewitness.

    Chapter 8: The giant serpent¶

    1. Talk to three witnesses, in any order:

      • Exhausted Soldier, /navi jor_mbase 189/249. Quest: Eyewitness 1.
      • Cult Member Escapee, /navi jor_mbase 218/298. Quest: Eyewitness 2.
      • Wounded Soldier, /navi jor_mbase 60/196. Quest: Eyewitness 3.

      Quest when all three are done: Gathering Eyewitness Complete! 62. Report to Tris (/navi jor_mbase 204/193). Quest: Find Tan and Nadoyo. 63. Click the Rock Crevice at /navi jor_raise1 199/190 and talk to Nadoyo (/navi luna_sf1 253/257). Quest: Suspicion Location in the Temple. 64. Click the Decorative Door at /navi jor_raise1 128/326 and talk to the Cult Priest (/navi luna_sf2 270/162). Quest: Serpent's Lair? 65. Talk to Nadoyo (/navi luna_sf2 268/158). He sends you back to Lunaforma. 66. Report to Tris (/navi jor_mbase 204/193). You are told to stay out of the coming battle. Quest: Excluded from the Final Battle. 67. In the central building, click the Small Door (/navi mbase_in 85/141) and talk to Nyar (/navi mbase_in 64/184). Quest: I can't miss it! 68. Click the Decorative Door at jor_raise1 128/326 again, pick Go in now. and talk to Nadoyo (/navi luna_sf2 254/162). Quest: Join the Final Battle. 69. Go through the Door at /navi luna_sf2 252/164, walk to /navi luna_sf2 260/45 and talk to Heine (luna_sf2 260/46). Quest: Final Battle. 70. Talk to Heine again to create and enter Final Battle (the party leader creates it with Hopi-hop spacial jump., then everyone picks Begin the Final Battle). At the end you are moved to luna_sf2 252/147. Quest: Cat Led Adventures.

    Chapter 9: Back to the present¶

    1. Talk to Nyar (/navi luna_sf2 254/150). Nyar drags you to a hidden room. Talk to Nyar there (/navi luna_sf2 258/257). Quest: Suspicious Book of Gimli.
    2. The party leader presses the Suspicious Book (/navi luna_sf2 255/257). This creates Secret Altar and a Shining Door appears. Quest: Shining Door.
    3. Enter through the Shining Door (/navi luna_sf2 250/257). Quest: Finally, A Real Mission!
    4. At the end of the altar you land at jor_crk_p 108/105. Quest: Successfully Blocked the Source.
    5. Talk to Nyar (/navi jor_crk_p 105/108). Heine asks you to swap his tablet for a new one hidden near the Lunaforma dock. Quest: Heine's Gift.
    6. Take the Small Boat to Lunaforma and search the Bushes at /navi jor_mbase 129/43. Your Heine's Tablet #1001627 is swapped for a Costume: Heine’s Tablet #410308 and you are sent back to jor_crk_p 79/69. Quest: Return to Scale Island.
    7. Talk to Nyar (/navi jor_crk_p 105/108) and pick Return to the original time. Back in jor_crk, step on /navi jor_crk 137/138. You are moved to jor_twig. Quest: Time to Confirm Casualty.
    8. Talk to the Nyar Clone (/navi jor_twig 122/164). Quest: Settling the Curse. This has a 10 minute wait.
    9. After the wait, talk to the Nyar Clone again. Quest: Lasgand?
    10. Talk to the Nyar Clone to create (Ok! Connect me to the 'Silent Sanctuary'!) and enter (Enter the 'Silent Sanctuary'!) Silent Sanctuary. At the end you are back at jor_twig 122/158. Quest: Butterfly Effect.
    11. Talk to the Nyar Clone. You are sent to jor_crk. Quest: End of the Heroes Era.
    12. Talk to Nyar (/navi jor_crk 105/108). Episode 21 is complete.

    What the story unlocks

    • After step 21: Nyar at /navi jor_mbase 220/165 and /navi jor_albe 62/150 opens your storage for 500 Zeny. Counselor Wool (/navi jor_albe 160/137) and Volunteer Vallen Wok (/navi jor_mbase 213/280) start their quest lines, which lead to dailies.
    • After step 51: the Lugenburg Brothers side story (see below).
    • After step 55: Nillem at /navi jor_albe 195/179 offers repeat runs of Mysterious Ghost Ship (Old Walter Memory Fragments, once a day; the wait ends at 04:00).
    • After step 82:
      • Final Battle Normal from Heine (/navi luna_sf2 260/46), once a day (the wait ends at 04:00). Hard mode needs 1,000 reputation with all seven Episode 21 factions and can be run once every three days (ends at 04:00). Normal and Hard each have their own cooldown.
      • The Episode 21 shops in Lunaforma: Wigner Merchant Popo (/navi mbase_in 220/127), Mage Lobelia (/navi mbase_in 228/131, ticket exchange and Gaebolg enchants), Wigner Merchant Operator (/navi mbase_in 232/122) and Blacksmith Sansan (/navi mbase_in 232/125).
      • Yorscalp (jor_sklf1), through /navi jor_raise1 113/334.
      • Valdaris (/navi mbase_in 302/123) starts We're the Mercenaries, but..., which opens six mercenary dailies. Lee (/navi mbase_in 298/121) gives the Yorscalp dailies and weeklies at Base Level 240 and 250.
      • Mandel (/navi jor_mbase 217/172) starts Supply Procurement, which becomes a daily.
      • The Heine Tablet (/navi mbase_in 21/168) gives the daily Overlooked Efforts (3 Bright Eye #1001641).
      • Ivan (/navi jor_mbase 233/277) starts Unexpected Request, a short follow-up with Lalaila in Alberta.

    After the story¶

    • Lugenburg Brothers starts with Wilhelm (/navi mbase_in 116/186) once step 51 is done. Talk to Reinhardt (/navi mbase_in 118/186), Wilhelm (/navi jor_mbase 140/201), Reinhardt again, Tris (/navi jor_mbase 197/277), Reinhardt (/navi jor_mbase 194/279), Wilhelm (/navi jor_mbase 196/280) and finally Tris. It gives 10 Wigner Premium Exchange Ticket #1001618, 91,298,960 Base EXP and 59,995,050 Job EXP, and opens Wilhelm's dailies Safe Escape Route 1 and 2 (clear the Northern or Southern Raised Land) at jor_mbase 140/201.
    • Libra at /navi jor_base 318/138 starts a side quest once you have reached step 8.
