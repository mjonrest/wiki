    Chapter 1: Call of the World Tree¶

    Dimensional rifts are opening all over the world, and the World Tree is calling adventurers to Ashridge, a village on its branches. From there you explore the Twisted Land of Darkness, help a plague-stricken Geffen in the second world, track the Immortal Legion into the Dark Whisper, meet the gray valkyries of Brimir and find a cure in Muspelheim, before you join Est to infiltrate the Dark Whisper itself.

    • Level: Base Level 200 or higher.
    • Starts at: the Small Ash Tree outside Prontera, /navi prt_fild05 353/252, or the one outside Payon, /navi pay_fild01 308/341.
    • Main hub: Ashridge (ygg_edge and its fruit rooms ygg_fruit), with Old Geffen (ch1_geffen and its interiors ch1_gef_in) as the second base.

    Before you start¶

    • Base Level 200. The Small Ash Tree only checks your level. You do not need to finish Episode 21 first.
    • 5 Dragon Scales. Debris asks for 5 Dragon Scale #1036 early in the story (step 13).
    • A party for Ominous Dark Whisper at the end of the story. The party leader creates it.
    • Getting around. Ashridge has fixed doors into its fruit rooms: the Sour Fruit (ygg_edge 164/140, Lapine Shasha), the Long Fruit (ygg_edge 150/127, Debris), the Fresh Fruit (ygg_edge 72/154, Assistant Baily), the Inn (ygg_edge 88/184) and the Shops (ygg_edge 139/211). Naviel (/navi ygg_edge 167/182) marks any of them on your mini-map. The Land of Darkness gate is at ygg_edge 31/117. From the Twisted Land of Darkness, the red gates lead to Hem Field (ygg_roots 157/242), the Unknown Ruins (ygg_roots 34/126) and Muspelheim (ygg_roots 343/252, too hot until step 62). Old Geffen is through the west edge of Hem Field (hem_fild 31/193).

    Story path¶

    Part 1: Call of the World Tree¶

    1. Click the Small Ash Tree at /navi prt_fild05 353/252 (or /navi pay_fild01 308/341). Lapine Lara invites you to Ashridge and sends you there. Quest: Call of The World Tree (1).
    2. Talk to the Lapine Messenger (/navi ygg_edge 253/246). She sends you to the Sour Fruit room. Quest: Call of The World Tree (2).
    3. Talk to Lapine Shasha in the Sour Fruit room (/navi ygg_fruit 80/122). Quest: Call of The World Tree (3).
    4. Talk to Maysel next to her (/navi ygg_fruit 82/120). Quest: Twisted Land of Darkness.

    Part 2: The Twisted Land of Darkness¶

    1. Take the Land of Darkness gate (ygg_edge 31/117). Walk over the three Investigation Points in order: /navi ygg_roots 334/138, /navi ygg_roots 299/59 and /navi ygg_roots 186/117. Quest: Regeneration (every step of this part uses this title).
    2. Click the Source of Sound (/navi ygg_roots 167/135), then talk to Yvain next to it (ygg_roots 166/135) and pick Lift the roots.
    3. Talk to Magi at /navi ygg_roots 153/203, then Magi at /navi ygg_roots 161/237.
    4. Take the Hem Field gate (ygg_roots 157/242) and talk to the Investigator at /navi hem_fild 208/22. Go back to Magi (/navi ygg_roots 161/237).
    5. Talk to the Investigator at /navi ygg_roots 112/246, then Mark at /navi ygg_roots 39/268, Tamarin at /navi ygg_roots 91/149 and Tamarin at /navi ygg_roots 40/129.
    6. Take the Unknown Ruins gate (ygg_roots 34/126) and talk to Alf (/navi uknw_ruin 244/239). Go back to Tamarin (/navi ygg_roots 40/129).
    7. Talk to the Investigator at /navi ygg_roots 204/178, the Investigator at /navi ygg_roots 297/200 and Mark at /navi ygg_roots 338/243. Quest: To Ashridge.

    Part 3: Fire resistance¶

    1. Talk to Lapine Shasha (/navi ygg_fruit 80/122). Debris needs material for a heat resistance potion. Quest: Fire Resistance Material.
    2. Bring 5 Dragon Scale #1036 to Debris in the Long Fruit room (/navi ygg_fruit 71/78). He sends you to the second world's Geffen. Quest: To Fallen Geffen.

    Part 4: The second world's Geffen¶

    1. Walk to /navi ygg_roots 164/231. Ascetic Jeon appears there. Talk to him. Quest: Parallel World Geffen (every step up to step 18 uses this title).
    2. In Hem Field, step on /navi hem_fild 102/161. You join the meeting in ch1_sf03. Talk to Reno (ch1_sf03 190/257) and pick Then, I'll go to Geffen. The exit at ch1_sf03 174/246 takes you back to Hem Field.
    3. Enter Old Geffen through hem_fild 31/193. Walk to /navi ch1_geffen 190/123 and talk to the Geffen Resident there twice.
    4. Talk to the Geffen Resident at /navi ch1_geffen 149/55. Pick I'll go to the Geffen Tower.
    5. Step on /navi ch1_geffen 120/115 at the Geffen Tower. Talk to Charles (ch1_geffen 120/115), then Clover (ch1_geffen 122/118), then Ascetic Jeon (ch1_geffen 117/117). He sends you to the clinic.

    Part 5: The clinic¶

    1. Enter the clinic (door at ch1_geffen 172/174) and talk to Doctor Neumann (/navi ch1_gef_in 70/64). He asks you to check the patients in the back rooms. Quest: Clinic Patients Examination (five of them).

    2. Visit the five patients, in any order:

      • Coughing Patient, /navi ch1_gef_in 109/39.
      • Bath Craved Patient, /navi ch1_gef_in 104/33. Fetch a towel from the Clinic Volunteer (/navi ch1_gef_in 115/40) and bring it back. Quest: Warm Towel.
      • Skinny Patient, /navi ch1_gef_in 65/112. Ask the Clinic Volunteer at /navi ch1_gef_in 113/102 for food, bring it back, and report the symptoms to Doctor Neumann at /navi ch1_gef_in 81/74. Quests: Request for Extra Rations, Extra Rations, Reporting Patients Symptoms.
      • Young Patient, /navi ch1_gef_in 108/106. Ask the Clinic Volunteer at ch1_gef_in 113/102, then go back to the patient. Quests: Sweet Food in a Dream, Sweet Food in Reality.
      • Elderly Patient, /navi ch1_gef_in 105/104.

      Once all of them are done, you are pointed to Einhar. Quest: Soren Syndrome Information. 21. Talk to Einhar (/navi ch1_gef_in 75/58) and ask about all three topics. Quests: Visiting the Acolyte's Clinic and Visiting the Wizard's Clinic. 22. Talk to Gregor in the Acolyte's Clinic (door at ch1_geffen 98/141, /navi ch1_gef_in 29/174) and Einhar in the Mage's Clinic (door at ch1_geffen 73/194, /navi ch1_gef_in 70/170). Quest: Research Clue. 23. Go to the city hall (door at ch1_geffen 102/197) and talk to Gregor (/navi ch1_gef_in 179/193). He wants three blood samples. Quests: Collecting Patient's Blood, Collecting Non-Infected Blood, Collecting Recovered Patient's Blood. 24. Collect them in any order: - Talk to Doctor Neumann at /navi ch1_gef_in 81/74, then the Patient in Care next to him (ch1_gef_in 79/73). - Talk to Aire in the street (/navi ch1_geffen 180/147). - Talk to Dapan (door at ch1_geffen 43/85, /navi ch1_gef_in 75/143). 25. Hand each sample to Einhar (/navi ch1_gef_in 183/194) or Gregor (ch1_gef_in 179/193) in the city hall. Once all three are in, talk to Gregor. Quests: Research's Conclusion, then What Dr.Neumann Doesn't Know. 26. Step on Doctor Neumann's house at /navi ch1_geffen 176/66. Go inside (door at ch1_geffen 182/59) and talk to Gregor (/navi ch1_gef_in 111/169), then Einhar further in (/navi ch1_gef_in 144/171). Quests: What Dr.Neumann is Hiding, Something that Dr.Neumann is Hiding, then To The Clinic. 27. Walk to /navi ch1_geffen 169/171 outside the clinic and talk to Ascetic Jeon (ch1_geffen 167/173). He has found a way through the barrier underground. Quest: Between Civilization and Barbarism. 28. Step on ch1_geffen 120/115 again and talk to Clover (ch1_geffen 122/118).

    Part 6: The decayed Verus¶

    1. The warp at ch1_geffen 120/123 now takes you to the decayed Verus (hem_dun01). Talk to Charles (/navi hem_dun01 25/116).
    2. Step on /navi hem_dun01 85/139. In ch1_sf03, talk to Clover (ch1_sf03 264/171), Sua (ch1_sf03 265/173) and Parsifal (ch1_sf03 261/175). The exit at ch1_sf03 271/189 takes you back.
    3. Step on /navi hem_dun01 182/199. In ch1_sf03, talk to Charles (ch1_sf03 107/173), Clover (ch1_sf03 116/181), Parsifal (ch1_sf03 111/181) and Ascetic Jeon (ch1_sf03 114/183). The exit at ch1_sf03 103/169 takes you back.
    4. Walk to /navi hem_dun01 220/210 and talk to Charles (hem_dun01 221/209). He takes you inside the Dark Whisper (ch1_dw). Quest: Sky Fortress' Dark Whisper.
    5. Talk to Ascetic Jeon (ch1_dw 260/295). Take the warp at ch1_dw 259/352 and talk to Clover (ch1_dw 269/67). He sends you to look for samples.
    6. Find both, in any order:
      • The Advanced Devices past the warp at ch1_dw 256/69. Only the one at ch1_dw 217/87 holds a sample; the others (217/96, 192/84, 210/90) are empty.
      • The three Immortal Corps past the warp at ch1_dw 275/69, at ch1_dw 340/85, 340/82 and 340/78. Searching each one spawns a monster. After all three you get the sample.
    7. Bring the samples to Ascetic Jeon (ch1_dw 264/66). You are sent back to hem_dun01 226/212.
    8. Step on hem_dun01 182/199 again and talk to Clover (ch1_sf03 108/171).
    9. Go back to Old Geffen, step on ch1_geffen 120/115 and talk to Clover (ch1_geffen 122/118). Quest: Plague's Essence.
    10. In the city hall, talk to Einhar (/navi ch1_gef_in 173/186), then Ascetic Jeon (ch1_gef_in 175/188). Quest: Plague's Identity.

    Part 7: Under the clinic¶

    1. Talk to the Wandering Patient in the clinic (/navi ch1_gef_in 75/66). Quest: The Locked Door, Opened.
    2. Take the door at ch1_gef_in 52/65 and step on /navi ch1_gef_in 26/70. Go down the stairs at ch1_gef_in 25/64 and click the Doorway (/navi ch1_gef_in 26/34). Quests: Art of Eavesdropping, then Urgent Rescue.
    3. Step on the door at /navi ch1_gef_in 29/22 and talk with Einhar. Then step into the portal that opens at ch1_gef_in 29/29. It drops you in Ashridge.
    4. Go to the Inn (door at ygg_edge 88/184) and talk to Ascetic Jeon (/navi ygg_fruit 40/72).
    5. Go back to the clinic basement and through ch1_gef_in 29/22, then take the corpse room door at ch1_gef_in 67/29 into ch1_que01. Quest: Disturbing Traces.
    6. Follow the trail: Gregor (ch1_que01 141/114), Ascetic Jeon (/navi ch1_que01 126/57), Ascetic Jeon (/navi ch1_que01 51/46), then Einhar (/navi ch1_que01 67/128). Quests: Ominous Traces, Unsettling Traces, What's at the Traces' End, then Searching for a Better Solution.
    7. Take the warp at ch1_que01 130/119 back and talk to Maxim (/navi ch1_gef_in 69/33). Quest: To Ashridge.

    Part 8: The Illusion Book¶

    1. Talk to Professor Telcos (/navi ygg_edge 209/209). Click the Illusion Book next to him (ygg_edge 211/211) to enter the record (ch1_vrgef2). Quest: Fantasy Series Exploration.
    2. Follow Ziegler: step near /navi ch1_vrgef2 139/199, then /navi ch1_vrgef2 190/137, then /navi ch1_vrgef2 50/93, and talk to him each time. Quests: Matthias Ziegler, We Met Again., We Met Once Again.
    3. Talk to Ziegler in the room at /navi ch1_vrgef1 66/137. Examine the Bed (75/140), Water Container (62/140), Clock (62/145), Desk (76/144), Wardrobe (77/137), Barrels (63/137) and Shelf (67/145). On the objects that ask, keep examining and only pick Call Ziegler when you reach something odd. Then talk to Ziegler and leave. Quests: Explore? Observe?, then See You Outside.
    4. Talk to Ziegler at ch1_vrgef2 50/93, step on /navi ch1_vrgef1 138/146 to watch a scene, then talk to Ziegler at /navi ch1_vrgef2 173/64. Quests: Farewell Without Promise, Unwanted Witness, then Completed Fantasy Series Exploration.
    5. Report to Professor Telcos (/navi ygg_edge 209/209). Quest: Truth About The Doctor.

    Part 9: The doctor and the nurse¶

    1. In the city hall, step on ch1_gef_in 182/182 and talk to Maxim (/navi ch1_gef_in 172/187). Quests: What Kind of Person is The Doctor? 1, 2 and 3.
    2. Ask the three Geffen Residents at /navi ch1_geffen 183/161, /navi ch1_geffen 61/92 and /navi ch1_geffen 129/197, then report to Maxim. Quests: Truth About The Doctor, then The Epidemic, Doctor, and Nurse.

    3. In Doctor Neumann's house, the door at ch1_gef_in 138/162 is now open. Talk to Nurse Margot (/navi ch1_gef_in 142/143) four times. Then:

      • Click the Water Kettle (/navi ch1_gef_in 104/173) and talk to Nurse Margot twice.
      • Click Neumann's Record (/navi ch1_gef_in 112/175) and pick Lift the mattress, then talk to Nurse Margot.

      Quests: The Doctor and Nurse's Story, then Cure Creation's Hope. 54. Talk to Assistant Baily in the Fresh Fruit room (/navi ygg_fruit 103/73), Nurse Margot (ch1_gef_in 142/143) and Assistant Baily next to her (ch1_gef_in 140/141). Back in Ashridge, click Baily's Shelf (/navi ygg_fruit 109/76) and pick Place the special blood sample. Quest: To Brimir.

    Part 10: Brimir¶

    1. Take the Unknown Ruins gate (ygg_roots 34/126). Elise Isha and Maura wait by the entrance (/navi uknw_ruin 256/238). Talk to either one. You are taken to Brimir (ch1_sf02).
    2. Talk to Elise Isha (ch1_sf02 185/40), One (ch1_sf02 184/41), Elise Isha again and Zero (ch1_sf02 189/44). Quests: The Gray Valkyrie, Land Without Divinity, Remaining Valkyries, then Brimir Exploration.

    3. Explore three places in the Unknown Ruins, in any order. Each one has an NPC to click (Enter.) that takes you to a part of Brimir, where a flower-shaped NPC (its name shows only as symbols) waits:

      • Dry Branch at /navi uknw_ruin 149/150, flower at ch1_sf02 114/46. Talk to it twice. Quest: Meaning of God's Love.
      • Broken Branch at /navi uknw_ruin 252/59, flower at ch1_sf02 111/271. Quests: Alice's Ears and Severed World.
      • Rotten Branch at /navi uknw_ruin 93/174, flower at ch1_sf02 45/54. Quest: Flow of Time.

      Quest when all three are done: To The Plaza. 58. Click One at /navi uknw_ruin 160/160 to reach the plaza, then talk to One (ch1_sf02 160/160). Hunt: 10 Shadow Jailers and bring 5 Broken Petal #1001976. Shadow Jailers live in uknw_ruin and uknw_ruin2. Quest: What They Want. 59. Report to One. Then visit the three flowers again, in this order: ch1_sf02 114/46 (through uknw_ruin 149/150), ch1_sf02 111/271 (through uknw_ruin 252/59) and ch1_sf02 45/54 (through uknw_ruin 93/174). Quests: Before The Flower Blooms (1), (2) and (3), then Colorful Flowers. 60. Talk to One at the plaza (ch1_sf02 160/160). Quest: Return to Ashridge.

    Part 11: Muspelheim¶

    1. Talk to Lapine Shasha (/navi ygg_fruit 80/122) twice. Quests: Bad News, then Good News.
    2. Talk to Debris (/navi ygg_fruit 71/78). His potion lets you into Muspelheim now. Quest: Land of Fire.
    3. Take the Muspelheim gate (ygg_roots 343/252) and walk over the Survey Point (/navi mu_fild01 95/154). Talk to Chez inside (ch1_sf01 190/214). Quest: Cool Headed and Warm Feet (every step up to step 66 uses this title).
    4. Visit these three in this order. Each one in Muspelheim Field takes you inside, where you talk to them again:
      • Harban at /navi mu_fild01 151/221, then Harban at ch1_sf01 55/214.
      • Tamarin at /navi mu_fild01 222/244, then Tamarin at ch1_sf01 265/226.
      • Yuvilus at /navi mu_fild01 198/186, then Yuvilus at ch1_sf01 110/227 (pick Talk about the energy fusion).
    5. Collect Hal from the eight Workers in order: /navi mu_fild01 237/299, 220/313, 166/370, 134/311, 126/306, 35/194, 91/196 and 121/236.
    6. Talk to Yuvilus (ch1_sf01 110/227), then Harban (ch1_sf01 120/224, pick Show the blemished Hal), then Yuvilus again. The special Hal can make the cure. Quest: Cure Creation.

    Part 12: The cure¶

    1. Talk to Assistant Baily (/navi ygg_fruit 103/73, pick Show the special Hal), Maxim in the city hall (/navi ch1_gef_in 172/187), Nurse Margot (/navi ch1_gef_in 142/143) and Maxim in her room (ch1_gef_in 139/142). Read Maxim's Journals in the city hall (/navi ch1_gef_in 175/187) and report to Maxim (ch1_gef_in 139/142). Quests: Distribution of the Cure 1, 2 and 3.
    2. Give the cure to the Weak Patient (/navi ch1_gef_in 61/76), the Skinny Patient (/navi ch1_gef_in 107/33) and the Drowsy Patient (/navi ch1_gef_in 110/102) in the clinic.
    3. Talk to Maxim in the city hall (/navi ch1_gef_in 172/187) twice. Quest: Destroying the Sky Fortress.

    Part 13: The Dark Whisper¶

    1. In the decayed Verus, walk to /navi hem_dun01 207/246 and talk to Est (hem_dun01 209/248). Quest: Disgusting Place's Infiltration.
    2. Make a party and talk to Est again to create and enter Ominous Dark Whisper. Inside, the party leader talks to Est to start. Stay close to her to keep the infected disguise, clear the infected and defeat the Immortal Monarch. Talk to Est at the end. Quests: Battle With The Dark Whisper, then Return to The Village.
    3. Talk to Lapine Shasha (/navi ygg_fruit 80/122). Chapter 1 is complete. Quest: Summoned By The Magic Professor.
    4. Talk to the Wizard Professor at /navi hem_dun01 205/248 (step on hem_dun01 207/246 to make him appear). From now on he runs Simulated Dark Whisper.

    Zero Cell extension¶

    Ruined Verus (ch1fild1) hides an abandoned homunculus laboratory. Newt sends you into its four sectors, and Bitoli in Ashridge crafts and enchants Rune Crowns.

    Unlocking it. You need Chapter 1 complete, Hazy Gate done and Base Level 260.

    1. In Ashridge, walk to /navi ygg_edge 173/185. Robin appears. Talk to him. Quest: Abandoned Laboratory Zero Cell (every step up to step 4 uses this title).
    2. Go to the Twisted Land of Darkness and take the passage at ygg_roots 151/345 into Ruined Verus. Walk to /navi ch1fild1 168/38 and talk to Newt.
    3. Talk to Robin at /navi ch1fild1 101/84.
    4. Talk to Robin at /navi ch1fild1 25/132, next to the scattered documents.
    5. Talk to Robin at ch1fild1 101/84 twice. Quest: Abandoned Laboratory Cleanup.
    6. Talk to Newt next to him (/navi ch1fild1 106/84). Quest: Laboratory Entry.

    Entering the sectors. Newt (ch1fild1 106/84) sends you to the east or west section of a sector. You need a protective buff that lasts one hour. Newt sells it for Root Gold Coin #1001972:

    Sector Map Base Level Buff cost
    Lux ch1zero1 260 50 Root Gold Coin #1001972
    Nox ch1zero2 260 50 Root Gold Coin #1001972
    Vita ch1zero3 270 70 Root Gold Coin #1001972
    Arc ch1zero4 270 70 Root Gold Coin #1001972

    The exit of every sector leads back to ch1fild1 104/26.

    Repeatable quests. Robin (ch1fild1 101/84) gives one cleanup quest per sector. Defeat the listed number of monsters in that sector and report back to him. Each cleanup can be done once a day: after you hand it in, it opens again at 04:00 server time. There are no weekly quests.

    Quest Base Level Hunt Reward
    Zero Cell Lux Cleanup 260 300 monsters in ch1zero1 15 Root Gold Coin #1001972, 1 Tangled Singularity #105161
    Zero Cell Nox Cleanup 260 300 monsters in ch1zero2 15 Root Gold Coin #1001972, 1 Tangled Singularity #105161
    Zero Cell Vita Cleanup 270 100 monsters in ch1zero3 20 Root Gold Coin #1001972, 1 Boundary Singularity #105162
    Zero Cell Arc Cleanup 270 100 monsters in ch1zero4 20 Root Gold Coin #1001972, 1 Boundary Singularity #105162

    Rune Crown enchant. Bitoli in the Shops room (/navi ygg_fruit 162/126) talks to you once you have finished Zero Cell Lux Cleanup. He sells Magic Tuning Formulas, and at Base Level 265 he also crafts Rune Crowns (one shop per class group) and enchants them. The enchant works on every Time Dimensions Rune Crown and Frontier Rune Crown. Slots are filled in the order 3, 2, 1:

    Slot Cost Possible enchants
    3 500,000 Zeny, 10 Boundary Magic Powder #1001997, 2 Abyss Magic Rune #1001555 STR, INT, VIT or LUK +1 to +5
    2 1,500,000 Zeny, 3 Boundary Magic Stone #1001998, 3 Rune of Chaotic Magic Power #1002012, 1 Rune of Heterogeneous Magic Power #1002015 Time Dimension Jewel, Lv 1 to 3
    1 100,000,000 Zeny, 85 Rune of Heterogeneous Magic Power #1002015, 100 Essence of Chaotic Magic Power #1002013 Your choice of Acute, Mettle, Tenacity, Master Archer, Magic Essence, Spell, Adamantine, Affection, Fierce Attack or Great Craftsman Jewel Lv 1

    Slot 1 has no random roll: you pick the jewel. A reset always succeeds and costs 1,000,000 Zeny, 5 Zelunium #25731, 5 Shadowdecon #25729 and 15 Mana Ring #1001996.
