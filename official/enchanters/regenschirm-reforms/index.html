    Regenschirm Reforms and OS Weapon Enchants¶

    In the Database

    Every item this enchanter works on, the enchants it adds and the NPC on the map: open its Database entry. Item pages link back here too.

    Three researchers in the Regenschirm Institute reform Thanos, OS and other weapons and armor, and enchant the Modified OS weapons.

    NPC Job Where
    Enchant Researcher (Torus) Enchants Modified OS weapons /navi rgsr_in 112/167
    Weapon Researcher (Frasa) Reforms Thanos weapons and OS weapons /navi rgsr_in 115/169
    Special Equipment Researcher (Stack) Reforms other weapons and armor /navi rgsr_in 136/171

    To get in, talk to the Equipment Reform PR Agent in Lighthalzen (/navi lighthalzen 99/156) or in the item mall (/navi itemmall 14/50) and choose Let's go.

    How reforms work¶

    Pick the reform in the menu, then put the item in the reform window from your inventory (unequip it first). A reform always succeeds and turns the item into a new one. Unless the tables say otherwise, enchants, the enchant grade and random options are kept.

    • Lower mods: cheaper, refine -5 (a +9 item becomes +4).
    • Intermediate mods: more expensive, refine -1 (a +9 item becomes +8).
    • Upper mods: refine +1 for weapons (see the tables for armor).

    "No cards" means the item must have no cards in it, or the window refuses it.

    Enchant Researcher (Torus)¶

    Choose Modified OS Weapon Enchant and the enchant window opens (only if you carry less than 70% of your weight limit). Put the weapon in from your inventory (unequip it first). Every enchant here is a selectable (perfect) enchant that always succeeds, and you then upgrade it step by step. Refine, cards and the enchant grade are never touched.

    Items it works on¶

    The 16 Modified OS weapons made by Frasa's OS weapon reform: Beam Claymore-OSAD [2] #600028, Meuchler-OSAD [2] #610039, Blasti-OSAD [2] #620018, Virtual Bow-OSAD [2] #700054, MH-P89-OSAD [2] #700055, AC-B44-OSAD [2] #700056, HR-55-OSAD [2] #810013, Burning Knuckle-OSAD [2] #560034, Cannon Rapier-OSAD [2] #500051, Saphir Mace-OSAD [2] #590043, Ultio-OSAD [2] #590044, Rutilus Stick-OSAD [2] #550075, Electric Fox-OSAD [2] #550076, Circuit Board-OSAD [2] #540051, Kuroiro-OSAD [2] #510066, Boost Lance-OSAD [2] #530031
