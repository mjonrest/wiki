    Azzam the Lucky (Nebula Armor and Signets of Star)¶

    Two NPCs called Azzam the Lucky stand side by side and enchant the Constellation Tower gear:

    • Azzam the Lucky #1 enchants the Nebula armors.
    • Azzam the Lucky #2 enchants the Signet of Star accessories.

    Both always succeed when adding an enchant. The only step that can fail is the Slot 2 upgrade on Signets. Refine, the card in the first slot and the enchant grade are always kept.

    Nebula Armor (Azzam the Lucky #1)¶

    Where: /navi grademk 42/185

    Items it works on¶

    You must be wearing the armor, and it must be refined to +9 or higher.

    Armor Own materials
    Nebula Armor of Power [1] #450169 Power Meteorite Powder #1000398, Power Meteorite Fragment #1000442
    Nebula Armor of Stamina [1] #450170 Stamina Meteorite Powder #1000399, Stamina Meteorite Fragment #1000443
    Nebula Suit of Concentration [1] #450171 Agile Meteorite Powder #1000400, Agile Meteorite Fragment #1000444
    Nebula Suit of Creative [1] #450172 Lucky Meteorite Powder #1000401, Lucky Meteorite Fragment #1000445
    Nebula Robe of Spell [1] #450173 Meteorite Powder of Spell #1000402, Meteorite Fragment of Spell #1000446
    Nebula Robe of Wisdom [1] #450174 Meteorite Powder of Wisdom #1000403, Meteorite Fragment of Wisdom #1000447

    "Own materials" means the powder and fragment that match your armor. The NPC asks for those by name.

    Enchants go in this order: Slot 4 → Slot 3 → Slot 2. The menu only offers the next empty slot.

    After each change the armor goes back to your inventory unequipped, so put it on again before you talk to Azzam again.

    Upgrading an enchant¶

    Every enchant above (except the top level) can be raised one level at a time. Upgrades always succeed.

    Slot Lv1 → Lv2 Lv2 → Lv3
    Slot 4 (stats, for example STR+3 #4702 → STR+4 #4703 → STR+5 #4704) 25 Meteorite Powder #1000372 + 250,000z 75 Meteorite Powder #1000372 + 750,000z
    Slot 3 25 of your armor's powder + 500,000z 75 of your armor's powder + 1,500,000z
    Slot 2 25 of your armor's fragment + 5,000,000z 75 of your armor's fragment + 15,000,000z

    Reroll and reset¶

    Option Cost What it does
    Reroll Slot 4 500 Meteorite Powder #1000372 Rolls a new Slot 4 stat from the Slot 4 table. Slots 2 and 3 are kept.
    Reset All Enchants 10 Meteorite Powder #1000372 + 1,000,000z Removes all three enchants. Refine, the first card and the grade are kept.

    Both always succeed. A reroll can land on the same enchant you already had.

    Reset¶

    30 Meteorite Powder #1000372 + 500,000z, always succeeds. Removes the enchants in slots 2, 3 and 4. Refine, the first card and the grade are kept. You pay even if the signet has no enchants.

    Meteorite Powder¶

    The machine OSC0007 at /navi e_tower 78/112 breaks down Meteorite Fragment #1000373: each fragment becomes 40 Meteorite Powder #1000372. Type how many fragments to break down.

    Tip

    The Reroll Slot 4 option needs 500 Meteorite Powder #1000372, which is 13 fragments' worth. Resetting and enchanting Slot 4 again costs only 15 powder and 1,050,000z, but it also wipes Slots 2 and 3.
