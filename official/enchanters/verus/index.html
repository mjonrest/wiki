    Verus Enchanters (Charleston and Excelion)¶

    Two enchant systems in Verus:

    • Mass Charleston robots trade, sell and enchant the Charleston Factory gear (Upgrade Parts and Supplement Parts) and upgrade the Pile Bunker.
    • PLUTO_09 and MARS_01 make and enchant the Excelion Wing and Excelion Suit.

    Charleston gear¶

    Getting the gear¶

    Mass Charleston (exchange) — /navi verus04 69/108

    Trade 10 Charleston Parts #6752 for one of:

    Reinforced parts Supplementary devices Slot
    Upgrade Part - Engine [1] #20733 Supplement Part Con [1] #20732 Garment
    Upgrade Part - Booster [1] #22044 Supplement Part Agi [1] #22043 Shoes
    Upgrade Part - Gun Barrel [1] #2996 Supplement Part Dex [1] #2995 Accessory

    Mass Charleston (shop) — /navi verus04 63/112

    • Sells Upgrade Part - Plate #15111 and Supplement Part Str #15110 (armor) for 3,999,999 zeny each.
    • Pile Bunker upgrade: trade 1 Pile Bunker #1549 + 300 Crushed Can Iron Plate #6751 + 15 Failed Engine #6750 for your choice of Pile Bunker S [1] #16030, Pile Bunker P #16031 or Pile Bunker T [1] #16032.

    Mass Charleston enchanter¶

    Where: /navi verus04 71/106

    Wear the item and choose its slot (Footgear, Garment, Clothes or Accessory). Accessories must be in the right accessory slot.

    Cost per try: 100,000 zeny + 1 Charleston Parts #6752 (enchant or reset). Refine and cards are always kept. You need 10,000 free weight.

    Item Max enchants Enchant table below +9 Choice at +9 or higher
    Upgrade Part - Plate #15111 3 Acceleration Acceleration or Attack
    Supplement Part Str #15110 3 Acceleration Acceleration or Defense
    Upgrade Part - Engine [1] #20733, Supplement Part Con [1] #20732 3 Garment Garment or Advanced Garment
    Upgrade Part - Booster [1] #22044, Supplement Part Agi [1] #22043 3 Shoes Shoes or Advanced Shoes
    Upgrade Part - Gun Barrel [1] #2996, Supplement Part Dex [1] #2995 2 Accessory Accessory only

    At +9 or higher the NPC asks which table to use each time you enchant.

    Each enchant try has its own risk:

    • 1st enchant: always succeeds.
    • 2nd enchant: can fail. On failure nothing is added and the materials are used up.
    • 3rd enchant: can fail the same way, and has a small chance to destroy the item.

    Tip

    The NPC warns that a failed 2nd enchant resets the refine. It does not: on failure the item comes back exactly as it was (same refine, cards and earlier enchants). Only the materials are lost.

    Reset¶

    Choose "Reset product." Costs 100,000 zeny + 1 Charleston Parts #6752. Always succeeds and removes every enchant; refine and cards are kept.

    The NPC does not check whether the item has enchants: resetting an item with none still costs the zeny and the Charleston Parts.

    Excelion gear¶

    Unlocking¶

    MARS_01 — /navi verus04 165/217

    Talk to MARS_01, choose to supply fuel, then bring 1 Old Fuel #6962 and put it in. This wakes up both robots.

    PLUTO_09: gear and designs¶

    Where: /navi verus04 163/219

    • Make gear: 10 Huge Metal Scrap #6961 + 5 Old Fuel #6962 for an Excelion Wing #20773 or an Excelion Suit #15128.
    • Make a design: 5 Old Fuel #6962 per try, 30% success. The design you get is random (table below). On failure you get a leftover item instead:
    Leftover on failure Chance per try
    Brigan #7054 26.92%
    Iron Ore #1002 26.92%
    Iron #998 10.77%
    Steel #999 5.38%
    Gold #969 0.005%

    MARS_01: enchanting Excelion gear¶

    Wear the Excelion Suit #15128 or Excelion Wing #20773 and choose "Enhanced ..." for it. Pick a design you own.

    • Cost: 1 design. No zeny.
    • Always succeeds. Refine and cards are kept.
    • Up to 3 enchants per item (4th slot, then 3rd, then 2nd).
    • Each design has a limit on how many times its enchant may appear on the same item. Some designs only work on one of the two items.
    • The four Property Reactor designs only work on the Suit, and only as the first enchant.
    • There is no reset for Excelion gear.
