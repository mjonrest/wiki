    Boots of Good and Evil¶

    In the Database

    Every item this enchanter works on, the enchants it adds and the NPC on the map: open its Database entry. Item pages link back here too.

    The Boots Enchanter in the Thanatos Tower lobby opens the enchant window for the job-specific Boots of Good and Evil.

    Where: /navi tha_t01 159/83

    How it works¶

    1. Talk to the NPC and pick your boots' job from the list.
    2. The enchant window opens (only if you carry less than 70% of your weight limit). Put the boots in from your inventory (unequip them first).
    3. Pick an enchant. All enchants and upgrades here are selectable and always succeed.

    Refine, cards and the enchant grade are never touched. Enchants go into slot 4 first, then slot 3.

    Slot 3¶

    Selectable (perfect) enchants — always succeed. You can pick the Good Vigor or Evil Vigor for your boots' job (see the table above).

    Cost: 50 × Curse Eroded Crystal #25739, 1 × Enchant Ticket #1000874, 5,000,000z.

    Reset¶

    Removes all enchants (cards are kept). Cost: 30 × Curse Eroded Crystal #25739, 30 × Curse Eroded Gemstone #25740, 1,500,000z. Success chance 90%; on failure the cost is used up and the enchants stay.
