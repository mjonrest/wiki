    RS26 (Illusion Armor Modules)¶

    In the Database

    Every item this enchanter works on, the enchants it adds and the NPC on the map: open its Database entry. Item pages link back here too.

    RS26 is a hologram robot in the Episode 17.1 Rebellion base that installs Modification Modules into the Illusion armor set that Elyumina makes. You pick the module yourself, so every enchant is guaranteed.

    Where: /navi sp_cor 106/136

    Items it works on¶

    Menu Equipment slot Items
    Enchant Illusion Armor Armor Illusion Armor A-type [1] #15376, Illusion Armor B-type [1] #15377
    Enchant Illusion Engine Wing Garment Illusion Engine wing A-type [1] #20933, Illusion Engine wing B-type [1] #20934
    Enchant Illusion Leg Shoes Illusion Leg A-type [1] #22196, Illusion Leg B-type [1] #22197
    Enchant Illusion Accessory [R] Right accessory Illusion Booster R [1] #32207, Illusion Battle chip R [1] #32209
    Enchant Illusion Accessory [L] Left accessory Illusion Booster L [1] #32208, Illusion Battle chip L [1] #32210

    The item must be equipped in that slot. You need 1,000 free weight and room in your inventory. There is no refine requirement.

    Enchanting¶

    1. Pick the equipment menu, then the module grade (Normal, Rare, Unique or Legendary).
    2. Pick the module from the list. Modules you do not have are greyed out.

    3. RS26 shows how many of that module are already on the item and asks you to confirm.

    4. Cost: 1 module of the chosen type. No zeny.

    5. Success: always succeeds. Refine and cards are kept and the item is never destroyed.
    6. Slots: each item takes up to 3 modules. They fill the 2nd, 3rd and 4th slots in that order. Once the 4th slot is used the item is at its limit.
    7. Each module type has its own limit per item (last column below).
    8. A module that is not in the item's table is refused ("You need to have a module that matches the efficacy").

    Getting the armor: Elyumina¶

    /navi sp_cor 111/130

    Elyumina is hidden until you talk to the Rebellion soldier (after Pure Facts is done), who calls her over. Pick "Request Illusion Armor.", choose a part and a type. Each piece costs 50 Cor Core #25723 and you choose exactly which one you get: Illusion Armor A-type [1] #15376, Illusion Armor B-type [1] #15377, Illusion Engine wing A-type [1] #20933, Illusion Engine wing B-type [1] #20934, Illusion Leg A-type [1] #22196, Illusion Leg B-type [1] #22197, Illusion Booster R [1] #32207, Illusion Booster L [1] #32208, Illusion Battle chip R [1] #32209, Illusion Battle chip L [1] #32210.

    She also trades 1 Damaged Weapon #25668 and 50 Mysterious Component #25669 for one random OS weapon (each weapon has the same chance).
