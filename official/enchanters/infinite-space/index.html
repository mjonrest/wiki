    Infinite Space Artifact Enhancer¶

    The Artifact Enhancer enchants the Infinity and Rift gear from Infinite Space with Broken Magic Stone #6905. The Artifact Appraiser next to her sells that gear.

    Where: Artifact Enhancer /navi cmd_fild07 60/275, Artifact Appraiser /navi cmd_fild07 57/275

    Getting the gear¶

    The Artifact Appraiser sells each item below for 50 Broken Magic Stone #6905.

    Type Items
    Weapons Infinity Whip [1] #1994, Infinity Violin [1] #1938, Infinity Shuriken [1] #13323, Infinity Pistol [1] #13126, Infinity Dagger [1] #28703, Infinity Two-Handed Staff [1] #2024, Infinity Mace [1] #16038, Infinity Two-Handed Sword [1] #21014, Infinity Axe [1] #28105, Infinity Bow [1] #18128
    Armor Rift Ancient Armor [1] #15141
    Shoes Rift Shoes [1] #22075
    Garment Rift Manteau [1] #20779
    Helm (upper headgear) Ancient Rift Ornament [1] #19033

    Enchanting¶

    Wear the item and pick its slot (Weapon, Armor, Shoes, Garment, Helm). The weapon must be in your right hand. You need 1,000 free weight.

    Cost: 20 Broken Magic Stone #6905 per enchant. It always succeeds. Refine and cards are kept.

    Two enchants per item: the 1st goes into slot 4, the 2nd into slot 3. Each time you pick a type (Physical, Magical or Range) and get a random enchant from that list. Every entry in a list has the same chance.

    Known issue: Quit does not cancel

    Choosing Quit on the Physical / Magical / Range menu does not end the conversation. To back out, choose I'll return later. on the next screen.

    Initialize (reset)¶

    Cost: 30 Broken Magic Stone #6905. Removes both enchants. Refine and cards are kept. The item must have at least one enchant.

    Known issue: the break warning is wrong

    The NPC warns that the item may be destroyed during the reset. In the current script the reset always succeeds and never destroys the item.
