    Varmundt Rune Gear (Biosphere)¶

    In the Database

    Every item this enchanter works on, the enchants it adds and the NPC on the map: open its Database entry. Item pages link back here too.

    Three Beta robots in Varmundt's mansion enchant and upgrade the Varmundt rune gear from the Biosphere. Whoopi in the grade workshop offers the same upgrades.

    NPC Job Where
    Beta (Equipment enchantment) Enchants the Varmundt rune gear /navi ba_in01 363/53
    Beta (Varmundt Equipment Modification) Upgrades rune gear into the stronger version /navi ba_in01 368/55
    Beta (Enchant Modified Equipment) Enchants the upgraded gear /navi ba_in01 371/55
    Whoopi Same upgrades as the modification Beta /navi grademk 51/169

    All three Betas open the game's enchant or upgrade window. Pick the element in the NPC menu, then put the item in from your inventory (unequip it first). The enchant window only opens if you carry less than 70% of your weight limit.

    Random enchants always succeed unless a chance is shown. Selectable enchants and enchant upgrades always succeed. Refine, cards and the enchant grade are kept.
