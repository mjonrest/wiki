    Infinite Space¶

    Infinite Space with hard mode (Episode 16.1)

    Overview¶

    • Entrance: Reckless Explorer at cmd_fild07 63/275 (/navi cmd_fild07 63/275) or Temporary Entrance at cmd_fild07 54/280 (/navi cmd_fild07 54/280)
    • Requirements: Level 100+, time limit 1h, cooldown 3 hours
    • Who can enter: Party (create a party first)
