    Octopus Cave¶

    Guide¶

    Octopus Cave is a light-hearted Malangdo memorial. A perverted octopus has been grabbing at the Starfish lady, so you cut off its four legs in the four wings of its cave and then skewer the Giant Octopus #2194 itself.

    Requirements and entry¶
    Party Required. Only the party leader can open the cave.
    Required item A rented Octopus Hunting Skewer #6442, needed by every member who enters
    NPC Starfish at /navi mal_dun01 151/235
    Entrance Weird Entrance at /navi mal_dun01 153/237
    Time limit 1 hour
    Cooldown 1 hour, starting when you enter

    The leader talks to the Starfish and picks Ask to open the gate. Then everyone uses the Weird Entrance and picks Go in.

    Bring the skewer

    Both the Starfish and the Weird Entrance check for a rental Octopus Hunting Skewer #6442. Anyone without one is turned away at the entrance. The skewer comes from the Octopus Hstick Box #16765 (23 hours), Octopus Hstick Box2 #16766 (3 days) or Octopus Hstick Box3 #16767 (7 days).

    Walkthrough¶

    The four legs

    1. When you step forward from the entrance (199/99), the octopus taunts you. Four Octopus Leg #2193 spawn, one at the far end of each wing of the cave:

      Leg Location
      Octopus Leg #1 West wing, 20/114
      Octopus Leg #2 North-west wing, 88/190
      Octopus Leg #3 North-east wing, 307/215
      Octopus Leg #4 East wing, 372/131

    2. The paths to each leg are lined with Octopus #2192 henchmen, Hydra #1068 and Stapo #1784. A few spots along the way set off extra ambushes when you walk over them, including one with Deep Sea Marse #2175 squids.

    3. When you get close to a leg, it calls for help, and two more groups of henchmen spawn 5 seconds later. A warp back to the central hall (198/99) opens once all of that leg's henchmen are dead, or 30 seconds after you reach the leg, whichever comes first.
    4. The doors in the central hall connect all four wings, so you can split the party up and take the legs in parallel.

    The Giant Octopus

    1. When the fourth leg dies, the whole party is pulled back to the central hall and a warp to the boss room opens at 198/116.
    2. The Giant Octopus #2194 (named Disgusting Octopus) waits at 199/188. Every 10 seconds it summons either one Octopus Leg #2193 or five Octopus #2192 around itself, up to 100 adds at a time.
    3. Kill the octopus. Its leftover adds disappear and two exits open, at 190/208 and 198/82.

    Tip

    The boss keeps adding monsters every 10 seconds for as long as it is alive. Focus it down instead of clearing every add.

    Rewards¶

    Reward Who Notes
    20–40 Instance Points Each player who uses an exit and picks Yes! Random amount. Counts toward the daily 1,200 point cap. You are then warped to /navi mal_dun01 153/233.

    The boss and the octopus monsters drop their normal loot; see the tables below.

    Instance Points

    Instance Points can be earned once per device for each instance run, up to 1,200 per account per day. A rented Charm Of Brightness #30034 adds 50 points per claim. Use @instancepoints to see your total.

    Overview¶

    • Entrance: Starfish at mal_dun01 151/235 (/navi mal_dun01 151/235) or Weird Entrance at mal_dun01 153/237 (/navi mal_dun01 153/237)
    • Requirements: Unlocked by its story quest, time limit 1h, cooldown 1 hour
    • Who can enter: Party (create a party first)
