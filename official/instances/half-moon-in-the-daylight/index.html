    Half Moon In The Daylight¶

    Guide¶

    A story instance from the Edda series. You walk into the Pope's dream in Rachel and follow her and her brother Luwmin through three dream areas, fighting temple guards and devotees along the way. The Pope fights at your side as a mercenary. It is effectively solo: only a party leader can step through the dream portal.

    How to enter¶
    NPC High Priestess Niren in Rachel: /navi rachel 174/138
    Portal Dream (next to Niren): /navi rachel 176/145
    Level 80+
    Party You must be a party leader (a party of one is enough). Only party leaders can use the Dream portal
    Cooldown Once per day ("Time to Rest"): after entering you must wait until the next 04:00 server time
    Time limit 2 hours

    The first time, talk through Niren's introduction. Then talk to her again to create the dream and use the Dream portal to go in.

    Difficulty¶

    The difficulty is set by the level of the player who starts the dream (first talks to the Pope inside):

    Mode Leader level Monsters Final fight Hunting quest given on entry
    Casual below 130 Lv 80 ~ 90 versions Enraged Luwmin #3516 Defeat Enraged Luwmin #3516
    Hard 130+ Lv 130 ~ 141 versions Enraged Luwmin #3524, then Vision of Ktullanux #3526 Defeat Vision of Ktullanux #3526

    The Pope joins you as a mercenary for 30 minutes at the start of each big fight. In hard mode she is the "Casual Pope" mercenary (Casual Pope #3569), in casual mode the "Official Pope" (Official Pope #3570). She leaves when the fight ends.

    Walkthrough¶

    Dream 1: the temple

    1. Talk to the Pope at the start and follow her through the portal that opens.
    2. Walk up to the Pope further along, then talk to her again in the hall (around 100/98). After a long scene she joins you and the fight starts in three waves: 11 Temple Guard #3510, then 15 Enraged Devotees, then a Enraged High Priest #3514. Each wave appears when the previous one is dead.
    3. Follow the Pope south, watch the scene with Luwmin, and take the portal at the bottom of the map.

    Dream 2: the escape

    1. Follow the Pope and Luwmin. Stepping on certain spots along the route (the game points you to them) brings out the next group of guards: Temple Guard #3510, Enraged Devotees and Enraged Dog #3513. Kill each group to move on.
    2. The area ends with another Enraged High Priest #3514 near 57/80.
    3. Follow the strange copy of the Pope to the portal at 130/22.

    Dream 3: the cold

    1. Talk to the Pope at 45/66. Enraged Luwmin #3516 appears with six Frozen Hearts (Frozen Heart #3515). Only Luwmin has to die; the Frozen Hearts are removed with him.
    2. Hard mode only: after Luwmin, Vision of Ktullanux #3526 appears with eleven Frozen Hearts. Again only the boss needs to die.
    3. Talk to the Pope (57/68) to finish. An exit appears to the east (79/67) that returns you to Rachel.

    The Old Doll and rewards¶

    • Each of the three dreams can drop an Old Doll #25087 on the floor as a glowing light: after the High Priest in dreams 1 and 2 (1 in 3 chance each), and after Luwmin in dream 3 (always, if you have none yet). Step on the light to pick it up.
    • At the end, talking to the Pope with the doll in your inventory exchanges it for 350,000 base EXP, 350,000 job EXP and 2 Fragment of Dreams #25088.
    • Back in Rachel, talk to Niren with the hunting quest done for another 126,000 base EXP and 90,000 job EXP.

    Match the leader's mode

    The hunting quest is given according to your own level (Luwmin below 130, Ktullanux at 130+), but the monsters follow the leader's level. A level 130+ player in a casual run never meets Ktullanux, and a lower-level player in a hard run fights the hard Luwmin, so neither can complete their quest that run.

    Tip

    Only the first player who talks to the Pope at the end gets the doll reward, so make sure that player is the one carrying the Old Doll #25087.

    Fragments of Dreams¶

    The Temple Item Manager next to Niren (/navi rachel 177/139) uses Fragment of Dreams #25088 to add a Goddess enchant (Justice, Mercy or Insight) to a worn Bishop Necklace #28387: 100 fragments for an A-class enchant, 1,000 for S-class.

    Episode 16.1 - Edda Half Moon In The Daylight

    Overview¶

    • Entrance: High Priestess Niren at rachel 174/138 (/navi rachel 174/138) or Dream at rachel 176/145 (/navi rachel 176/145)
    • Requirements: Level 80+, time limit 2h, cooldown 4 hours
    • Who can enter: Party (create a party first)
