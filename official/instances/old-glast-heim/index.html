    Old Glast Heim¶

    Discover the history of events that took place in the Glast Heim castle and how it ended up in ruins.

    Overview¶

    • Entrance: Hugin at glast_01 204/273 (/navi glast_01 204/273)
    • Requirements (Old Glast Heim): Level 130+, time limit 1h, no cooldown
    • Requirements (Old Glast Heim Advanced): Level 130+, time limit 1h, no cooldown
    • Who can enter: Party (create a party first)
    • Modes: Old Glast Heim, Old Glast Heim Advanced
