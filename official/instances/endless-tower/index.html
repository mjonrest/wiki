    Endless Tower¶

    A 100-floor climb for a party, ending with Nacht Sieger. Every floor has to be cleared before its gate opens. MVPs guard every fifth floor, and the last floors throw several of them at you at once. On Miracle the tower has five difficulty modes: harder modes give monsters more HP and damage, and pay more experience and Instance Points. Every player must meet the chosen mode's level to enter, and nobody can enter once Nacht Sieger is defeated.

    Endless Tower Memorial Dungeon. Consists of 101 floors.

    Overview¶

    • Entrance: Tower Protection Stone at e_tower 81/105 (/navi e_tower 81/105)
    • Requirements: Level 50+, time limit 4h, cooldown 1 day
    • Who can enter: Party (create a party first)

    Difficulty modes¶

    The party leader picks a mode when creating the tower. Every party member must meet its level. Monsters get the bonuses below on top of their normal stats.

    Mode Level Monster HP Monster damage Damage they take DEF / MDEF HIT / FLEE Bonus exp Instance Points

    Veteran 100+ +50% +7000% -70% +200% / +200% +200% / +200% +200× monster exp 100
    Nightmare 150+ +100% +8000% -80% +200% / +200% +200% / +200% +500× monster exp 150
    Hell 200+ +150% +9000% -90% +150% / +150% +150% / +150% +1000× monster exp 200
    Torment 240+ +200% +10000% -99% +200% / +200% +200% / +200% +1500× monster exp 500
