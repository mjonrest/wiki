    Nightmarish Jitterbug¶

    Guide¶

    You join the White Wings guild on a trip into a time loop to find their former master Newoz. The instance is one long map of about twenty small rooms. Each room is a fight followed by a story scene, and the run ends with Awakened Ferre #3073. You trade the Jitterbug's Tooth #6719 you collect here to Mingmin for random gear.

    Entering¶

    • Level: 120 or higher.
    • Quest: talk to Roel at /navi moc_para01 26/95 and accept his request. You have to do this before every run: Runane only opens the portal while Roel's quest is active.
    • Party: you must be in a party. The party leader talks to Runane at /navi moc_para01 29/95 and picks Open the dimensional portal. Then everyone picks Enter Nightmarish Jitterbug from Runane.
    • Cooldown: entering gives you the Nightmarish Jitterbug: Waiting quest. The cooldown ends at the next 04:00 server time. Runane's dialogue says "20 hours" and "23 hours", but the real reset is 04:00. When the cooldown is over, talk to Runane once to clear it.

    How a room works¶

    1. The party leader talks to the NPC that starts the scene in the current room (usually Newoz). Partway through the scene, the door to the next room opens and that room's monsters spawn: mostly Ferre #3069 and its stronger variants (Ferre #3070, Ferre #3071, Ferre #3072).
    2. Go through and kill every monster in the new room.
    3. About 1.5 seconds after the last one dies, the White Wings members appear there for the next scene.

    Only the party leader can move the story forward. Other members can talk to the NPCs but nothing happens.

    Hurry

    Once the leader has finished the story once, the leader gets a Hurry option on each story NPC. It skips the dialogue and opens the next room straight away.

    Extra Jitterbugs from your answers¶

    In some scenes the party leader picks an answer. Some answers spawn an extra Jitterbug1 #3108 in the next room, so there are more monsters to kill and more Jitterbug drops. When the leader uses Hurry, the answer they gave last time is used again.

    Boss: Awakened Ferre¶

    After room 22 is cleared, the leader talks to Newoz in the boss room. Ferre awakens as Awakened Ferre #3073 and brings 12 Ferre with her (4 of each of the first three kinds). Once the fight starts, the door from room 22 closes, so anyone left behind cannot come in.

    • Every 30 seconds a Storm Gust effect lands on the boss. Every party member within 10 cells of the middle of the room is frozen for 15 seconds, every time. Fight away from the centre or have a way to cure Freeze.
    • Only Awakened Ferre has to die. When she is dead, the leader talks to Newoz again to open the portal to the exit room.

    Warning

    The portal from the boss room to the exit does not take dead players with it. Get resurrected before you use it.

    Exit and rewards¶

    • Mingmin in the exit room (1@jtb 375/28) runs the exchanges below.
    • The exit portal completes Roel's quest, gives 20–40 Instance Points (+50 with Charm Of Brightness #30034, within the daily Instance Point limit) and sends you back to moc_para01.
    • Talk to Roel again after you leave to get 1,500,000 Base EXP and 500,000 Job EXP. You get this after every run, not only the first one.

    Jitterbug's Tooth: hand over 50× Jitterbug's Tooth #6719 for one random item. Each item has the same chance (1 in 12):

    Oz's New Wing Ring [1] #2988 Floral Bracelet Of Aigu [1] #2989

    Frozen Breastplate [1] #15100 Hardened Breastplate [1] #15101

    Strange Pendant: bring one Pendant of Harmony #2990 and one Pendant of Chaos #2991 (not equipped). Mingmin combines them into Pendant of Maelstrom [1] #2992. It always works.

    Mingmin will not trade if you are carrying too much: you need at least 1,000 free weight.
