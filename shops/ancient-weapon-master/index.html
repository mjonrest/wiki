    Ancient Weapon Master¶

    Location: moc_para01 12, 24 · Quest Room

    Recycle old weapons into Ancient Tokens (1 item = 1 token) and spend the tokens on ancient weapon upgrades.

    Recycling scans your whole inventory

    Recycling takes every listed item in your inventory, including equipped ones. Put anything you want to keep in storage first.
