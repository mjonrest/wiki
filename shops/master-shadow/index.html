    Master Shadow¶

    Location: moc_para01 14, 14 · Quest Room

    Recycle old Shadow gear into Shadow Essence #1001253, then exchange essences and +10 Shadows for Master Shadows.

    Shadow refine You get
    +0 ~ +9 3~5 Shadow Essence
    +10 12~15 Shadow Essence

    Recycle All recycles every +0 to +9 Shadow in your inventory at once and skips +10 ones.
