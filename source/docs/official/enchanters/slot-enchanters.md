# Slot Enchanters (Seiyablem and Leablem)

<!-- npcs: Seiyablem; Leablem -->

The brothers Seiyablem and Leablem add a card slot to selected weapons and armor. Each NPC has its own item
list. You trade the unslotted item plus materials and zeny for the slotted version.

## How it works (both NPCs)

- Carry the **unslotted item in your inventory** (it does not need to be equipped).
- Carry **only one** copy of it. If you have several, a random one is taken.
- You pay the zeny and materials **whether you succeed or fail**.
- **On success** you get a brand-new slotted item: **+0, no cards**. Refine and cards on the old item are lost.
- **On failure** the item is **destroyed**, along with any refine and cards on it.
- The chance is fixed per item. Refine level and which town you use do not change it.

!!! tip
    Both NPCs hint that the success chance depends on the item's refine and on the town. It does not. Always
    slot a +0 item with no card, since refine and cards are lost either way.
