# Gray Wolf Equipment (Emmet)

<!-- npcs: Emmet -->

**Emmet** in the Gray Wolf village trades {{ item(1000405) }} for the Gray Wolf equipment and enchants it.

**Where:** `/navi wolfvill 164/137`

Emmet only offers her services once you are far enough into the Episode 18 main quest. Before that she just
asks you to collect Amethyst Fragments.

| Menu | What it does |
|---|---|
| Exchange Armor | Trade window: Gray Wolf equipment for {{ item(1000405) }} |
| Gray Wolf Enchantment | Pick Armor, Garment, Shoes, Right(R) Accessory or Left(L) Accessory, then the enchant window opens |

## How it works

1. Choose **Gray Wolf Enchantment** and the part you want to enchant.
2. The enchant window opens (only if you carry less than 70% of your weight limit). Put the item in from
   your **inventory** (unequip it first). Only the items listed for that part are accepted.
3. Pay and the next empty slot is enchanted. Every enchant here always succeeds. Refine, cards and the
   enchant grade are never touched.

Every part has three enchant slots (slot 4, then slot 3, then slot 2). Slot 2 can also be bought as a
selectable (perfect) enchant.
