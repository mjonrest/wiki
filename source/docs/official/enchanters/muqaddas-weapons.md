# Muqaddas Weapons (Weapon Master)

<!-- npcs: Weapon Master -->

The **Weapon Master** sells the Muqaddas weapons and enchants them.

**Where:** `/navi ra_pol01 300/109`

**Weapon Shop** opens a trade window for the weapons. **Weapon Enchant** opens the enchant window.

## How it works

1. Talk to the NPC and choose **Weapon Enchant**.
2. The enchant window opens (only if you carry less than 70% of your weight limit). Put the weapon in from
   your **inventory** (unequip it first).
3. Pick an enchant. Refine, cards and the enchant grade are never touched.
