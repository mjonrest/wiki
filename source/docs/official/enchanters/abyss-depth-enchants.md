# Abyss Depth Enchant Manager (Rune Crowns and Dimension Weapons)

<!-- npcs: Enchant Manager; Rune Crown Manager; Specimen Refine Manager -->

The research team in the Biosphere Abyss Depth lobby crafts **Time Dimensions Rune Crowns**, refines the rune
materials, and enchants Rune Crowns and **Dimensions** weapons through the enchant window.

**Where:** `/navi ba_chess 28/16` (Enchant Manager). The Rune Crown Manager is at `/navi ba_chess 25/19` and the
Specimen Refine Manager at `/navi ba_chess 22/16`.

## Requirements

- You must have started the *Biosphere Abyss Depth* quest.
- **Enchant Manager** and **Rune Crown Manager:** at least **500** Depth 2nd floor research reputation.
- **Specimen Refine Manager:** the *Abyss Researcher* quest must be completed.

## How the enchant window works

Talk to the Enchant Manager and pick **"Rune Crown Enchant"** or **"Dimension Weapon Enchant"**. A window opens.

- The item must be in your **inventory, not equipped** (and not in the equip-switch window).
- Slots are filled in a fixed order. **Random enchant** rolls from the list for the next empty slot.
  **Perfect enchant** lets you pick a specific enchant for that slot. **Upgrade** raises an enchant you already
  have.
- Every enchant, perfect enchant, upgrade and reset here has a **100% success rate**. Refine and cards are never
  touched.
