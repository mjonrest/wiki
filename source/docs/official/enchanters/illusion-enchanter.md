# Illusion Enchanter

<!-- npcs: Illusion Enchanter; Gemcutter; Great Merchant; Illusion Stone Research; Equipment Researcher; Village Soap; Horen; From; Bear Wanting Illusion Stone; Esmeralda -->

The Illusion Enchanter in Prontera adds up to two random enchants to **Illusion equipment** for
{{ item(25271) }}. Illusion equipment itself is made by the exchange NPCs inside (or next to) each Illusion
dungeon, listed at the bottom of this page.

**Where:** `/navi prontera 90/115` (near the Town Office)

## Before you start

- The first time you talk to him he explains his service and asks if you want to join his member list. Answer
  **"Sure!"**. After that you can use the enchant and reset menus.
- You need 2,000 free weight and room in your inventory.
- The item must be **equipped**. You pick the equipment slot from a menu (right-hand weapon, armor, shoes,
  garment, right or left accessory, upper headgear, left-hand weapon/shield).
- Items with **random options** are refused.
- There is no refine requirement.

## Enchanting

Pick **"Add stats to Illusion equipment."** and the slot.

- **Cost:** 5 {{ item(25271) }} per enchant. No zeny.
- **Success:** always succeeds. Refine and cards are kept and the item is never destroyed.
- **Order:** the first enchant goes into the **4th slot**, the second into the **3rd slot**. After two enchants
  the item is "enchanted to the maximum limit".
- The possible results are the same for both enchants. For {{ item(28023) }} you first choose
  **Melee**, **Ranged** or **Magic**, which picks the list below.

## Reset

Pick **"Reset enchanted Illusion equipment."** and the slot. Only items with **both** enchants (3rd and 4th slot)
can be reset. You choose how much zeny to pay, which sets the success chance:

| Zeny | Success chance |
|---|---|
| 100,000 | 50% |
| 200,000 | 60% |
| 300,000 | 70% |
| 400,000 | 80% |
| 500,000 | 90% |

- **On success** both enchants are removed. Refine and cards stay.
- **On failure** the zeny is gone **and the item is destroyed**, together with its cards and refine.

A failed reset destroys the item along with its cards and refine. The chance of failure is 10% to 50%, depending on the zeny you pay.

## Getting Illusion equipment

These NPCs turn a normal item (refined to **+9 or higher** where shown) plus {{ item(25271) }} and dungeon
materials into its Illusion version. They are barter shops: the new item comes **without refine, cards or
enchants**, so take your cards out first. Where no refine is shown, any refine is accepted.
