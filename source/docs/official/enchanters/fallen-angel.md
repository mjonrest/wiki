# Fallen Angel (Archangel Wings enchant)

<!-- npcs: Fallen Angel -->

The Fallen Angel adds one random enchant to the 4th slot of {{ item(2573) }}.

**Where:** `/navi mal_in01 15/16`

## Enchanting

- **Wear** the {{ item(2573) }} in your garment slot.
- **Cost:** 1,000,000 zeny per attempt.
- The 4th slot must be empty. If the wing is already enchanted, reset it first.
- On success refine and the card are kept.
- On failure (5.22%) the wing is **destroyed**, together with its card and refine.

!!! tip
    Getting {{ item(4848) }}, {{ item(4849) }} or {{ item(4852) }} is announced to the whole server.

## Reset

Choose "Enchant Initialization" while wearing the wing. It costs 3 {{ item(6417) }} and always works, as long as the 4th slot holds an enchant: only the 4th-slot enchant is removed, refine and card stay.
