# Varmundt Rune Gear (Biosphere)

<!-- npcs: Beta; Whoopi -->

Three **Beta** robots in Varmundt's mansion enchant and upgrade the Varmundt rune gear from the Biosphere.
**Whoopi** in the grade workshop offers the same upgrades.

| NPC | Job | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Beta (Equipment enchantment) | Enchants the Varmundt rune gear | `/navi ba_in01 363/53` |
| Beta (Varmundt Equipment Modification) | Upgrades rune gear into the stronger version | `/navi ba_in01 368/55` |
| Beta (Enchant Modified Equipment) | Enchants the upgraded gear | `/navi ba_in01 371/55` |
| Whoopi | Same upgrades as the modification Beta | `/navi grademk 51/169` |

All three Betas open the game's enchant or upgrade window. Pick the element in the NPC menu, then put the
item in from your **inventory** (unequip it first). The enchant window only opens if you carry less than 70% of
your weight limit.

Random enchants always succeed unless a chance is shown. Selectable enchants and enchant upgrades always
succeed. Refine, cards and the enchant grade are kept.
