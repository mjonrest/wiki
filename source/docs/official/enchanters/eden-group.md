# Eden Group Equipment (Thorn and BK)

<!-- npcs: Blacksmith Thorn; Weapons Expert BK -->

Two craftsmen in the Eden Group headquarters enchant the Eden Group gear you get from the Eden Group's
intermediate missions. **Blacksmith Thorn** enchants the armor, garment and boots, and **Weapons Expert BK**
upgrades the Eden weapons.

**Where:** Blacksmith Thorn `/navi moc_para01 111/83`, Weapons Expert BK `/navi moc_para01 112/79`

Both work on the item in your **inventory**. Each upgrade takes the item and gives you a new one, so the
new item is always **+0** and any card in it is **lost**. Carry only one copy of the item you want to
upgrade: BK refuses if you carry two of the same weapon, and Thorn may take the other copy.

## Blacksmith Thorn

You need the Eden Group Mark to talk to Thorn. Choose **Give me new equipment.** once during the Eden Group
intermediate missions to get a set of Eden Group gear and an Eden weapon for your job.

### Errand

Each enchant must be paid for with an errand. Choose **Personal Request** and accept it, then bring:

| Your base level | Bring |
|---|---|
| Below 80 | 20 {{ item(1002) }} and 10 {{ item(998) }} |
| 80 or higher | 20 {{ item(7319) }} |

## Weapons Expert BK

BK upgrades one Eden weapon in three steps. Each step unlocks as you progress in the Eden Group
intermediate missions, and all of them are free.

Weapons: {{ item(1197) }}, {{ item(13434) }}, {{ item(13066) }}, {{ item(1289) }}, {{ item(1391) }},
{{ item(1434) }}, {{ item(1658) }}, {{ item(16014) }}, {{ item(18106) }}, {{ item(1583) }}, {{ item(1931) }},
{{ item(1986) }}, {{ item(13114) }}, {{ item(1831) }}, {{ item(13310) }}, {{ item(26101) }}

| Step | You choose | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The weapon, then physical or magical | Slot 4: {{ item(4767) }} (physical) or {{ item(4806) }} (magical) |
| 2 | A monster type | Slot 3: the card for that type (table below) |
| 3 | The weapon | Slot 2: a second copy of the same card |

| Type | Physical (ATK +3%) | Magical (MATK +3%) |
|---|---|---|
| Brute | {{ item(4060) }} | {{ item(4472) }} |
| Plant | {{ item(4068) }} | {{ item(4470) }} |
| Insect | {{ item(4063) }} | {{ item(4476) }} |
| Fish | {{ item(4080) }} | {{ item(4469) }} |
| Dragon | {{ item(4118) }} | {{ item(4471) }} |
| Increased Heal | {{ item(4805) }} | {{ item(4805) }} |

Your choices are saved on your character. Step 3 builds the finished weapon from those choices, whichever
Eden weapon you pick. There is no reset: once step 3 is done, BK has nothing more to offer.

!!! warning "Known issue: step 2 picks the weapon for you"
    In step 2 BK does not ask which weapon to use. He takes the **first Eden weapon he finds**, in the order
    of the weapon list above. If you carry more than one Eden weapon, step 2 can go on the wrong one. Carry
    only the weapon you upgraded in step 1.
