# Verus Enchanters (Charleston and Excelion)

<!-- npcs: Mass Charleston; PLUTO_09; MARS_01 -->

Two enchant systems in Verus:

- **Mass Charleston** robots trade, sell and enchant the Charleston Factory gear (Upgrade Parts and Supplement
  Parts) and upgrade the Pile Bunker.
- **PLUTO_09** and **MARS_01** make and enchant the Excelion Wing and Excelion Suit.

## Charleston gear

Trade 10 {{ item(6752) }} for one of:

  of {{ item(16030) }}, {{ item(16031) }} or {{ item(16032) }}.

### Mass Charleston enchanter

**Where:** `/navi verus04 71/106`

Wear the item and choose its slot (Footgear, Garment, Clothes or Accessory). Accessories must be in the
**right** accessory slot.

**Cost per try:** 100,000 zeny + 1 {{ item(6752) }} (enchant or reset). Refine and cards are always kept. You
need 10,000 free weight.

| Item | Max enchants | Enchant table below +9 | Choice at +9 or higher |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{ item(15111) }} | 3 | Acceleration | Acceleration or Attack |
| {{ item(15110) }} | 3 | Acceleration | Acceleration or Defense |
| {{ item(20733) }}, {{ item(20732) }} | 3 | Garment | Garment or Advanced Garment |
| {{ item(22044) }}, {{ item(22043) }} | 3 | Shoes | Shoes or Advanced Shoes |
| {{ item(2996) }}, {{ item(2995) }} | 2 | Accessory | Accessory only |

At +9 or higher the NPC asks which table to use **each time** you enchant.

Each enchant try has its own risk:

- **1st enchant:** always succeeds.
- **2nd enchant:** can fail. On failure nothing is added and the materials are used up.
- **3rd enchant:** can fail the same way, and has a small chance to **destroy the item**.

!!! tip
    The NPC warns that a failed 2nd enchant resets the refine. It does not: on failure the item comes back
    exactly as it was (same refine, cards and earlier enchants). Only the materials are lost.

#### Reset

Choose **"Reset product."** Costs 100,000 zeny + 1 {{ item(6752) }}. Always succeeds and removes every enchant;
refine and cards are kept.

!!! warning "Known issue"
    The NPC does not check whether the item has any enchants. Resetting an item with no enchants still takes the
    zeny and the Charleston Parts.

### MARS_01: enchanting Excelion gear

Wear the {{ item(15128) }} or {{ item(20773) }} and choose "Enhanced ..." for it. Pick a design you own.

- **Cost:** 1 design. No zeny.
- **Always succeeds.** Refine and cards are kept.
- Up to **3 enchants** per item (4th slot, then 3rd, then 2nd).
- Each design has a limit on how many times its enchant may appear on the same item. Some designs only work on
  one of the two items.
- The four **Property Reactor** designs only work on the Suit, and only as the **first** enchant.
- There is **no reset** for Excelion gear.
