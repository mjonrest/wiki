# Time Gap Weapons (Kkat)

<!-- npcs: Kkat -->

**Kkat** in the second depth of the Varmundt Biosphere crafts Time Gap weapons and enchants them.

**Where:** `/navi bl_depth2 210/264`

## Crafting

**Craft a Time Gap weapon** opens a trade window. Each Time Gap weapon takes a **+10** Glacier weapon, a
**+10** Dim Glacier weapon, a **+10** OSAD weapon, a **+12** Episode 17.2 weapon and 2,000 elemental gems or
crystals. The exact recipe for each weapon is shown in the window. The Dagger and Kurojin come as a box that
holds both the (A) and (B) versions.

## How it works

1. Talk to the NPC and choose **Enchantment**.
2. The enchant window opens (only if you carry less than 70% of your weight limit). Put the weapon in from
   your **inventory** (unequip it first).
3. Pick an enchant. Refine, cards and the enchant grade are never touched.

Every enchant here is a selectable (perfect) enchant: you choose it and it always succeeds.
