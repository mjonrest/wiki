# Mayomayo (Malangdo weapon enchants)

<!-- npcs: Mayomayo -->

Mayomayo adds random enchants to a long list of high-level weapons, paid with Malangdo coins. Enchanting **never fails** and never touches refine or cards.

**Where:** `/navi malangdo 213/167`

## How it works

- **Equip** the weapon in your right hand, then pick "Enchant High Ranked Weapon".
- Choose which coin to pay with. The coin decides the enchant table, and the weapon's price tier decides how many coins you need.
- The 1st enchant goes into the **4th slot**, the 2nd into the **3rd slot**. Weapons with 3 card slots only get one enchant.
- When the slots are full, reset the enchants and start again.
- Refine and cards are always kept.

## Reset

Pick "Initialize Enchant" while wearing the weapon. It costs 1 {{ item(6417) }}, always works, and removes all enchants. Refine and cards are kept. It only works if the weapon has an enchant in its 4th slot.
