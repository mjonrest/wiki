# Devil Enchant Master (Upgrade weapons)

<!-- npcs: Devil Enchant Master -->

The Devil Enchant Master adds one high stat enchant to the 4th slot of "Upgrade" weapons, using an Enchant Book.

**Where:** `/navi itemmall 29/71` (Cash Mall)

## Items it works on

The weapon must be equipped in your **right hand**:

{{ item(1292) }}, {{ item(1394) }}, {{ item(1491) }}, {{ item(1585) }}, {{ item(2015) }}, {{ item(13071) }},
{{ item(13115) }}, {{ item(16019) }}, {{ item(18112) }}, {{ item(21000) }}

You need 10,000 free weight and a free inventory slot.

## Enchanting

Choose **"I heard that you are the best!"**, then pick a series.

- **Cost:** 1 {{ item(6484) }} per try (used up on success and on failure). No zeny.
- **One enchant only**, in the 4th slot. If the weapon already has one, reset it first.
- Cards are always kept.
- **On failure** no enchant is added and the weapon **loses a random amount of refine**, anywhere from 0 up
  to all of its refine levels (each amount equally likely). The weapon itself never breaks.

## Reset

Choose **"Please initialize the enchant."** with the enchanted weapon equipped.

**Cost:** 100,000 zeny. Always succeeds. Removes the 4th-slot enchant; refine and cards are kept.
