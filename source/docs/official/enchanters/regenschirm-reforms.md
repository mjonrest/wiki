# Regenschirm Reforms and OS Weapon Enchants

<!-- npcs: Enchant Researcher; Weapon Researcher; Special Equipment Researcher -->

Three researchers in the Regenschirm Institute reform Thanos, OS and other weapons and armor, and enchant
the Modified OS weapons.

| NPC | Job | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Enchant Researcher (Torus) | Enchants Modified OS weapons | `/navi rgsr_in 112/167` |
| Weapon Researcher (Frasa) | Reforms Thanos weapons and OS weapons | `/navi rgsr_in 115/169` |
| Special Equipment Researcher (Stack) | Reforms other weapons and armor | `/navi rgsr_in 136/171` |

To get in, talk to the **Equipment Reform PR Agent** in Lighthalzen (`/navi lighthalzen 99/156`) or in the
item mall (`/navi itemmall 14/50`) and choose **Let's go.**

## How reforms work

Pick the reform in the menu, then put the item in the reform window from your **inventory** (unequip it
first). A reform always succeeds and turns the item into a new one. Unless the tables say otherwise,
enchants, the enchant grade and random options are kept.

- **Lower mods:** cheaper, refine **-5** (a +9 item becomes +4).
- **Intermediate mods:** more expensive, refine **-1** (a +9 item becomes +8).
- **Upper mods:** refine **+1** for weapons (see the tables for armor).

"No cards" means the item must have no cards in it, or the window refuses it.
