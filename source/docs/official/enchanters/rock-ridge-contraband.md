# Rock Ridge Contraband Processor

<!-- npcs: Contraband Processor -->

The Contraband Processor adds random stat enchants to the "contraband" gear sold in Rock Ridge.

**Where:** `/navi har_in01 17/74` (inside Rock Ridge)

## Items it works on

You must be **wearing** the item. Pick the slot (Armor, Garment or right Accessory) in the menu.

| Slot | Items | Max enchants |
|---|---|---|
| Armor | {{ item(15176) }}, {{ item(15177) }}, {{ item(15178) }}, {{ item(15179) }}, {{ item(15180) }} | 3 |
| Garment | {{ item(20820) }}, {{ item(20821) }}, {{ item(20822) }} | 3 |
| Accessory (right slot only) | {{ item(28441) }}, {{ item(28442) }} | 2 |

- The armor pieces are bought from **Howard** in the same building for 24 {{ item(25250) }} each. The garments
  (3,000,000z) and accessories (1,500,000z) are sold by the **Affable Lady** next to him.
- The item must not have a normal card in its 2nd, 3rd or 4th slot. A card in the first slot is fine and is kept.
- You need at least 10,000 free weight and a free inventory slot.

## Enchanting

**Cost:** 2 {{ item(25250) }} per try (one enchant per try). Enchanting **always succeeds** and never breaks the
item. Refine and the card in the first slot are kept.

Enchants are added one at a time (4th slot first, then 3rd, then 2nd for armor and garments). Once all enchant
slots are filled you must reset before enchanting again.

## Reset

Choose **"Reset contraband item Enchantment level."** while wearing an enchanted item.

- **Cost:** 2 {{ item(25250) }}.
- Removes **all** enchants. Refine and the first-slot card are kept.
- **Chance the item is destroyed:** 30% for armor and garments, 45% for accessories. A destroyed item is gone
  for good and the coins are not returned.

!!! tip
    The NPC says it charges "2 Rock Ridge Coins per socket". A reset costs 2 coins in total, no matter how many
    enchants the item has.
