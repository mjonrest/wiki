# Pudding (Bio Lab Enchants)

<!-- npcs: Sorcerer; Weird old man -->

Pudding, a trainee Sorcerer (the NPC is labelled **Sorcerer**), enchants the equipment that drops in or is traded
for in the Bio Laboratory, using the three Bio Lab materials {{ item(6469) }}, {{ item(6470) }} and {{ item(6471) }}. Enchanting
**can destroy the item**.

**Where:** `/navi lhz_cube 233/24`

## Items it works on

The item must be **equipped**. You need 1,200 free weight. There is no refine requirement.

| Enchant table | Items |
|---|---|
| Melee weapons | {{ item(1291) }}, {{ item(1392) }}, {{ item(1393) }}, {{ item(1435) }}, {{ item(13069) }}, {{ item(13070) }}, {{ item(16017) }}, {{ item(1490) }}* |
| Ranged weapons | {{ item(18109) }}, {{ item(18110) }}, {{ item(18111) }} |
| Magic weapons | {{ item(1584) }}, {{ item(1659) }} |
| Armor | {{ item(2160) }}, {{ item(2161) }}, {{ item(2162) }}, {{ item(2892) }}, {{ item(15044) }}, {{ item(2582) }}*, {{ item(18570) }}* |

Items marked * use a wider roll, which makes them slightly more likely to be destroyed (see the tables).

!!! warning "Known issue"
    {{ item(1290) }} is on Pudding's list of "discovered" weapons and the Weird old man trades it, but Pudding does not
    accept it: she always answers "This equipment cannot accept the power". It cannot be enchanted here.

## Enchanting

Pick **"Here you are, sir."**, choose the equipment slot, then the material.

| Material | Cost | Slot |
|---|---|---|
| {{ item(6469) }} | 10 | 4th slot |
| {{ item(6470) }} | 10 | 3rd slot |

- The slot must be empty ("This equipment already has the power of ...").
- Both slots use the same roll table. You can do them in any order.
- No zeny is needed.
- **On success** the item keeps its refine, cards and any other enchant.
- **On failure the item is destroyed**, with its cards and refine. The materials are used up either way.

## Removing an enchant

Pick **"Consume the 'Chill of the Dead'"** and choose which enchant to remove.

- **Cost:** 10 {{ item(6471) }} per enchant removed.
- Always succeeds. Only the chosen enchant is removed; refine, cards and the other enchant stay.
