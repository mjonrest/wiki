# Apprentice Craftsman (armor enchant)

<!-- npcs: Apprentice Craftsman -->

The Apprentice Craftsman adds one random +1 to +3 stat enchant to many classic armors. It is risky: the armor
can be destroyed, and refine and cards are always lost.

**Where:** `/navi prontera 165/60`

## Items it works on

The armor must be in your **inventory** (not equipped), and you must carry **exactly one** of it.

## Enchanting

**Cost:** 400,000 zeny per try (taken on success and on failure).

- One enchant only, in the 4th slot.
- On success you get back a **+0 armor with no cards** and the new enchant. Any old enchant is replaced.
- On failure the armor is **destroyed**.
