# Garden of Time Vending Machines

<!-- npcs: Subspace vending machine -->

Four **Subspace vending machines** in the Garden of Time trade, upgrade and enchant the Manteau of Mystical
Beast cloaks, the Circulation of Life cloaks and the Signets of Circulation.

| Machine | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| #1 | Trade windows for energies and consumables | `/navi t_garden 129/123` |
| #2 | Trade windows for the accessories and cloaks, and the cloak upgrade | `/navi t_garden 132/120` |
| #3 | Enchants the Manteau of Mystical Beast cloaks | `/navi t_garden 135/117` |
| #4 | Enchants the Signets and the Circulation of Life cloaks | `/navi t_garden 138/114` |

**Requirements:** the quest **Meeting with the Spirit** must be complete. The machines are hidden until you
walk up to them after that. You also need room for more items (the machines refuse to talk to you when your
inventory is too full or too heavy).

## Cloak upgrade (machine #2)

Choose **Upgrade Divine Beast's Cloak**. Put the cloak in the window from your **inventory** (unequip it
first). It always succeeds and keeps refine, cards and enchants.

## Enchanting (machines #3 and #4)

Pick the item in the menu, then the enchant window opens (only if you carry less than 70% of your weight
limit). Put the item in from your **inventory** (unequip it first). Refine, cards and the enchant grade are
never touched.

The cloaks get three fixed enchants: each slot has only one possible enchant and it always succeeds. There is
no reset for the cloaks.
