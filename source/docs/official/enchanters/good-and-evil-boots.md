# Boots of Good and Evil

<!-- npcs: Boots Enchanter -->

The **Boots Enchanter** in the Thanatos Tower lobby opens the enchant window for the job-specific
Boots of Good and Evil.

**Where:** `/navi tha_t01 159/83`

## How it works

1. Talk to the NPC and pick your boots' job from the list.
2. The enchant window opens (only if you carry less than 70% of your weight limit). Put the boots in from
   your **inventory** (unequip them first).
3. Pick an enchant. All enchants and upgrades here are selectable and always succeed.

Refine, cards and the enchant grade are never touched. Enchants go into slot 4 first, then slot 3.

## Reset

Removes all enchants (cards are kept). Cost: 30 × {{ item(25739) }}, 30 × {{ item(25740) }}, 1,500,000z. Success chance **90%**; on failure the cost is used up and the enchants stay.
