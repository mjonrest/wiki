# Malangdo Costume Enchanters

<!-- npcs: Aver De Dosh; Lace La Zard; Gregio Grumani; Designer Heidam -->

The Fashion Stone shop in Malangdo turns costume enchant stones into enchants on your costumes. Each stone gives one fixed enchant; there is no random roll except success or failure.

| NPC | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Aver De Dosh | Headgear costume stones (Upper, Middle, Lower) | `/navi mal_in01 22/113` |
| Lace La Zard | Costume garment stones (1st slot) and Dual stones (2nd slot) | `/navi mal_in01 20/107` |
| Gregio Grumani | Visual effect stones (4th slot) | `/navi mal_in01 24/121` |
| Designer Heidam | Trades costumes for Costume Enchant Stone Boxes | `/navi mal_in01 20/124` |

All three enchanters work on the costume you are **wearing**. Refine and existing enchants in other slots are kept. If the costume is account-bound, it stays account-bound.

## Aver De Dosh (headgear costumes)

**Where:** `/navi mal_in01 22/113`

- **Cost:** 1 stone per attempt.
- **Success chance:** 50%. On failure the stone is used up and the costume comes back unchanged.
- On success the new enchant replaces whatever was in that slot before.

| Stone type | Goes into | The costume must cover |
|---|---|---|
| Upper | 1st slot | the upper headgear position |
| Middle | 2nd slot | the middle headgear position |
| Lower | 3rd slot | the lower headgear position |

!!! tip "Which costume he picks"
    Aver only works on one costume: your upper costume if you wear one, otherwise your middle one, otherwise your lower one. To put a Middle stone on a separate middle costume, take off your upper costume first (and the middle one for a Lower stone).

On failure only the stone is lost; the costume keeps its old enchant.
