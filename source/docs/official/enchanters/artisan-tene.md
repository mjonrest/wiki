# Artisan Tene (Kagerou / Oboro gear)

<!-- npcs: Artisan Tene -->

Artisan Tene adds random stat enchants to the 4th and 3rd slots of Kagerou and Oboro weapons and armor.

**Where:** `/navi que_ng 75/20`

## Items it works on

The item must be **equipped** in the right place.

| Type | Items | Wear it in |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon | {{ item(13076) }}, {{ item(13078) }}, {{ item(13313) }}, {{ item(13314) }}, {{ item(13315) }} | Right hand |
| Armguard | {{ item(2172) }}, {{ item(2173) }} | Shield slot |
| Armor | {{ item(15054) }}, {{ item(15055) }}, {{ item(15056) }} | Armor slot |

## Enchanting

**Cost:** 100,000 zeny per enchant. Enchanting **always succeeds**. Refine and cards are kept, and the item
never breaks.

1. The first enchant goes into the **4th slot**.
2. The second enchant goes into the **3rd slot**.
3. After that the item is full; reset it to enchant again.

The possible enchants depend on the item's **refine level at the moment you enchant**.

## Removing enchants

Choose **"I want to remove option."**, then pick the item (it must be equipped).

**Cost:** 100,000 zeny. Always succeeds; refine and cards are kept.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Reset only the 3rd Slot | Removes the 3rd-slot enchant and keeps the 4th-slot one. Needs an enchant in the 3rd slot. |
| Reset all | Removes both enchants. Needs an enchant in the 4th slot. |
