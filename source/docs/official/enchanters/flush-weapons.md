# Flush Weapons (Flush MK4U)

<!-- npcs: Flush MK4U -->

**Flush MK4U** in Einbech turns the Einbech "safety" weapons into Flush weapons and enchants them.

**Where:** `/navi einbech 153/257`

| Menu | What it does |
|---|---|
| Refrorm Weapon | Pick a weapon, then the reform window opens |
| Buy Hammer | Sells {{ item(102124) }} for 10,000,000z |
| enchant flush weapon | Opens the enchant window |

## Reform

Pick the weapon in the menu, then put it in the reform window from your **inventory** (unequip it first).
The reform always succeeds and the weapon changes into its Flush version.

What every reform does to the weapon:

- The refine goes back to **+0**.
- All random options are **removed**.
- Cards and enchants are kept, except for Metal Detector Mk47-2 (see below).

## Enchant

1. Choose **enchant flush weapon**.
2. The enchant window opens (only if you carry less than 70% of your weight limit). Put the weapon in from
   your **inventory** (unequip it first).
3. Pick an enchant. Every enchant here is selectable (perfect) and always succeeds; you then upgrade it
   step by step. Refine, cards and the enchant grade are never touched.
