# Enchanters

Official NPCs that enchant, socket, upgrade or reform gear on Miracle. Each page lists where the NPC stands, which
items it accepts, the cost and success chance of every step, every possible enchant with its chance, and what
happens on failure. Everything is read from the server scripts.

Enchants opened through the enchant window (`item_enchant`) and Laphine items are also in the
[Enchants database](../../db/enchants.md). Miracle's own enchanters are on the [Enchants](../../enchants/index.md) pages,
and enchanters inside instances are explained on each [instance page](../instances/index.md).
