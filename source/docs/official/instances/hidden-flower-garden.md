# Hidden Flower Garden

## Guide

The Hidden Flower Garden is the story instance of Episode 17.2 that introduces the automatic dolls' secret garden behind Varmundt's Mansion. It is mostly a guided tour with conversations, plus one short fight. Clearing it gives you **Security Clearance**, which unlocks the daily [Security Area 1](security-area-1.md) and [Security Area 2](security-area-2.md).

### How to enter

- **Where:** Walk up to the security perimeter at `/navi ba_maison 120/320` in Varmundt's Mansion. The hidden **Flower Garden Manager** appears.
- **Story:** You need to have started the Episode 17.2 main story.
- **Party:** You need a party, and only the **party leader** can talk to the manager. Pick **"Authenticate Identity"** to create the instance. Then talk to the manager again and pick **"Enter"**.
- The manager only offers this story version until you have finished it once. After that, she offers the Security Areas instead.

### Walkthrough

1. **Control screen.** Inside, touch the screen of the device (`56/278`). It may take a few tries ("Try entering a different pattern"). An automatic doll boots up and asks you to act as a temporary administrator, then asks you to **name her**. She guides you from here on.
2. **The garden tour.** Follow the doll and talk to her and the other dolls along the way. She takes you past the carrot fields, a wandering Pitaya keeper and the dolls' workplaces. The instance moves you from area to area as the story goes on.
3. **Intruder alert.** At the third area (around `201/287`), follow the doll. The security system detects you and sends **3 Surveillance Dolls** ({{ mob(1739) }}). Defeat all three, then talk to the doll again. Ask your questions until **"Guide me to the security area."** appears.
4. **Sigma.** You are moved to the security area entrance (`85/106`). The doll leads you to **Sigma**, who looks just like Varmundt. Talk through the whole conversation and answer his questions. At the end, another Red Pepper doll cuts in and shuts the area down.
5. **Reward.** Talk to Sigma one last time to finish the quest and leave.

### Rewards

| Reward | Notes |
|---|---|
| {{ item(1000103) }} ×20 | First clear only. Completes "Security Clearance" |
| 20–40 [Instance Points](../../content/instances.md) | Counts toward the daily 1,200 cap |

You are returned to `ba_maison 136/309`.
