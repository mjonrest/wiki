# Confused Snake's Nest

## Guide

Confused Snake's Nest is a story instance from the **Episode 19 main quest** (*Chaotic Snake Nest*). The upper-class
Rgan have suddenly turned violent, and you follow Aurelie, Leon, Voglinde and Miriam through the nest to find out
why. Along the way you shut down the injection devices that are mutating the Rgan. There is no item reward:
finishing it moves your main quest on to *Those Who Vanished*.

### Entering

- **Requirement:** you must be on the *Chaotic Snake Nest* step of the Episode 19 main story. Talk to **Lehar** at
  `/navi jor_que 32/37` to see the blocked entrance scene.
- **Party:** you must be in a party. One member talks to Lehar again and picks *Yes, signal.* to create the
  instance, then everyone picks *I'll go in.*
- **Time limit:** the instance lasts **1 hour** and closes after 5 minutes with nobody inside.

Party members who are on the same story step all get the quest update at the end.

### Walkthrough

The nest is one big map. Important targets are marked on your minimap as you go.

**Area 1: the entrance**

1. Walk up to Aurelie's group at `1@jorchs 261/34`. Monsters spawn through the area.
2. Kill all the {{ mob(21535) }} in the area.
3. Find the two **Rgan the Scared** at `1@jorchs 249/79` and `1@jorchs 114/20`. Each turns into a
   {{ mob(21534) }} when you get close. Kill both.
4. When both tasks are done, the group gathers at `1@jorchs 142/58`. Walk up to Horr to continue.

**Area 2: the west tunnels**

1. Kill all the Split Rgan in the area.
2. Find the two tied-up Rgan at `1@jorchs 23/57` and `1@jorchs 21/97` and kill the Frightened Rgan they turn into.
3. Walk up to Horr at `1@jorchs 112/119`. You are ambushed by two {{ mob(21536) }} and two Rgan, and then a second
   group of five. Kill both groups.
4. Walk up to Horr again to move on.

**Area 3: the first injection device**

1. Clear the Rgan along the path to the east (7 monsters).
2. Walk up to Voglinde at `1@jorchs 251/171`. The injection unit starts.
3. Step forward to `1@jorchs 243/166`. The wall of Rgan in front of you comes to life: kill all 8.
4. Click the **Injection device** at `1@jorchs 219/148`. Until you do, more mutated Rgan keep spawning nearby every
   30 seconds.
5. Kill the Modified Heart Hunters that come out.

**Area 4: two more devices**

1. Monsters spawn around the northern area. Click both **Injection devices**, at `1@jorchs 191/203` and
   `1@jorchs 226/244`, and kill every monster in the area.
2. Talk to Aurelie at `1@jorchs 151/179`. Click the next **Injection device** at `1@jorchs 192/206` and kill the
   monsters it releases.
3. Talk to Aurelie again. The wall of monsters at `1@jorchs 138/179` attacks: kill all 9.

**Area 5: the research devices**

1. Click the **Research devices** at `1@jorchs 24/142` and `1@jorchs 20/195`, and clear the monsters in the area.
2. Walk up to the Modified Heart Hunter at `1@jorchs 39/251`. Three waves attack: 6, then 8, then 12 monsters. One
   monster in the last wave takes only a tenth of normal damage.
3. Click the **Center door** at `1@jorchs 37/274`.
4. Step into the portal at `1@jorchs 36/257`. Your quest updates and you are sent to `jor_back3`.

!!! tip
    Mutated Rgan keep spawning near each injection or research device until it is switched off, so turn off the
    devices first and then clean up.
