## Instance Points

Claiming the reward from the Wizard Professor gives [Instance Points](../../content/instance-points.md) based on the level you cleared:

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instance Points | 40-60 | 50-70 | 60-80 | 70-90 | 80-100 |

Only clearing your highest unlocked level unlocks the next one.
