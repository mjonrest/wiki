# Farm Lost in Time

## Guide

The Farm Lost in Time is a short daily instance in Varmundt's Mansion (Episode 17.2). The clumsy automatic doll **Silk** leads your party through six Pitaya habitats. You "play with" each group of Pitayas, then lure out and defeat **Boss Meow**. Pitaya tails you collect along the way are turned in for **Barmil Tickets**.

### How to enter

- **NPC:** Luina, in front of the farming facility at `/navi ba_maison 311/206`.
- **Level:** Base Level 150 or higher.
- **Story:** You must have reached the Episode 17.2 main quest point where you can visit the farm. Talk to Luina twice to finish her introduction ("Keeping Breeding Ground").
- **Party:** You need a party, even for one person. The party leader picks **"Create Farm Lost in Time"**. Then everyone picks **"Enter the dungeon."**.
- **Cooldown:** Entering starts a daily cooldown that resets at **04:00** server time ("Daily farm experience - Standby"). Talk to Luina once after it ends to clear it.
- **Inventory:** You need room for 20 Barmil Tickets.

!!! note
    Entering also gives you the "Tail Collection" quest. While it is active, every Pitaya you defeat in the farm has a **15% chance** to drop a Pitaya tail matching its colour. Any tails still in your inventory are taken away the next time you talk to Luina, so turn them in before you leave.

### Walkthrough

All Silk conversations must be done by the **party leader**.

1. **Start area.** The leader walks to the start line near `161/40`. A **Wooden Box** (Silk hiding) appears. The leader talks to it and 7 Pitayas pounce. Defeat them all, then talk to **Silk**.
2. **Second habitat.** Everyone is moved to `218/149`. The leader talks to Silk. She scatters food, and 7 more Pitayas come out. Clear them and talk to Silk again.
3. **Sand playground.** Everyone is moved to `86/101`. Same as before: talk, clear, talk. Silk counts down how many are left.
4. **Oasis.** Everyone is moved to `226/256`. Talk, clear, talk.
5. **Sleepy area.** Everyone is moved to `242/117`. Talk, clear, talk.
6. **Last habitat.** Everyone is moved to `116/165`. Talk, clear, talk. Silk then marks **two Big Meows** on the minimap, at `38/253` (west) and `287/253` (east).

### The trap path

Pick **one** of the two Big Meows and walk up to it. This spawns a Big Meow and arms **5 trap spots** along the path from that side to the boss nest at the top of the map.

- Walking over each trap spot releases a group of 5 Pitayas. One spot on the west path releases 10.
- You must set off **all 5 traps** and defeat every Pitaya from them, plus the Big Meow. After that, the Wooden Box at the nest (`161/315`) is ready.

### Boss Meow

The leader talks to the **Wooden Box** at `161/315`. {{ mob(20659) }} appears together with **17 Pitayas** of every colour.

- More trap spots on both sides of the nest (around `129–135/317` and `195–201/317`) release 5 extra Pitayas each when someone steps on them.
- The adds are **not** removed when Boss Meow dies. Clean them up or ignore them.

After Boss Meow is defeated, the leader talks to the Wooden Box, then to **Silk**. After that, **every player** talks to Silk to turn in their tails and leave.

### Rewards

Silk takes all your Pitaya tails ({{ item(1000093) }}, {{ item(1000094) }}, {{ item(1000095) }}, {{ item(1000096) }}, {{ item(1000097) }}) and pays in {{ item(1000103) }}:

| Tails turned in | Barmil Tickets |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 20 | 2 |
| 20 or more | 4 |
| Bonus: you have a {{ item(1000098) }} | +2 |

Leaving through Silk also gives **20–40 [Instance Points](../../content/instances.md)**, which count toward the daily 1,200 cap.

**First clear bonus:** After your first run, talk to Luina outside to finish "It's Boss Meow!". You get **20 Barmil Tickets** and **3,094,009 Base EXP**.

!!! tip
    - Tails only drop while the "Tail Collection" quest is active, so make sure every member entered through Luina.
    - Collecting 20 or more tails doubles your ticket reward, so kill every Pitaya you see, including the Boss Meow adds.
