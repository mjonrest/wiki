# Bangungot Hospital 2F

## Guide

The second floor of Port Malaya's hospital has been taken over by the nightmare spirit Bangungot. Clear his minions
ward by ward, fight him in the boss room while he drags the party into the patient wards below, and finish the
**Pillar of Spirit** before it revives him.

### How to enter

| | |
|---|---|
| **NPC** | Nurse Maenne in the hospital: `/navi ma_dun01 147/10` |
| **Level** | 100+ |
| **Party** | Required. The party leader prepares the second floor, then everyone selects "Enter the second floor." |
| **Entry limit** | Once per day per character; the lockout ends at the next **04:00** server time |
| **Time limit** | 1 hour |

!!! note "First visit vs. later visits"
    Your first run only counts as *"Explore Hospital 2F"*. From your next day on, every entry also gives the
    hunting quest *"Get Rid of Bangungot from Hospital 2F"*, which is what pays the reward below.

### Walkthrough

1. **The corridor.** Walk forward from the start; a {{ mob(2339) }} appears. Killing it starts a chain through the
   wards along the corridor: each ward spawns more Mangkukulam (2, 2, 3, 3, then 4), and clearing one starts the
   next.
2. **The sixth ward.** After the fifth ward, a {{ mob(2338) }} appears in the last ward (around 96/140). Killing it
   summons **Bangungot** ({{ mob(2317) }}) in the boss room to the east, and the boss-room doors (around 105/157)
   open.
3. **Boss room.** Fight Bangungot. Entering the boss room starts his pattern:
    - About **55 seconds** in, he sends his minions to torment the patients downstairs, and at about
      **60 seconds** the floor of the boss room and the upper wards warps everyone to the lower floor.
    - Downstairs, touching the spot near 118/63 starts the ward event: about 10 seconds later 6
      {{ mob(2340) }} and Mangkukulam appear in one random patient ward (a patient screams to show which one).
    - **Kill all of them** to stop the pattern and reopen the way back up. If you are slow, extra
      {{ mob(2338) }} spawn in the boss room (at roughly 90, 110 and 130 seconds), and after about 130 seconds
      the lower floor sends you back up anyway.
    - The pattern starts again each time you walk back into the boss room.
4. **Pillar of Spirit.** When Bangungot dies, a **Pillar of Spirit** ({{ mob(2327) }}) appears in the centre of
   the boss room (131/157) and heals him. You have about **30 seconds** to destroy it; if you fail, Bangungot comes
   back to life a few seconds later and you have to kill him again.
5. **Exit.** Destroying the pillar ends the run. Talk to the **Exit** NPC that appears at 133/161 to leave.

!!! tip
    Bring area damage for the Tiyanak wave downstairs, and keep burst damage ready for the pillar: it only stays
    vulnerable for about 30 seconds.
