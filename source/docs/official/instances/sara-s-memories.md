# Sara's Memories

## Guide

Part 1 of the *Heroes' Trail* story. Adventurer Leon sends you through a dimensional rift into the past, where you
witness how young Sara Irene came to believe her father killed her mother. The run is a chase across one large map:
clear each group of Payon guards to open the path to wherever Sara ran next, then face Doyen Irene himself.

### How to enter

| | |
|---|---|
| **NPC** | Adventurer Leon in the Dimensional Gap: `/navi dali 130/107` |
| **Portal** | Dimensional Device: `/navi dali 139/118` |
| **Level** | 99+ |
| **Party** | Required. The party leader boots up the device, then everyone uses it to enter |
| **Prerequisite** | Talk to Adventurer Leon first (he accepts you on the first visit; repeat runs need his OK again, see below) |
| **Cooldown** | Once per day per character: after entering you must wait until the next **04:00** server time |
| **Time limit** | 1 hour |

### Walkthrough

Only the **party leader** can advance the story by talking to Sara and Doyen Irene.

1. **The girl.** Talk to the girl at the start (around 260/156). After the scene a portal opens to the west
   (240/145).
2. **Escape with Sara.** Talk to Sara (107/325) and choose *"Escape with Sara."* Payon guards flood the area:
   {{ mob(2543) }} and {{ mob(2545) }}. **Every guard in the wave must die** before the next portal opens.
3. **The chase.** Each time a wave is cleared, Sara runs off again and a portal opens towards her; the minimap marks
   where to go. The next waves mix in {{ mob(2544) }} and {{ mob(2546) }}.

    | Wave | Area | Next portal |
    |---|---|---|
    | 1 | West village (around 107/325 down to 206/251) | 208/250 |
    | 2 | North-east (around 230/311 down to 220/203) | 226/190 |
    | 3 | South (around 255/91 across to 81/67) | 166/67 |
    | 4 | Centre | 155/180 |

4. **Doyen Irene.** Talk to Sara (152/233). Eight guards attack first; when they are down, {{ mob(2542) }} draws
   his blade and fights you himself.
5. **The truth.** After Doyen Irene falls, talk to him (156/236). Follow the marked portal (88/188) to find Sara
   (39/142), talk to her, then take the exit at 16/143 back to the Dimensional Gap.

!!! tip
    The guards of a wave are spread over a large area. If the portal does not open, someone has missed a few guards
    further along the path.

### Rewards

| When | Reward |
|---|---|
| Every clear (exit portal) | 20 ~ 40 Instance Points, see [Instance Points](../../content/instances.md) |
| First clear, report to Leon | 770,000 base EXP and 1,000,000 job EXP |
| Repeat clears, report to Leon | 550,000 base EXP and 550,000 job EXP |

To repeat the story: after reporting, talk to Leon again once your cooldown has ended and agree to relive the past.
Leon must be asked again before every repeat run, and the EXP is only given if you leave through the exit portal at
the end.
