# High Priest's Villa

## Guide

The High Priest's Villa is a **one-time story instance** from the Episode 18 main quest. With Miriam and the guide
Ira you search the villa of Yus Amal, find the keys to its locked rooms, beat Schulang the Traitor, and finally face the
Twisted God. Clearing it advances the main quest; there is no item reward. The repeatable version is Villa of
Deception.

### Entering

- **Quest:** only at the matching step of the Episode 18 main quest. **Ira** appears at `/navi oz_dun01 219/117`
  when you reach it.
- **Party:** the **party leader** picks *Apply for 'High Priest's Villa'*; then everyone picks *Enter*.
- **Cooldown:** none.
- **Time limit:** 1 hour.

Most steps inside only react to the **party leader**.

### Walkthrough

**1. Ground floor**

1. The party leader talks to **Miriam** at the entrance and listens to her and Ira. Talk to her again: she goes to
   question one employee and asks you to find another.
2. Talk to the **Villa Employee** to the west and ask about Amal. Then return to Miriam.
3. Two disguised {{ mob(21312) }} attack. Kill them.
4. Back at the entrance, the party leader talks to **Miriam** again. 12 more Heart Hunters attack along the hall.
   When they are dead Miriam walks north up the central stairs; follow her and talk to her at the top.

**2. The keys**

1. Miriam heads east and 11 Heart Hunters appear along the **west** corridor. Kill them all to find the **Silver Key**
   at the north end of the corridor.
2. Use the Silver Key on the **Locked Door** on the west side. Kill the 3 Heart Hunters in the room, then pick up the
   **Gold Key**.
3. Talk to **Miriam** in the east wing, then talk to the **Villa Employees** in the east room.
4. Open the **Locked Door** to the central room with the key. 11 Heart Hunters spawn around it; kill them all.

**3. The garden**

1. Click both **Brilliant Articles** on either side of the ornate door to the north, then go through the door.
2. Five devices are spread across the garden. **Only one of them**, chosen at random, opens the way: use them (5 seconds
   each) until the right one works.
3. Go through the **Elegant Door** to the north.

**4. Schulang**

The party leader talks to **Schulang**. He attacks together with two Heart Hunters: kill {{ mob(21314) }} and both
guards. The way into the last room opens.

**5. The Twisted God**

The party leader talks to **Yus Amal**. After the revelation, {{ mob(21315) }} appears together with a resurrected
Schulang, who immediately drops **Cane of Evil Eye** on four spots south of the throne, where you are standing. Only
the Twisted God has to die; the resurrected Schulang disappears with it.

### Rewards

Talk to **Miriam** in the boss room afterwards to advance the Episode 18 main quest. The **exit** NPC next to her takes
you back to `oz_dun01`.
