# OS Occupation

## Guide

OS Occupation is the story memorial that follows **Old Memories** in Episode 17.1. You join Est's rebel squad as call sign **Echo** and storm the OS facility. You hold the gas-filled entrance, follow the missing Alpha team's trail through two outposts, and face the mutant waiting at the end.

### Requirements and entry

| | |
|---|---|
| Quest | Finish **Old Memories**, then take **OS Occupation** from Est |
| NPC | **Est** at `/navi sp_cor 163/56` |
| Party | Required. Only the party leader can prepare the memorial. |
| Time limit | 2 hours |

Est is hidden until you have finished Old Memories. Walk up to her spot in Cor and she appears. Talk to her once to get the **OS Occupation** quest. Then the leader talks to her again and picks **Prepare for OS Occupation.**, and everyone picks **Enter**.

### Walkthrough

**1. Holding the entrance**

1. The memorial starts when the party leader steps forward from the landing point (`30/352`). The rebel squad and Est appear, and poison gas lingers around the entrance.
2. While Est talks on the radio, up to 12 incomplete mutants ({{ mob(20348) }}, {{ mob(20349) }} and {{ mob(20350) }}) spawn around the entrance, one every 10 seconds. Fight them off.
3. When Est calls for Echo, click the **radio** next to her (`29/360`) and send any reply. Do it a second time when she asks you to check Alpha's coordinates.
4. Foxtrot's artillery wipes out every mutant and the gas around the entrance. Est then sends you the coordinates of Alpha's last contact.

**2. Following Alpha's trail**

1. Go to the radio at `52/177` and report that there is only a radio, no trace of Alpha. Eight mutants spawn along `63/214-242`.
2. Kill them all. The map announces how many survivors are left every 10 seconds. When it says **Intermediate point secured.**, a marker appears at `84/256`. Step on it.
3. Thirteen more mutants spawn along the road around `126-174/266`, plus another gas cloud. Clear them and step on the marker at `163/266`.
4. Fifteen mutants spawn around `132-151/196-243`. Clear them and step on the marker at `138/174`.

**3. The last point**

1. A mutant marked **????** (a {{ mob(20347) }}) appears at `161/178`. It spawns with only **5% of its HP**, so it goes down quickly.
2. When it dies, **Est** and the fallen **Miguel** appear at `165/176`. Talk to Est to finish the story.

### Rewards

You get these from Est while your **OS Occupation** quest is active:

| Reward | Amount |
|---|---|
| {{ item(25668) }} | 1 |
| {{ item(25723) }} | 5 |
| Base / Job EXP | 300,000 / 300,000 |

Est then sends you back to `/navi sp_cor 162/63`. The quest is completed, so the reward is one-time.

!!! tip
    After the story, the **Operation Officer** next to Est in Cor opens the repeatable version of this map, **2nd OS Search**.
