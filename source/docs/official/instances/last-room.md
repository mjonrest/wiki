# Last Room

## Guide

The Last Room is a short instance behind a sealed door underground. You follow Verity's group into an abandoned facility, survive its automated defense protocols, and confront the mysterious machine **T_W_O** in the Mother Room.

### How to enter

- **NPC:** Mark, `/navi un_myst 163/38`.
- **Level:** Base Level 150 or higher.
- **Party:** You need a party. The party leader picks **"Open door."**. Then everyone picks **"Go in."**.
- **Cooldown:** Entering starts a **4 hour** cooldown ("Final Room"). Talk to Mark once after it ends to clear it.
- On your first entry you also get the hunting quest "Final Room", which asks you to defeat T_W_O.

### Step 1: First protocol

1. The **party leader** talks to **Verity** near the entrance. She suddenly feels ill, and Tamarin stays behind with her.
2. Walk through the door at `140/45`. The facility wakes up ("Intruder detected"), and the first protocol starts:
    - **24 {{ mob(3251) }} / {{ mob(3252) }}** spawn along the corridor, from the entrance up toward `67/97`.
    - One {{ mob(3253) }} spawns somewhere in the western area (around `30–70 / 33–73`).
    - If the protocol is still running after 5 minutes, another 24 Machine Components spawn, and again at 10 minutes.
3. The step ends when **every** monster of the protocol is dead, including that one System message. Then **Tamarin** appears at `67/97`.

!!! tip
    Do not forget the lone System message in the west. The protocol does not end while it is alive, and the extra waves keep coming.

### Step 2: Zone 2

The **party leader** talks to **Tamarin**. He tells you Verity ran ahead, and the warp at `100/121` opens to Zone 2.

- The floor of Zone 2 has **15 cracks**. Stepping on one sets off an alarm, and **3 Machine Components** spawn in the south-east part of the zone.
- Follow the path to **Verity** at `224/29` and pick **"Go inside."** to enter the Mother Room.

### Step 3: Mother Room, T_W_O

Talk to **Verity** inside the Mother Room. A few seconds later the final protocol starts and {{ mob(3254) }} appears.

- T_W_O uses Piercing Attack, Silence Attack, Critical Wounds, and wide Sleep, Stone and Soul Drain.
- At 30 seconds, then 1:30, 2:30 and 3:30 into the fight, **4 System messages** spawn around the room. They cast **Heal** on allies whose HP is below 50%, including T_W_O. Kill them as soon as they appear (they only have 100 HP).

When T_W_O dies, the remaining System messages disappear and **Verity** comes back to her senses.

### Rewards

- T_W_O's boss drops (see below).
- Talking to Verity after the fight gives **20–40 [Instance Points](../../content/instances.md)**, which count toward the daily 1,200 cap. She also warps you out to `un_bunker 299/162`.
