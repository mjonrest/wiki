# Oz Labyrinth

## Guide

Oz Labyrinth (the Maze of Oz) is a short story memorial from the Episode 18 main quest **Gray Children**. Miriam and her companions guide you through the ash-covered tunnels below the fields near Rachel. There are only a few small fights on the way, and the run ends when you climb out on the far side of the canyon.

### Requirements and entry

| | |
|---|---|
| Quest | Episode 18 main story, at **Gray Children (14)** or **(16)** |
| NPC | **Merchant** (Disguised Miriam) at `/navi ra_fild10 179/176` |
| Party | Required. Miriam only lets the **party leader** in, so this is effectively a solo memorial; use a one-person party. |
| Time limit | 1 hour |

On your first talk at **Gray Children (14)**, Miriam moves your quest on to **Gray Children (16)**. Talk to her again, pick **Open the door.**, then talk once more and pick **Enter.**

### Walkthrough

1. Talk to **Miriam** at the entrance (`282/175`). She warns you about the lava ash and tells you to head north.
2. Talk to Miriam again at `271/272`, then at the bridge (`241/272`). She crosses the bridge first. Follow her west.
3. At the far end of the bridge (`157/272`), talk to Miriam. Two {{ mob(21295) }} attack. Kill both. **Maram** joins the group and tells you to keep heading down to the fork in the road.
4. Talk to Maram at `98/172`. A scream rings out, and Maram and Miriam run ahead to `68/165`.
5. Talk to Maram there. A {{ mob(21295) }} and a {{ mob(21296) }} attack **Imril**. Kill both to save him.
6. Follow the path north. When you reach `58/256`, Maram appears at `44/255`. Talk to her, then follow the road down to `21/189`.
7. Talk to Maram at `21/189`. Your quest moves on to **Gray Children (17)** and the **Rope** next to her (`28/190`) unlocks. Climb it to come out at `/navi gw_fild01 275/337`.

!!! note
    Several hidden spots along the route (eight in total) each spawn one {{ mob(21295) }} and one {{ mob(21296) }} the first time someone walks over them. They are optional fights.

!!! tip
    The Rope at the entrance (`284/164`) takes you back up to `/navi ra_fild10 173/176` if you need to leave early.

### Rewards

The memorial gives no items or Instance Points. Its reward is your progress in the **Gray Children** quest.
