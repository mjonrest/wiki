# Twilight Garden

## Guide

Twilight Garden is a one-time story instance from the Episode 17.2 main quest (*Pest Extermination*). With Est and Almond you fight through the Heart Hunters in the garden and take down Sweety. The same maps are reused by the daily *Hey! Sweety* instance, which is entered separately.

### Requirements and entry

| | |
|---|---|
| **NPC** | Est: `/navi ba_in01 51/157` |
| **Prerequisite** | Episode 17.2 main quest, after Est gives you *Pest Extermination* |
| **Party** | Required. Only the party leader can create the instance |
| **Time limit** | 60 minutes (closes after 5 minutes with nobody inside) |
| **Repeatable** | No, Est only offers it until you have beaten Sweety |

1. Talk to Est to receive *Pest Extermination* (kill Sweety).
2. The party leader talks to Est again and picks **Create 'Twilight Garden'.**
3. Everyone picks **Enter 'Twilight Garden'.** You arrive at `1@bamn 100/320`.

### Walkthrough

The early steps only react to the **party leader**; later NPCs can be clicked by anyone.

1. **Entrance.** The leader steps forward to make Est appear, then talks to her. Five Heart Hunters show up to the south-east. When the leader walks up to them, 6 {{ mob(20681) }} attack. Kill them all.
2. **Second ambush.** The leader talks to Est again (`119/299`). Five more {{ mob(20681) }} spawn around `210/283`. Kill them.
3. **Meet Almond.** Est and Almond wait at `202/274`. Talk to Est, then to Almond, who walks ahead to `245/248`.
4. **The Repeater.** Talk to Almond again: 4 {{ mob(20681) }} spawn at `247/207`. Kill them, then talk to the **Intruder** (`242/206`) to spawn 2 {{ mob(20699) }}. Kill both.
5. **To the rebels.** Talk to Almond. She walks east to `327/189`. Talk to her again and follow her to the rebel standoff near `326/158`; walking up to it moves the scene along and the Intruder appears at `333/141`. Talk to it.
6. **Regroup.** Talk to Almond once more and follow her to `261/87`, where Est is waiting at `257/84`. Talk to Est to open the way to Sweety.
7. **Path to the warp.** Head to the warp at `33/224`. Three ambush points on the way (around `67/195`, `78/204` and `61/214`) each spawn 3 {{ mob(20699) }} when you walk past.
8. **Sweety.** The warp takes you to `1@bamq 103/39`. Walk up to the Intruder at `125/39` and {{ mob(20642) }} (500,000 HP) attacks. Killing it completes *Pest Extermination*.
9. Take the exit warp at `101/39` (leads to `ba_maison 33/220`) and report back to Est.

### Rewards

This is a story step: there is no item reward inside the instance. Reporting to Est after killing Sweety advances the Episode 17.2 main quest (she takes back the {{ item(1000092) }}).
