# Heart Hunter War Base 1

## Guide

Heart Hunter War Base 1 is the Rebellion's assault on the Heart Hunter base in the **Terra Gloria** main quest. You
follow Julian through three security lines, blowing up the barricades with gunpowder, then survive an encounter with
Ebel until Julian can stop her. It is a **one-time story step**: after you clear it, Julian sends you to
[Heart Hunter War Base 2](heart-hunter-war-base-2.md) instead.

### Entering

- **Quest:** you must have reached the Heart Hunter base step of the Terra Gloria main quest.
- **Briefing:** talk to **Julian** at `/navi ein_fild04 281/337` to get the *Heart Hunter Base occupation* quest. You
  need a party to talk to him.
- **Create:** the **party leader** talks to Julian again and picks *I'm ready*.
- **Enter:** everyone talks to the **Striker Unit Commander** at `/navi ein_fild04 275/342`.
- **Cooldown:** entering starts *Base maintenance*, which ends at the next **04:00** server time.
- **Time limit:** 1 hour.

!!! tip
    Which base Julian opens depends on the **party leader's** story progress, and the commander sends you to the base
    that matches **your own** progress. Party up with players at the same step, or the commander cannot find your
    instance.

### Walkthrough

!!! warning "Robot turrets"
    The robots along each security line hit anyone who walks next to them for **40% of their Max HP**. Wait for Julian
    to disable them.

**Start.** Walk forward from the entrance. Julian gives a short speech, the Rebellion soldiers move out, and
24 {{ mob(3627) }} appear in the yard ahead.

**Section 1**

1. Walk up to Julian at the first barricade (around 105, 50). He warns about the security system and a **Pile of
   gunpowder** appears to the north-west (around 58, 90).
2. Click the pile. 5 {{ item(25154) }} drop on the ground next to it; pick them up. The alarm sounds and about 9 seconds
   later 24 more Heart Hunter Guards arrive near the barricade.
3. Give the gunpowder to Julian. He blows up the barricade, which also kills all the guards in the section, and
   disables the robots in the gap.

**Section 2**

1. Walk through to Julian at the second barricade (around 140, 50). He sends you north to check for threats.
2. Push the **Suspicious instrument** (around 150, 119). 16 Heart Hunter Guards spawn around it. Each guard you kill
   has a 5% chance to call an {{ mob(3626) }}. A counter shows how many are left.
3. When they are all dead, go back to Julian. He blows up the second barricade.

**Section 3**

1. Walk on to Julian at the last barricade (around 238, 39). He has run out of gunpowder, and 18 Heart Hunter Guards
   attack.
2. Find the **Pile of gunpowder** to the west (around 163, 58) and click it for 5 more {{ item(25154) }}.
3. Give the gunpowder to Julian. He opens the last barricade.

**Ebel**

Walk east to the communication room (around 310, 47). After a long conversation, {{ mob(3628) }} appears. You do not
have to kill her: hold out for about **30 seconds** until Julian fires his paralyzing shot and she is removed. Warps at
the room's exits push you back if you try to run.

### Rewards

Talk to **Julian** afterwards. If this is your first clear (your *Heart Hunter Base occupation* quest is still open),
you get **10 {{ item(25155) }}**, the quest completes and your story moves on. Julian then sends you back to
`ein_fild04`.
