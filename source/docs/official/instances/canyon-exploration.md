# Canyon Exploration

## Guide

Canyon Exploration is a short story instance from the **Episode 20 main quest**. You follow Lehar into the canyon,
overhear Bagot and Lasgand plotting, and slip past the guards with the help of a cat. There are no monsters to
fight and no item reward: finishing it moves your main quest on to *Return to the Ice Castle*.

### Entering

- **Requirement:** you must be on the *Canyon Exploration* step of the Episode 20 main story. Walk up to
  `/navi icecastle 202/168` and **Lehar** appears next to you at `icecastle 201/171`.
- **Party:** you must be in a party, and only the **party leader** can talk to Lehar. The leader picks
  *Prepare Canyon Exploration* and then *Enter Canyon Exploration*.
- **Time limit:** the instance lasts **1 hour** and closes after 5 minutes with nobody inside.

!!! warning
    Lehar only lets the party leader in, so each character has to run this step as party leader (a party of one
    works).

### Walkthrough

The minimap guide arrow points you to each next stop.

1. Talk to **Lehar** at the entrance. She sends you east.
2. Two **Strange Iwin** appear as you get close. Talk to the one at `1@20cn1 171/105` to be moved to the second
   canyon.
3. Talk to **Lehar** at `1@20cn2 271/134`. You overhear Bagot and Lasgand's conversation. Talk to her again and a
   cat shows up.
4. Once the cat has walked over, talk to it. It takes you to the north-west of the canyon.
5. Talk to **Lehar** at `1@20cn2 62/258`, then to her again at `1@20cn2 64/224` after you have watched the Rgan
   and the fake Iwin.
6. You are moved to `1@20cn2 101/95`. Talk to the **cat** at `1@20cn2 107/65`. An alarm goes off and the cat runs
   ahead. Talk to it again at `1@20cn2 96/65` as the Rgan guards arrive.
7. Head to the south-west corner and talk to **Lehar** at `1@20cn2 32/22`.
8. Step into the portal at `1@20cn2 33/15`. Bagot finds what he was looking for, your quest updates and you are
   sent back to `icecastle`.
