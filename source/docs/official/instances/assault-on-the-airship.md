# Assault on the Airship

## Guide

A short **solo** instance. Loki and Fenrir's airship is attacked by Wyverns mid-flight. You clear three decks of
monsters, then deal with the Wyvern and the captain, who loses control of himself. It is quick, and every floor
works the same way: kill everything, then move on.

### Entering

- **Level:** 125 or higher.
- **Party:** you must be in a party, but **only the party leader can enter**. Make a one-person party.
- **Entrance:** the **Dimensional Device** at `/navi dali02 142/81`. Pick *Power Up the Device* to create the
  instance, then click the device again and pick *Use the Device*.
- **Cooldown:** the first entry starts the cooldown. For **5 minutes** after that you can still go back in (for
  example after dying). After that you are locked out until the next **04:00** server time. The device says
  "23 hours", but the real reset is 04:00.

### Walkthrough

You start on the top deck (`1@air1`) next to Loki, Fenrir, Iris, Chaos and **Captain Ferlock**.

1. **Talk to Captain Ferlock.** A Wyvern unit attacks and the first floor fills with monsters. A warp opens near
   the start of the deck that leads below.
2. **Passenger deck:** kill all 30 monsters, 15× {{ mob(3177) }} and 15× {{ mob(3175) }}. Every monster on a floor
   has to die before you can go on.
3. **Lower deck:** two warps now lead further down. Kill all 30 monsters there: 15× {{ mob(3176) }} and
   15× {{ mob(3179) }}.
4. **Talk to Iris** at the engine room entrance (with Chaos). After her scene, the warps up to the second airship map
   (`1@air2`) open.
5. **Upper floor:** kill all 30 monsters, 15× {{ mob(3177) }} and 15× {{ mob(3178) }}. The warp to the deck opens.
6. **Deck: talk to Fenrir.** After the scene a {{ mob(3180) }} attacks. Kill it.
7. **Talk to Iris** again. **Captain Ferlock** goes berserk and turns into {{ mob(3181) }} (3,000,000 HP). Kill
   him.
8. About 6 seconds later Chaos and the captain reappear. **Talk to Chaos** to finish the story.

### Rewards

When you finish Chaos's dialogue you get:

- **100,000 Base EXP and 65,000 Job EXP**
- **20–40 Instance Points** (+50 with {{ item(30034) }}, within the daily Instance Point limit)

The exit warp on the deck then opens and takes you back to `/navi dali02 137/82`.

!!! tip
    The EXP and points come from the dialogue with Chaos, not from the exit warp. Do not leave before you talk to
    him.
