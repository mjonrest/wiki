# Geffen Magic Tournament

## Guide

A solo instance set in the past: you travel back to Geffen during its famous Magic Tournament, qualify by beating a
Desert Wolf, then fight your way through thirteen one-on-one matches against Geffen's mages, ending with the blue
wizard Fenrir. Every match you win pays **Geffen Magic Tournament Coins**, which buy equipment from the Coin
Operator.

### How to enter

| | |
|---|---|
| **NPC** | Emmitt White in the Dimensional Gap: `/navi dali 98/141` |
| **Portal** | Dimensional Device next to him: `/navi dali 100/147` |
| **Level** | 90+ |
| **Party** | **Solo**: you must be the leader of a party with no other members |
| **Cooldown** | 23 hours per character, counted from when you enter |
| **Time limit** | 2 hours |

Talk to Emmitt White to create the instance, then use the Dimensional Device to enter.

### Walkthrough

1. **Arrival.** You appear in festival Geffen. Walk south to the fountain area; the announcer tells everyone to
   register there.
2. **Qualifying test.** Talk to the **Receptionist** (around 122/58) and choose *"How do I Register?"*. A
   {{ mob(1106) }} appears and you have **60 seconds** to kill it. If you fail, talk to her again; you can retry as
   often as you like.
3. **Registration.** Talk to the Receptionist once more after passing. A portal opens to the south (around 120/34)
   that leads into the arena.
4. **The matches.** Step up to the Academy Master in the arena to start. Each match is one-on-one:
    - **3 minutes** per match (**5 minutes** for the final).
    - If time runs out, the judges always give the win to your opponent.
    - If you die, you have **10 seconds** to be revived or the match is lost.
    - Between matches, talk to the **Tournament Healer** for a full HP/SP heal, then to the **Tournament Guide** to
      collect your coins and begin the next match.

### Leaving and other rewards

- The exit portal back to the Dimensional Gap opens at the north of Geffen (around 120/213). Walking through it gives
  **10 Instance Points** (see [Instance Points](../../content/instances.md)).
- If you are knocked out of the tournament, you are sent back to Geffen. Loki, Lydia, Iris, Chaos and Fenrir then
  wait in the town square (around 120/108); each gives you one random potion when you talk to them.
