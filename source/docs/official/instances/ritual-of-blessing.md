# Ritual of Blessing

## Guide

Ritual of Blessing is a short story memorial from the Episode 16.1 royal family storyline. You attend the ritual that blesses the new representative of the seven royal families. Heart Hunter intruders crash the ceremony, and you help the royal families drive them out.

### Requirements and entry

| | |
|---|---|
| Quest | **Attend the Ritual** (Episode 16.1 main story) |
| NPC | **Mage Nillem** at `/navi prt_lib 91/85` |
| Entrance | **Room of Consciousness** portal at `/navi prt_lib 88/90` |
| Party | Required. Only the party leader can create and enter, so this is a solo memorial; use a one-person party. |
| Time limit | 1 hour |

Talk to Nillem as party leader and pick **Enter now.** The portal next to him becomes visible. Click it and pick **Enter.**

### Walkthrough

1. You arrive at the back of the hall (`101/12`). Walk north to the ceremony at the front of the hall.
2. When you reach the royal families near `107/77`, Kronecker G. Heine begins the ceremony. Watch the scene. Just before the blessing is finished, an intruder attacks and Peter Heine falls.
3. The intruder calls in more of his group. Three {{ mob(3454) }} attack around `98-107/73-83`. Each has 10,000 HP. While you fight, the other intruders duel the royal family members around the hall.
4. Some of the royals help you if you talk to them:
    - **Jurgen Wigner** casts Increase AGI on you.
    - **Isaac Wigner** casts Blessing on you.
    - **Catherine D. Geoborg** heals you.
    - **Nyhill M. Heine** heals you and casts Kyrie Eleison when you stand next to him.
5. When all three intruders are dead, the rest of them flee. Talk to **Kronecker G. Heine** at `101/93` to finish the story.

After that, the warp at the entrance (`101/10`) takes you back to `/navi prt_lib 88/80`. It stays closed until you have spoken to Kronecker.

### Rewards

The memorial gives no items or Instance Points. Finishing the conversation with Kronecker moves your quest on from **Attend the Ritual** to **Surprise Attack**.
