## Instance Points

The repeatable trials give [Instance Points](../../content/instance-points.md) when you claim the reward from Nyar:

| Trial | Instance Points |
|---|---|
| Easy | 40-60 |
| Normal | 60-80 |
| Hard | 80-100 |

The story Final Battle gives no points. Normal and Hard each have their own cooldown.
