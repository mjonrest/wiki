# Endless Tower

A 100-floor climb for a party, ending with Nacht Sieger. Every floor has to be cleared before its gate opens.
MVPs guard every fifth floor, and the last floors throw several of them at you at once. On Miracle the tower has
five difficulty modes: harder modes give monsters more HP and damage, and pay more experience and Instance Points.
