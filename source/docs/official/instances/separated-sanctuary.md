# Separated Sanctuary

## Guide

Separated Sanctuary is a boss memorial from the Episode 20 main quest. Nyar has sealed Lasgand away in a separated space, and Aurelie sends you in to drain his power before he fully absorbs a fragment of Ymir's Heart. The whole memorial is one fight against {{ mob(21979) }}.

### Requirements and entry

| | |
|---|---|
| Quest | **Separate Sanctuary** (Episode 20 main story) |
| NPC | **Aurelie** at `/navi jor_twig 138/152` |
| Party | Required. Only the party leader can open the connection. |
| Time limit | 30 minutes |

The leader talks to Aurelie and picks **Yes! Connect with 'Separated Sanctuary'!**, then everyone picks **Please let me enter 'Separated Sanctuary'!**

### Walkthrough

1. The party leader talks to the illusion of **Aurelie** at `99/67`. She warns that killing Lasgand will probably be difficult.
2. The party leader talks to **Lasgand** at `94/84`. He breaks loose, and {{ mob(21979) }} (62,647,916 HP) appears at `96/80`.
3. Defeat him. The whole party is moved to `96/66`, and three of Nyar's alter egos appear around the spot where he fell.
4. Talk to **Aurelie** at `99/67` to finish. She sends you back to `/navi jor_twig 136/150`.

!!! warning "30-minute limit"
    The memorial closes 30 minutes after it is created. Make sure the party is ready and can bring Lasgand down within that time before the leader opens it.

### Rewards

You get these from Aurelie while your quest is at the **Separate Sanctuary** step:

| Reward | Amount |
|---|---|
| {{ item(1001254) }} | 1 |

Your quest then moves on to **The Immortal**. Aurelie tells you to take the fragment to the Ice Castle. Aurelie only opens the connection while you are at the Separate Sanctuary step, so this is a one-time run.
