# Charleston in Distress

## Guide

The robot Charleston 2 has been hacked and taken over the Charleston Factory, and you are brought in to help get it
running again. The factory is one big map. You get from task to task by following the announcements and the
minimap marks, and the run ends with a fight against the combat unit {{ mob(3124) }}.

### Entering

- **Level:** 130 or higher.
- **Quest:** talk to **Charleston** at `/navi verus04 75/114` and accept. You need her quest active **every time**
  you enter. After a clear, talk to her again to take it again (*"I'll try again if there's a sliver of hope."*).
- **Party:** you must be in a party. The **party leader** uses the **Machine** at `/navi verus04 81/112` and picks
  *Open the dimensional portal*. Then everyone picks *Enter the Charleston Factory*.
- **Cooldown:** entering gives you *Charleston Factory - Hold*. It ends at the next **04:00** server time. The
  machine says "20 hours", but the real reset is 04:00. When it has ended, click the machine once to clear it.
- **Late joining:** you can't enter once the boss is defeated.

!!! tip "Bring an Explosive Powder"
    Partway through you have to hand over 1× {{ item(6213) }}. {{ mob(3125) }} in the factory drop it, but it is
    faster to bring one with you.

Most story steps only move forward when the **party leader** talks to the NPC.

### Walkthrough

**1. Disarm the four gates**

Walk up to **Charleston 1** at the entrance. She sends you to Dr. Ve's lab north of Factory Line 3, and a Security
Robot appears. Each gate has one guard robot, and when you kill it the gate opens and the next guard spawns
somewhere else. The announcements tell you which way to go (southwest, southeast, east, then the centre). After the
4th robot dies, the way to the lab opens.

**2. Dr. Ve's lab**

The leader walks up to **Dr. Ve** and agrees to help (*"I can help."*). Then the leader talks to **Charleston 1**.

**3. Locksteps**

Seven {{ mob(3126) }} spawn around the factory, marked on your minimap. You only need to kill **3** of them.
Charleston 1 then calls you back to the lab.

**4. Generators**

Talk to **Charleston 1** again. Three **Backup Generators** are marked on the minimap. Walk onto all three, in any
order. Five Security Robots also spawn around the factory, but you do not have to kill them. When the third
generator is on, the power comes back and you are called back to the lab.

!!! tip
    If you lose the minimap marks, **Shalosh** (`1@mcd 77/167`) shows them again when you pick *I need help*.

**5. Charleston 2 runs**

The leader talks to **Charleston 1**. Charleston 2 wakes up and runs east. Follow her and talk to her. For about a
minute she sends waves of {{ mob(3128) }} plus one {{ mob(3126) }} at you, and then 3 Security Robots. Kill those
last 3 robots to end the fight, then talk to **Charleston 2** again. She heads for the Core to steal Charleston 3.

**6. The Core**

Go to the Core door in the middle of the factory (`1@mcd 132/129`). Walking up to Charleston 2 starts a scene of
about a minute. Then the leader gives **Charleston 1** one {{ item(6213) }}. She blows the door open and the
entrance to the Core appears.

**7. Boss: Charleston 3**

Inside the Core, talk to **Charleston 1** to start the last scene (about 1.5 minutes). Charleston 2 climbs into
{{ mob(3124) }} (23,671,401 HP) and attacks.

- **Every 10 seconds** one {{ mob(3128) }} spawns next to the boss.
- **Every 30 seconds** a ring of 11 {{ mob(3161) }} spawns around the arena. The bombs from the previous ring are
  removed first.

**8. Ending**

After the boss dies:

1. Talk to **Charleston 2** in the Core, and once her scene ends, talk to her again. The way out of the Core opens.
2. Go back to the lab and talk to **Charleston 2** there. Wait for the scene with Dr. Ve to finish.
3. Talk to **Dr. Ve** twice.
4. Talk to **Shalosh** (`1@mcd 77/167`) and pick *I want to go back*, then *Go back*.

### Rewards

| From | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Shalosh (leaving the instance) | 3× {{ item(6752) }}, 20–40 Instance Points (+50 with {{ item(30034) }}, within the daily Instance Point limit) |
| Charleston at `verus04 75/114`, after each clear | 1,500,000 Base EXP and 500,000 Job EXP |
| Main Computer side task (see below) | 1,000,000 Base EXP, 300,000 Job EXP and 1× {{ item(6752) }} |

Each player has to talk to Shalosh and to Charleston to get their own reward.

### Side task: Main Computer

Once you have spoken to Dr. Ve, the **Main Computer** in the lab (`1@mcd 109/219`) gives a task: kill **50**
{{ mob(3125) }}. About 100 Steps and 20 Security Robots roam the factory, and each one respawns 2–3 minutes after it
dies. Talk to the computer again to turn the task in. You can do it again after the next **04:00** server time.
