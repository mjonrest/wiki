# Drift Ice Zone

## Guide

Drift Ice Zone is a story instance from the **Episode 20 main quest** (*An Unexpected Reunion*). Past a shaky
wall in the maze you find a frozen zone full of Rgan and Jormungandr's guardians, and at the end of it an unexpected
meeting with Lasgand, Salekgandh and Bagot. There is no item reward: finishing it moves your main quest on.

### Entering

- **Requirement:** you must be on the *Where the maze ends* / *An Unexpected Reunion* step of the Episode 20 main
  story. Walk to `/navi jor_maze 140/68` and a **Shaky Wall** appears at `jor_maze 140/72`. Examine it once to
  find the traces on the floor.
- **Party:** you must be in a party. The **party leader** clicks the Shaky Wall again and picks *Create device*,
  then everyone picks *Go inside*.
- **Time limit:** the instance lasts **1 hour** and closes after 5 minutes with nobody inside.

Every party member who is on this story step gets the quest update at the end.

### Walkthrough

A minimap guide arrow points you to each next stop.

1. **Party leader:** talk to the **Rgan Bishop** at `1@twsd 314/94`. Two {{ mob(21994) }} attack. When they are
   dead, the Jormungand Guardians come to life: kill the {{ mob(21991) }} and {{ mob(21992) }}.
2. Talk to the **Rgan Shaman** at `1@twsd 319/100`. Two {{ mob(21993) }} attack. Kill them.
3. Take the portal at `1@twsd 294/115`. On the other side, 4 Rgan Warlocks attack. Kill them all to open the way
   north.
4. Take the portal at `1@twsd 220/154` and go to the **Rgan Bishop** at `1@twsd 163/190`. Talk to him: two Rgan
   Warlocks attack, then two Jormungandr Guardians, then two more Rgan Warlocks further north.
5. Talk to **Lehar** at `1@twsd 162/245` and take the portal at `1@twsd 164/260`.
6. Three Rgan Warlocks attack on the way west. Kill them, then go to **Salekgandh** at `1@twsd 48/326`.
7. Talk to Salekgandh: 5 Heart Hunters attack. Talk to him again after they are dead: 3 Rgan Warlocks attack.
8. Talk to **Bagot** at `1@twsd 46/328` once the fighting is over, then to **Lehar** at `1@twsd 58/327`.
9. A portal opens at `1@twsd 56/315`. Step in to finish. Your quest updates and you are sent to `jor_back1`.

!!! tip
    Every step waits until all monsters of the current group are dead, so check the corners before moving on
    if the next NPC does not respond.
