# Eclage Interior

## Guide

Eclage Interior is a short story instance from the **Eclage main quest** (Episode 14.2). You are sent into the
Orb's lighting room in the heart of Eclage, where suspicious creatures have broken in, and it ends with the
betrayal that leads into the next part of the story.

### Entering

- **Requirement:** you must be on the *Orb's lighting room* step of the Eclage main quest.
- **Party:** you must be in a party, and only the **party leader** can go in. The leader talks to the
  **Chief of Staff** at `/navi ecl_hub01 132/12` and picks *Enter it.* to create the instance, then clicks the
  door (*It is closed shut.*) next to him at `/navi ecl_hub01 130/15`.
- **Time limit:** you have to go through the door within **20 minutes** of talking to the Chief of Staff, and the
  instance itself closes after **20 minutes**.

!!! warning
    Both the Chief of Staff and the door only respond to the party leader. Each character has to do this step as
    party leader (a party of one works).

### Walkthrough

1. Walk towards the four **Suspicious Creatures** near the entrance. When you get close they attack: kill all
   four {{ mob(2375) }}.
2. An alarm goes off in the lighting room and more creatures appear further in. Walk up to them and eight
   {{ mob(2376) }} attack. Kill them all.
3. **Shenime** appears at `1@ecl 58/69`. Talk to her. She drains **99% of your HP** and **blinds you for 60
   seconds**, then sends you to the next room.
4. Talk to **Avant** at `1@ecl 148/97`.
5. You are moved to the room with Loki and Nydhog. Talk to **Hisie** at `1@ecl 144/27`. She heals you, your
   quest moves on to *Betrayal*, and you are sent to `ecl_in03`.

Leaving through Hisie gives **20–40 Instance Points** (+50 with {{ item(30034) }}), within the server's daily
Instance Point limit.

!!! tip
    Heal up before you talk to Shenime: you arrive in the next room with 1% HP and blinded.
