# Tomb of Remorse

## Guide

Tomb of Remorse (also called "Tomb of Confession" in its announcements) is a short three-area instance. You lay the restless dead of Princess Tiara's castle to rest, defeat {{ mob(20893) }} and finish with {{ mob(20892) }}. Its main reward is {{ item(1000367) }}, earned from four quests reported to the Repentant Marquis at the end.

### Requirements and entry

| | |
|---|---|
| Level | Base Level 220 or higher |
| Party | Required. Only the party leader can create the instance and trigger the boss events. |
| NPC | **Princess Tiara** at `/navi dali02 78/116` |
| Cooldown | Once per day. Entering starts the cooldown, which resets at 04:00 server time. |

The leader picks **Create Tomb of Remorse**, then everyone picks **Enter Tomb of Remorse**. Entering also gives you any of the four instance quests (see Rewards) that you don't already have and that aren't on cooldown.

### Walkthrough

**Area 1: The hall**

- An {{ mob(20899) }} waits north of the entrance with 12 {{ mob(20900) }}. **Only the Noble has to die.** Killing it opens a warp portal in the north (around `1@spa2 51/259`).
- Pick up the memory fragments in this area before you leave (see Rewards).

**Area 2: Tiara's chamber**

- 30 undead spawn: {{ mob(20896) }}, {{ mob(20897) }} and {{ mob(20898) }}. Kill them all.
- {{ mob(20893) }} then appears. Kill her, staying close together: party members need to be nearby to get credit for the "Tears of Sorrow" quest.
- **Sakurai** appears. The leader talks to him to play a short scene with Himmelz. A portal then opens in the upper left of the area (around `1@spa2 210/254`).

**Area 3: The castle grounds**

- 30 more undead spawn across the area: {{ mob(20894) }}, {{ mob(20895) }}, {{ mob(20896) }} and {{ mob(20897) }}. Kill them all to open the portal in the centre (around `1@spa2 57/41`).
- In the last room, the leader talks to **Sakurai** to summon {{ mob(20892) }}.
- Once Sakray dies, the **Repentant Marquis** and the exit portal appear.

### Rewards

Talk to the **Repentant Marquis** (around `1@spa2 211/81`) to turn in your quests:

| Quest (menu option) | Requirement | Reward | Cooldown after turn-in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severing Fate | Kill {{ mob(20892) }} 10 times (counts across runs) | {{ item(1000367) }} x10 | Until the next 04:00 reset |
| Tears of Sorrow | Kill {{ mob(20893) }} 8 times (counts across runs) | {{ item(1000367) }} x8 | Until the next 04:00 reset |
| Shattered Memories | The party collects all **5** Memory Fragments in this run | {{ item(1000367) }} x1 | Until the next 04:00 reset |
| Shattered Past | The party finds the special Memory Fragment near Princess Tiara's memory | {{ item(1000367) }} x1 | Until the next 04:00 reset |

Memory Fragment locations:

- **Shattered Memories (5):** `1@spa2 74/211`, `1@spa2 83/255` (area 1) and `1@spa2 215/245`, `1@spa2 230/252`, `1@spa2 201/254` (area 2)
- **Shattered Past (1):** `1@spa2 28/226` (area 1). Clicking it plays a short memory of Princess Tiara.

Each fragment can be clicked only once per run, by anyone in the party, and counts for the whole party. Every member can then claim the two fragment quests.

Leaving through the **exit portal** next to the Marquis gives **20 to 40 Instance Points** and warps you to Prontera, so claim your quest rewards first.

**Exchange:** spend {{ item(1000367) }} at the **Marquis of Remorse** at `/navi dali02 78/112`. He offers jewel, weapon and reform-material exchanges, plus the Thanatos helmet enchant.

!!! tip "Instance Points"
    Instance Points can be earned once per device for each instance run, up to 1200 per account per day. A rented {{ item(30034) }} adds 50 points per claim. Use `@instancepoints` to see your total.
