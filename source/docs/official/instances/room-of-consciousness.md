# Room of Consciousness

## Guide

Room of Consciousness takes you into the Past Memories of the Royal Family, sealed under Prontera Castle. Alongside Fenrir and Iris you fight skeleton waves and the {{ mob(3448) }}, then try to take the piece of Ymir's Heart back from {{ mob(3450) }}. You run it once during the Episode 16.1 story. After the story, Friedrich S. Heine offers it as a **daily** quest.

### Requirements and entry

| | |
|---|---|
| Story run | At the quest **Once More!** (Episode 16.1 main story) |
| Daily run | Finish the Episode 16.1 story, then take **Hope You Cannot Let Go** from **Friedrich S. Heine** at `/navi prt_cas_q 21/83` |
| NPC | **Nillem** at `/navi prt_lib_q 91/85` |
| Entrance | **Swaying Space** at `/navi prt_lib_q 88/90` |
| Party | Required. Only the party leader can create and enter, so this is a solo memorial; use a one-person party. |
| Time limit | 1 hour |
| Cooldown | Daily. After you hand in the daily quest, you cannot take it again until 04:00 server time. |

Talk to Nillem as party leader. On the daily run, pick **Connect to the Room of Consciousness**, then **Connect now.** The Swaying Space appears next to him. Click it and pick **Enter.**

### Walkthrough

**1. Skeleton waves**

1. Talk to **Fenrir** at the entrance (`103/40`), then walk north and talk to **Iris** at `100/94`.
2. Two waves of seven skeletons spawn in the hall, one after the other: three {{ mob(3446) }}, two {{ mob(3447) }} and two {{ mob(3445) }} each. The second wave appears a few seconds after the first is cleared.

**2. Renovated Amdarais**

1. Talk to **Iris** at `103/85`. The {{ mob(3448) }} (10,000,000 HP) attacks.
2. A few seconds after you first damage the boss, Fenrir and Iris take up position at one of four random spots in the hall, and Fenrir shouts **"Lure it to where I am!"** Pull the boss into Fenrir. When it touches her, Iris throws her amulet at it, and about 10 seconds later Fenrir hits it for **700,000 to 1,300,000 damage**.
3. After each hit, Fenrir and Iris leave and reappear at another random spot about 25 seconds later. Keep luring the boss into them until it dies.

**3. Bijou**

1. Talk to **Iris** at `100/90`. {{ mob(3450) }} appears. This first encounter is scripted: as soon as you damage her, she backs off.
2. Talk to **Fenrir** at `103/90`. Bijou (10,000,000 HP) comes back for the real fight. Fenrir and Iris use the same trick as against Amdarais, so lure Bijou into Fenrir for the big hits.
3. When Bijou dies, the party leader talks to **Iris** at `100/95`. You are sent back to `/navi prt_lib_q 88/83`.

!!! tip
    Fenrir's hit deals 700,000 to 1,300,000 damage, so a 10,000,000 HP boss takes at least 8 hits if you do no damage yourself. Keep your own damage on the boss while you pull it towards her.

### Rewards

**Story run:** finishing the room moves your quest from **Once More!** to **Lost Imir Heart**.

**Daily run:** killing Bijou completes **Hope You Cannot Let Go**. Report back to Friedrich S. Heine at `/navi prt_cas_q 21/83`:

| Reward | Amount |
|---|---|
| {{ item(6919) }} | 10 |
| Base / Job EXP | 400,000 / 400,000 |

Turning it in starts the daily lockout. Friedrich and Nillem will not start another run until the next 04:00 reset.
