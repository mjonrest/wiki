# Octopus Cave

## Guide

Octopus Cave is a light-hearted Malangdo memorial. A perverted octopus has been grabbing at the Starfish lady, so you cut off its four legs in the four wings of its cave and then skewer the {{ mob(2194) }} itself.

### Requirements and entry

| | |
|---|---|
| Party | Required. Only the party leader can open the cave. |
| Required item | A rented {{ item(6442) }}, needed by **every** member who enters |
| NPC | **Starfish** at `/navi mal_dun01 151/235` |
| Entrance | **Weird Entrance** at `/navi mal_dun01 153/237` |
| Time limit | 1 hour |
| Cooldown | 1 hour, starting when you enter |

The leader talks to the Starfish and picks **Ask to open the gate.** Then everyone uses the Weird Entrance and picks **Go in.**

!!! warning "Bring the skewer"
    Both the Starfish and the Weird Entrance check for a **rental** {{ item(6442) }}. Anyone without one is turned away at the entrance. The skewer comes from the {{ item(16765) }} (23 hours), {{ item(16766) }} (3 days) or {{ item(16767) }} (7 days).

### Walkthrough

**The four legs**

1. When you step forward from the entrance (`199/99`), the octopus taunts you. Four {{ mob(2193) }} spawn, one at the far end of each wing of the cave:

    | Leg | Location |
    |---|---|
    | Octopus Leg #1 | West wing, `20/114` |
    | Octopus Leg #2 | North-west wing, `88/190` |
    | Octopus Leg #3 | North-east wing, `307/215` |
    | Octopus Leg #4 | East wing, `372/131` |

2. The paths to each leg are lined with {{ mob(2192) }} henchmen, {{ mob(1068) }} and {{ mob(1784) }}. A few spots along the way set off extra ambushes when you walk over them, including one with {{ mob(2175) }} squids.
3. When you get close to a leg, it calls for help, and two more groups of henchmen spawn 5 seconds later. A warp back to the central hall (`198/99`) opens once all of that leg's henchmen are dead, or 30 seconds after you reach the leg, whichever comes first.
4. The doors in the central hall connect all four wings, so you can split the party up and take the legs in parallel.

**The Giant Octopus**

1. When the fourth leg dies, the whole party is pulled back to the central hall and a warp to the boss room opens at `198/116`.
2. The {{ mob(2194) }} (named Disgusting Octopus) waits at `199/188`. Every 10 seconds it summons either one {{ mob(2193) }} or five {{ mob(2192) }} around itself, up to 100 adds at a time.
3. Kill the octopus. Its leftover adds disappear and two exits open, at `190/208` and `198/82`.

!!! tip
    The boss keeps adding monsters every 10 seconds for as long as it is alive. Focus it down instead of clearing every add.
