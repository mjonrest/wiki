# Hey! Sweety

## Guide

Hey! Sweety is a daily from Episode 17.2. Sweety, the battle-hungry automaton in the unofficial lounge, wants a rematch,
and Manager Beta Almond lets you fight him in the twilight garden. Two daily quests are attached: beat Sweety, and
collect broken doll cores in the garden. Both pay {{ item(1000103) }} and EXP.

### Entering

**Unlocking (one time)**

1. Finish the Episode 17.2 main story up to the unofficial lounge.
2. Talk to **Manager Beta Almond** at `/navi ba_in01 28/255`, then to **Sweety** next to her at
   `/navi ba_in01 32/250` (*Conversation with Sweetie*).

**Every day**

1. Talk to **Sweety** and pick *Apply for battle*, then *I can.* This gives the hunting quest *Hey! Sweetie*
   (defeat {{ mob(20642) }} once).
2. Talk to **Almond**. While your hunting quest is open she lets you create and enter the instance. If your core
   collection is not on cooldown she also gives you the *Core collection* quest.
3. You need a **party**. The **party leader** picks *Create*, then everyone picks *Enter*.

- **Cooldowns:** each daily quest has its own cooldown that ends at the next **04:00** server time. The instance
  itself has no cooldown, but Almond only lets you in while *Hey! Sweetie* is open.
- **Inventory:** Almond and Sweety refuse to talk if you are carrying too much.
- **Time limit:** 1 hour.

### Walkthrough

1. You arrive in the lounge corridor. When the **party leader** steps forward from the entrance, Sweety and Almond
   appear, a passage to the twilight garden opens at the west end, and **16 monsters** spawn in the garden:
   11 {{ mob(20681) }} and 5 {{ mob(20699) }}.
2. **Optional, for the core quest:** go into the garden. The Heart Hunters there drop {{ item(1000226) }} (50% each
   while you have *Core collection*). There are also **9 broken Manager Beta bodies** lying around the garden;
   clicking one gives a core 60% of the time. Each body can only be searched once per instance, by anyone.
3. Talk to **Sweety** and pick *Let's go!* to start the fight. Defeat {{ mob(20642) }}.
4. Talk to **Almond** and pick *Let's wrap up.* to return to the lounge.

### Rewards

| Quest | Turn in to | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Hey! Sweetie (defeat Sweety) | Sweety | 4 {{ item(1000103) }} and 206,267 Base EXP |
| Core collection (10 {{ item(1000226) }}) | Almond | 2 {{ item(1000103) }} and 206,267 Base EXP |

!!! tip
    The garden only has 16 monsters and 9 bodies per run, so 10 cores is not guaranteed in one visit. Bodies already
    searched by a party member are empty for everyone.
