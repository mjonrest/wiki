# Nightmarish Jitterbug

## Guide

You join the White Wings guild on a trip into a time loop to find their former master Newoz. The instance is one
long map of about twenty small rooms. Each room is a fight followed by a story scene, and the run ends with
{{ mob(3073) }}. You trade the {{ item(6719) }} you collect here to Mingmin for random gear.

### Entering

- **Level:** 120 or higher.
- **Quest:** talk to **Roel** at `/navi moc_para01 26/95` and accept his request. You have to do this **before every
  run**: Runane only opens the portal while Roel's quest is active.
- **Party:** you must be in a party. The **party leader** talks to **Runane** at `/navi moc_para01 29/95` and picks
  *Open the dimensional portal*. Then everyone picks *Enter Nightmarish Jitterbug* from Runane.
- **Cooldown:** entering gives you the *Nightmarish Jitterbug: Waiting* quest. The cooldown ends at the next
  **04:00** server time. Runane's dialogue says "20 hours" and "23 hours", but the real reset is 04:00. When the
  cooldown is over, talk to Runane once to clear it.

### How a room works

1. The **party leader** talks to the NPC that starts the scene in the current room (usually Newoz). Partway through
   the scene, the door to the next room opens and that room's monsters spawn: mostly {{ mob(3069) }} and its
   stronger variants ({{ mob(3070) }}, {{ mob(3071) }}, {{ mob(3072) }}).
2. Go through and kill **every** monster in the new room.
3. About 1.5 seconds after the last one dies, the White Wings members appear there for the next scene.

Only the party leader can move the story forward. Other members can talk to the NPCs but nothing happens.

!!! tip "Hurry"
    Once the leader has finished the story once, the leader gets a **Hurry** option on each story NPC. It skips the
    dialogue and opens the next room straight away.

### Extra Jitterbugs from your answers

In some scenes the party leader picks an answer. Some answers spawn an extra {{ mob(3108) }} in the next room, so
there are more monsters to kill and more Jitterbug drops. When the leader uses **Hurry**, the answer they gave last
time is used again.

| Scene | Answer that adds a Jitterbug | Where it spawns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Room 9: Newoz asks for your favorite song | *Newoz's Red Potion (feat. Muka)* | Room 10 |
| Room 14: Mingmin asks about the voice in the dream | *She was talking with someone.* | Room 15 |
| Room 17: Aigu asks about your significant other | *We're happy.* | Room 18 |
| Room 20: your reply to Gelka | *You just made up those words to look cool!* | Room 21 |
| Room 21: how to search the maze | *Search alone.* | 2 Jitterbugs in the boss room |

### Boss: Awakened Ferre

After room 22 is cleared, the leader talks to **Newoz** in the boss room. Ferre awakens as {{ mob(3073) }} and
brings 12 Ferre with her (4 of each of the first three kinds). Once the fight starts, the door from room 22 closes,
so anyone left behind cannot come in.

- **Every 30 seconds** a Storm Gust effect lands on the boss. Every party member within **10 cells of the middle of
  the room** is **frozen for 15 seconds**, every time. Fight away from the centre or have a way to cure Freeze.
- Only Awakened Ferre has to die. When she is dead, the leader talks to **Newoz** again to open the portal to the
  exit room.

!!! warning
    The portal from the boss room to the exit does not take dead players with it. Get resurrected before you use it.

### Exit and rewards

- **Mingmin** in the exit room (`1@jtb 375/28`) runs the exchanges below.
- The **exit portal** completes Roel's quest, gives **20–40 Instance Points** (+50 with {{ item(30034) }}, within
  the daily Instance Point limit) and sends you back to `moc_para01`.
- Talk to **Roel** again after you leave to get **1,500,000 Base EXP and 500,000 Job EXP**. You get this after
  every run, not only the first one.
