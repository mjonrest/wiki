# Official Quests

Miracle loads the official Renewal quest scripts listed below. Most give their usual official rewards, and
your quest log (`Alt+U`) tracks the steps.

## Episode quest lines

The main and side quests of **Episodes 13.1, 13.2, 14.3, 15.1, 15.2, 16.1, 16.2, 17.1, 17.2, 18, 19 and 20** are
loaded, including their daily quests. Many of them unlock one of the [official instances](instances/index.md); an
instance whose level shows **—** there needs its episode quest first.

## Other quest groups

- **Illusion dungeons**: the quest series that opens the Illusion dungeons.
- **Level 200 dungeons**: the advanced quests that open the newer high level maps.
- **Garden of Time**: leads to the Hall of Life and Lake of Fire instances.
- **Town quests** for Aldebaran, Izlude, Lighthalzen, Malangdo, Malaya, Mora, Morocc, Niflheim, Rockridge, Veins,
  Dewata, Dicastes, Eclage and more.
- **Skill and job quests** that are still loaded: the Ninja and Gunslinger skill quests, the Homunculus S
  quest, Magic Books and the Pile Bunker quest.
- **Headgear quests** from 2004 to 2012, plus the cooking and juice maker quests.
