# Episode 18: Direction of Prayer

Elly's tablet leads you to Rachel, where a masked girl and a nervous priest pull you into a plot inside the Rachel Temple. Following the "gray children" through Rachel's market, you cross the Oz Labyrinth to the Gray Wolf Forest and its village, Wolfvill. There you earn the trust of the natives, trace the source of the weapons stirring up conflict, and finally uncover what was planned for the Rachel Temple.

**Level:** Base Level 170 or higher. Some repeatable content needs Base Level 200. **Starts at:** Smart Elly in the Barmil mansion (`/navi ba_in01 26/266`), then the Masked Girl in Rachel (`/navi rachel 182/176`).

## Before you start

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Base Level | 170 or higher. Smart Elly only starts the episode at 170, and the [Wolves Gathering Place](../instances/wolves-gathering-place.md) instance also checks for 170. |
| Episode 17.2 | [Episode 17.2](ep17-2.md) must be finished up to Smart Elly (step 35 on that page). At Base Level 170 she gives you {{ item(400127) }} and the quest *Towards Rachel (1)*. |
| Mini Elly | You need {{ item(400127) }} twice: **worn** on your head at the very first step (step 2), and **in your inventory but not worn** for the last two steps. The last step takes it from you. |
| Wolfvill reputation | Partway through, the story waits until you have **1,000 Wolfvill reputation** (see step 37). Expect to do several Wolfvill side quests. |
| Party | Several instances need a party, and the leader creates them: [Oz Labyrinth](../instances/oz-labyrinth.md), [Thor Military Base](../instances/thor-military-base.md), [Wolves Gathering Place](../instances/wolves-gathering-place.md), [High Priest's Villa](../instances/high-priest-s-villa.md). |

!!! tip "Episode Skip NPC"
    The custom **Episode Skip NPC** in Prontera (`/navi prontera 164/163`) can skip Episode 18 for one {{ item(1000288) }} (3rd class only). It marks the story and its side quests as finished, sets Wolfvill reputation to the maximum, and gives {{ item(400127) }}, 500 {{ item(1000405) }} and a large amount of EXP.

## After the story

- **Imril** (`/navi wolfvill 143/113`) has two daily hunts in the Oz Labyrinth once *The Labyrinth and the Merchant* is done:
    - *Oz Labyrinth 1F*: defeat 20 {{ mob(21295) }}. Reward: 4,364,880 Base / 3,000,000 Job EXP, Amethyst Fragments, +30 reputation.
    - *Oz Labyrinth 2F* (Base Level 200+): defeat 20 {{ mob(21298) }}. Reward: 11,588,320 Base / 3,000,000 Job EXP, Amethyst Fragments, +30 reputation.

    Each has a 4-hour cooldown.
- The **Ordinary Person** in Rachel (`/navi rachel 169/245`) offers repeat runs of [Wolves Gathering Place](../instances/wolves-gathering-place.md). The **Priest** next to him (`/navi rachel 167/244`) offers [Sanctuary Purification](../instances/sanctuary-purification.md). Both need Base Level 170.
- Many Wolfvill NPCs keep offering repeatable reputation quests.
