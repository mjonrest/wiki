# Episode 17.2: Sage's Legacy

After the battle in Cor, Rookie takes you through the sewers into the Barmil mansion, the home of the late sage. You help the mansion's automatic dolls, track down a mysterious body and the records of a mercenary called "H", and hunt down Elyumina. The episode ends back in Prontera Castle.

**Level:** Base Level 130 or higher. **Starts at:** Rookie at the Cor waterway (`/navi sp_cor 255/285`).

## Before you start

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Base Level | 130 or higher. Rookie will not take you before that. |
| Episode 17.1 | [Episode 17.1](ep17-1.md) must be completed (the last quest, *Pure Facts*, finished with this same Rookie). |
| Items to bring | 1 {{ item(610) }} (step 3), 10 {{ item(7110) }} and 10 {{ item(7326) }} (step 31). |
| Party | The [Twilight Garden](../instances/twilight-garden.md) instance needs a party, and the leader creates it. |

!!! tip "Episode Skip NPC"
    The custom **Episode Skip NPC** in Prontera (`/navi prontera 164/163`) can skip 17.1 and 17.2 together for one {{ item(1000287) }} (3rd class only). It also gives {{ item(400127) }} and opens Episode 18.

## Story path

### Chapter 1: Into the mansion

1. **Rookie** at the Cor waterway (`/navi sp_cor 255/285`).
    *Quest:* Ready to enter.
2. Talk to **Rookie** again and choose "Let's Go!". He sends you into the mansion sewer (`ba_pw02`).
    *Quest:* Mansion Sewer.
3. Find the collapsed person, **Dew** (`/navi ba_pw02 90/93`). He is too weak to move.
    *Quest:* Sewer Pipe Straggler.
4. Talk to him again with a {{ item(610) }} in your inventory. The leaf is used.
5. Go to `/navi ba_pw01 14/115` in the upper sewer. A scene plays.
6. **Alp** (`/navi ba_pw01 87/290`). He sends you into the mansion building (`ba_in01`).
7. **Lasis** (`/navi ba_in01 20/319`).
8. **Tamarin** in the mansion grounds (`/navi ba_maison 70/145`). You receive **401,075 Base EXP** and 5 {{ item(1000103) }}.

### Chapter 2: The repeaters

9. **Mansion Manager** (`/navi ba_maison 74/150`).
    *Quest:* Mansion's Guest.
10. Talk to the **Mansion Manager** again.
    *Quest:* Repeater Confirmed: Restaurant.
11. **Tasty** at the restaurant (`/navi ba_maison 146/109`).
    *Quest:* Parts of the repeater.
12. Hunt {{ mob(20630) }} until you have at least one {{ item(1000232) }}. Bring it to **Tasty**. All the chips you carry are taken. You receive **300,000 Base EXP** and 5 {{ item(1000103) }}.
    *Quest:* Repeater Confirmed: Riverbank.
13. **Repeater** at the riverbank (`/navi ba_maison 334/143`). You receive **300,000 Base EXP** and 5 {{ item(1000103) }}.
    *Quest:* Repeater Confirmed: Central Garden.
14. **Gabriel** in the central garden (`/navi ba_maison 245/206`). You receive **300,000 Base EXP** and 5 {{ item(1000103) }}.
    *Quest:* Repeater confirmation complete.
15. **Mansion Manager** (`/navi ba_maison 74/150`). You receive **900,000 Base EXP** and 15 {{ item(1000103) }}.
    *Quest:* Joining the company.

### Chapter 3: The story of the managers

16. **Philofontes** inside the mansion building (`/navi ba_in01 206/106`).
    *Quest:* I would like to know what it is.
17. **Dien** (`/navi ba_in01 203/103`). She sends you to four mansion managers.
    *Quest:* The story of the managers (four entries).
18. Talk to all four managers in the grounds. The order does not matter:
    - **Yunas** (`/navi ba_maison 189/56`)
    - **Skippy** (`/navi ba_maison 318/169`)
    - **Adore** (`/navi ba_maison 249/161`). She sends you on to the **Cleaning Robot** (`/navi ba_maison 357/291`), so talk to it too.
    - **Nono** (`/navi ba_maison 232/310`)
19. **Rookie** (`/navi ba_in01 202/108`). Tell him about the mysterious body and hand over the data. The answer you pick (Enterprise, Government or Mercenaries) only changes the dialogue.
20. **Ridsh** (`/navi ba_in01 208/110`).
21. **Kaya Toss** (`/navi ba_in01 207/102`).
22. **Rookie** (`/navi ba_in01 202/108`).
23. **Kaya Toss** in the grounds (`/navi ba_maison 103/92`).
24. **Dien** (`/navi ba_maison 100/90`).
25. **Kaya Toss** (`/navi ba_maison 103/92`). You receive **900,000 Base EXP** and 15 {{ item(1000103) }}.
26. **Tatio** (`/navi ba_in01 205/109`). You receive **900,000 Base EXP** and 20 {{ item(1000103) }}.

All of steps 16 to 26 show in the quest log as *I would like to know what it is*.

### Chapter 4: Tracking Elyumina

27. Walk to `/navi ba_maison 106/65`. A scene plays. Talk to **Rookie** (`/navi ba_maison 116/67`).
    *Quest:* Broken core collection.
28. Search the broken dolls around the grounds and collect **5** {{ item(1000226) }}. There are 10 bodies, and each one gives one core:

    | | | | | |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | `ba_maison 150/47` | `ba_maison 208/87` | `ba_maison 221/193` | `ba_maison 314/146` | `ba_maison 338/267` |
    | `ba_maison 275/313` | `ba_maison 207/276` | `ba_maison 131/197` | `ba_maison 64/187` | `ba_maison 70/256` |

    *Quest:* To the designated point.
29. **Elena Bolkova** (`/navi ba_maison 303/141`). The cores are taken. You receive **515,668 Base EXP** and 10 {{ item(1000103) }}.
    *Quest:* Unexpected big fish.
30. In the mansion building, the **Rebellion** guard (`/navi ba_in01 35/142`) lets you into the back room. Talk to **Elena Bolkova** (`/navi ba_in01 20/145`).
    *Quest:* Tracking Illusion.
31. **Elyumina** (`/navi ba_in01 15/147`). Bring 10 {{ item(7110) }} and 10 {{ item(7326) }}. You receive an {{ item(1000092) }}, **1,031,336 Base EXP** and 10 {{ item(1000103) }}.
    *Quest:* Strategy planning.
32. **Est** (`/navi ba_in01 51/157`).
    *Quest:* Pest Extermination.
33. With **Est**, as party leader, create and enter **[Twilight Garden](../instances/twilight-garden.md)**. Defeat {{ mob(20642) }} inside.
34. Talk to **Est** again. The radar is taken back.
    *Quest:* To the non-official lounge.
35. **Smart Elly** in the lounge (`/navi ba_in01 26/266`). You receive **1,547,004 Base EXP** and 15 {{ item(1000103) }}.

### Chapter 5: A guest at the palace

36. Walk to `/navi ba_maison 33/220` and talk to **Crux** (`/navi ba_maison 38/219`). Agree to go and he takes you to Prontera Castle. If you decline, he waits at `/navi ba_in01 48/146` to take you later.
    *Quest:* A guest.
37. A scene plays when you arrive (`prt_cas 138/342`). **Chamberlain Bell** (`/navi prt_cas 140/344`) can let you out.
38. **Nyhill M. Heine** (`/navi prt_cas 325/263`). This completes Episode 17.2.
    *Quest:* Greeting.

!!! note "Episode 18"
    At Base Level 170 or higher, **Smart Elly** (`/navi ba_in01 26/266`) has more to say after step 35. She gives you {{ item(400127) }} and starts Episode 18 (*Towards Rachel (1)*). Keep the Mini Elly: Episode 18 needs it at the start and at the very end.
