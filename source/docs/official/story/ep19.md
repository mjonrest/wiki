# Episode 19: Issgard, Land of Snow Flowers

Maram has news from the north. The guardians of Issgard have sent an invitation, and Varmundt's airship takes you past the barrier to the Ice Castle. There the Iwins are fighting the snake-like Rgans, who are being experimented on by the Illusion scientists Juncea and Bagot. Disguised as a newborn Rgan, you sneak into the Snake's Nest and uncover what the two are doing.

- **Level:** Base Level 200 or higher.
- **Starts at:** the Grey Wolf Village, `/navi wolfvill 223/83`.
- **Main hub:** the Ice Castle (`icecastle` and its interiors `icas_in`).

!!! info "Which script is loaded"
    This page follows the combined quest file for Episode 19, which is the one the server loads. The split versions (separate main, misc, daily and side quest files) are switched off in the server config, so anything that exists only in them is not available here.

## Before you start

- **Base Level 200.** Below that, the Grey Wolf Villager only tells you there are quests for level 200 and up.
- **Episode 18 finished.** The story only starts once the last step of the Episode 18 main quest is done.
- **Rgan Transformation Scroll.** For most of the episode you have to look like a Rgan. Rgans in the Snake's Nest (and the scouts outside it) throw anyone in human form back to `jor_back3`. You get your first {{ item(101162) }} from the story (step 14). Each one lasts 20 minutes. More can be bought from **Lunch** at `/navi jor_back2 250/30` for 2 {{ item(1000707) }} and 2,000 Zeny each.
- **Rgan cores.** The story asks for {{ item(1000707) }} three times: 10, 10 and 20. They drop from the Rgans around `jor_back3`, `jor_dun01` and `jor_dun02` (Primitive Rgan, Lowest Rgan, Junior Rgan, Intermediate Rgan). Save some extra to buy scrolls.
- **Free weight.** Several NPCs refuse to continue if you can't carry the reward, so keep your inventory light.
- **A party.** [Iwin Patrol](../instances/iwin-patrol.md), [Bagot Laboratory](../instances/bagot-laboratory.md) and [Confused Snake's Nest](../instances/confused-snake-s-nest.md) all need a party. A one-person party is enough.

!!! tip "Episode Skip NPC"
    The custom **Episode Skip NPC** in Prontera (`/navi prontera 164/163`) can mark Episode 19 as complete if you have an **Episode 19 Pass Ticket** (item 1001263), are a 3rd class or higher, and are under 50% weight. It gives the story's Snow Flowers, reputation and EXP plus a bonus. The catch: it only accepts characters that used the same NPC to skip Episode 18.

## Story path

### Chapter 1: The invitation

1. **Grey Wolf Villager**, Grey Wolf Village, `/navi wolfvill 223/83`. Maram, Miriam and Suad appear and ask you to take them to Varmundt's Mansion. You can also talk to **Maram** (`/navi wolfvill 220/85`) once he has appeared.
   Quest: *Guest from the North Wind 1*.
2. Walk into the main building of Varmundt's Mansion (`/navi ba_in01 375/102`). The group gathers there. Talk to **Smart Ellie** at `/navi ba_in01 384/114`.
   Quest: *Guest from the North Wind 2*.
3. Talk to **Crux** (`/navi ba_in01 386/110`). He takes you to Prontera Castle. Walk inside and talk to **Crux** at `/navi prt_cas 320/270`.
   Quest: *Guardian's Box and Invitation 1*.
4. Go into the king's room (the door at `prt_cas 174/342`) and talk to **Nihil** at `/navi prt_cas 331/343`. You receive a {{ item(1000607) }}.
   Quest: *Guardian's Box and Invitation 2*.
5. Go back to Varmundt's Mansion. A scene plays when you reach `/navi ba_maison 186/251`.
   Quest: *Guardian's Box and Invitation 3*.
6. Talk to **Navigator Ginger** (`/navi ba_in01 30/264`) and board the airship.
7. On the airship, give the invitation to **Navigator Ginger** (`/navi air_if 46/71`).
   Quest: *Guardian's Box and Invitation 4*.
8. Talk to **Lehar** on the airship (`/navi air_if 32/63`).
   Quest: *Guardian's Box and Invitation 5*.
9. Talk to **Navigator Ginger** again and choose to get off. You land at `jor_tail`. Talk to **Lehar** at `/navi jor_tail 221/53`.
   Quest: *Into the Ice*.

!!! note
    From now on Navigator Ginger on the airship and at `/navi jor_tail 216/51` flies you between Varmundt's Mansion and Issgard.

### Chapter 2: The Ice Castle

10. Head north through `jor_back1` to the Ice Castle (the castle gate is at `jor_back1 385/229`). Lehar waits along the way at `jor_back1 381/232`. Inside, talk to **Lehar** at `/navi icas_in 141/216` (castle entrance: `icecastle 213/175`).
    Quest: *Encounter in the Ice Castle*.
11. Talk to **Guardian Leon** at `/navi icas_in 138/220`. You receive 40 {{ item(1000608) }}. Leon also starts the *Purification Saint* side quest.
    Quests: *Meet the Irwin Scouts.* and *Let's meet Voglinde.*
12. Outside, talk to the **Captain** of the Eagle Patrol (`/navi icecastle 23/115`) and sign the papers.
    Quest: *Eagle Patrol*.
13. Make a party (one person is fine) and enter [Iwin Patrol](../instances/iwin-patrol.md) through the Captain. During the patrol, talk to **The mysterious young man** at `1@iwp 260/390`, near the fourth checkpoint.
    Quest: *Subject 210426 Encounter*.
14. Go to the Frozen Scale Plains and walk up to `/navi jor_back2 250/30`. The young man appears again. Talk to him. You receive a {{ item(101162) }} and 2 {{ item(1000608) }}.
    Quests: *Rgan Transformation Scroll* and *"[Standby] Lunch Menu Selection"* (the young man, now called Lunch, becomes a daily quest NPC).
15. Back at the Ice Castle, walk to `/navi icecastle 27/126`. **Horuru** appears. Talk to her.
    Quest: *Accompany Reconnaissance Report*.
16. Go to the dining hall inside the castle (door at `icas_in 83/233`) and talk to **Aurelie Petit** at `/navi icas_in 34/189`.
    Quest: *Reiji's Plan*.

### Chapter 3: Into the Snake's Nest

!!! warning "Stay transformed"
    From here on, use a {{ item(101162) }} before you talk to anyone at the Rgan base or in the Snake's Nest. Without it the Rgans send you back to `jor_back3 65/321`.

17. Go to the Rgan base at `/navi jor_back3 99/318`. Transform, then talk to **Horuru**. You receive 25 {{ item(1000608) }}.
    Quest: *Snake's Nest Infiltration*.
18. Enter the cave and talk to **Miriam** at `/navi jor_dun02 263/170`. You receive 25 {{ item(1000608) }}.
19. Follow the Rgans to the upper level (`jor_dun02` exit at `18/31` leads to `jor_nest`). Talk to the **Rgan Priest** at `/navi jor_nest 185/49`.
20. Talk to the **Rgan Bishop** at `/navi jor_nest 196/202`.
21. Talk to the **Rgan Bishop** at `/navi jor_nest 115/187`.
22. Talk to the **Rgan Bishop** at `/navi jor_nest 53/244`.
23. Walk to `/navi jor_nest 22/141` and talk to **Juncea** twice (health check, then her research). She asks for your eggshell.
24. Go back to **Miriam** at `/navi jor_dun02 263/170`. She sends you to the higher-grade hatchery.
25. Talk to the **Rgan Priest** at `/navi jor_nest 23/58`. You receive a {{ item(1000606) }}.
26. Bring the eggshell to **Juncea** (`/navi jor_nest 22/141`).
27. Talk to **Bagot** next to her (`/navi jor_nest 26/142`).
28. Talk to **Juncea** again. She gives you a Rgan name.
    Quest: *Infiltration Interim Report*.
29. Walk outside to `/navi jor_nest 82/163`. Chime's robot finds you and Lazy.
30. Go to the Illusion's airship at `/navi jor_nest 24/252` and talk to **Lazy**.
31. Go down to the lower level and talk to **Rgan Security** at `/navi jor_dun02 153/239`.
32. Walk to `/navi jor_dun02 150/269` and talk to **Miriam**. You find the humans held there.
33. Return to the Ice Castle dining hall and talk to **Aurelie Petit** (`/navi icas_in 34/189`). You receive 50 {{ item(1000608) }}, 119,725,365 Base EXP and 7,000,000 Job EXP.
    Quests: *Aircraft Destruction Operation* and *Vulnerability Investigation*. This unlocks [Airship Destruction](../instances/airship-destruction.md).

### Chapter 4: Gathering evidence

34. Back in the nest (transformed), talk to **Lazy** at `/navi jor_nest 127/207`. He gives you three places to investigate.
    Quests: *Finding Clues - Jungkea's Lab*, *Finding Clues - Baggot's Lab*, *Finding Clues - Rgan Guard*.
35. Check all six spots. The order doesn't matter.
    - **Juncea's lab:** walk in (around `jor_nest 43/141`), then search the **Pile of Papers** at `/navi jor_nest 21/195` and `/navi jor_nest 19/142`.
    - **Bagot's lab:** step on `/navi jor_nest 66/260` to be moved to `jor_dun03`, then search the **Pile of Papers** at `/navi jor_dun03 74/71` and `/navi jor_dun03 49/80`.
    - **Rgan guards:** walk to `jor_nest 177/176`, search the **Pile of Papers** at `/navi jor_nest 181/173` (you get a {{ item(1000845) }}) and listen to the **Rgan Guard** at `/navi jor_nest 173/177`.

    Quest when all six are done: *Share the clues*.
36. Report to **Lazy** (`/navi jor_nest 127/207`). The choices in this talk don't change anything.
    Quest: *Whispering Silently*.
37. Talk to **Juncea** at `/navi jor_nest 31/140`. She wants something sweet.
38. Go to the adventurers' dorm in the Ice Castle (`/navi icas_in 245/197`). Ask **Jaerorong** or **Comfort** for an {{ item(1000846) }}.
39. Give the cookie to **Juncea** (`/navi jor_nest 31/140`).
40. **Juncea** wants more: bring 1 {{ item(1000846) }} or 10 {{ item(1000707) }}.
41. Do it again: 1 {{ item(1000846) }} or 10 {{ item(1000707) }}. The Iwins only hand out cookies during step 38, so plan on bringing 10 cores each time.
42. Keep talking to **Juncea**. Show her the {{ item(1000845) }}, then listen to Bagot.
    Quest: *Accumulating Doubts*.
43. Go back to the dorm and talk to **Lazy** (`/navi icas_in 245/197`), then **Miriam** (`/navi icas_in 249/199`).
    Quest: *Intermittent difference*.
44. In Juncea's lab (transformed), check the **Unnoticed Box** at `/navi jor_nest 19/190`.
45. Take a clean tube from the **Empty Test Tube** at `/navi jor_nest 20/194`. You receive a {{ item(1000604) }}.
46. Use it on the **Unnoticed Box** again. You get a {{ item(1000605) }}.
47. A **Rgan** stops you at `/navi jor_nest 24/185` and sends you to Bagot's laboratory.

### Chapter 5: Bagot's laboratory

48. Step on `/navi jor_nest 66/260` to reach `jor_dun03` and talk to **Juncea** at `/navi jor_dun03 57/63`. She wants to talk to you alone.
49. With a one-person party, talk to **Juncea** again to create and enter [Bagot Laboratory](../instances/bagot-laboratory.md). Talk to Juncea inside and defeat her transformed form. Then talk to her once more. You are sent back to the Ice Castle (`icecastle 27/123`).
50. Talk to **Horuru** at `/navi icecastle 27/126`.
    Quest: *Chaotic Snake Nest*.
51. Go to Horuru's house (door at `icecastle 62/137`) and talk to **Horuru** at `/navi icas_in 247/116`. She takes the {{ item(1000605) }}.
52. Bring 20 {{ item(1000707) }} to **Vellgunde** (`/navi icas_in 244/117`). You receive 25 {{ item(1000608) }}.
53. Talk to **Juncea** (`/navi icas_in 243/120`).
54. Talk to **Aurelie** (`/navi icas_in 249/113`).

### Chapter 6: The confused nest

!!! warning "Point of no return"
    Once you go through the warp below for the search, you can't enter the normal 2nd floor of the Snake's Nest until the operation is finished.

55. Go to `/navi jor_dun01 8/238` and step on the warp. An Iwin scout asks if you're ready. Say yes and you enter the nest under search (`jor_que`). Talk to the **Iwin Recon** at `/navi jor_que 271/88`. You receive 25 {{ item(1000608) }}.
56. Talk to **Miriam** at `/navi jor_que 144/253`.
57. Talk to the **Intermediate Rgan** at `/navi jor_que 131/148`.
58. Walk to `/navi jor_que 36/38` and talk to **Lehar** (`jor_que 32/37`).
59. Talk to **Lehar** again to create and enter [Confused Snake's Nest](../instances/confused-snake-s-nest.md) (a party is required). Clear it. When it's over, take the warp portal Aurelie opens. It takes you to `jor_back3`.
    Quest: *Those Who Vanished*.
60. Report to **Aurelie Petit** in the dining hall (`/navi icas_in 34/189`). You receive 50 {{ item(1000608) }}. **Episode 19 is complete.**

**What the story unlocks**

- After step 12: the Ice Castle dailies. Lunch's *Select Menu for Lunch* opens after step 14.
