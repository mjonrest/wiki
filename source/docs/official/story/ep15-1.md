# Episode 15.1: Phantasmagorika

Rekenber has found ancient ruins at the end of a tunnel in Juperos and named them **Phantasmagorika** (the Verus
maps). The Eden Group joins the dig through the **Atnad Excavation Team**, led by the stubborn archaeologist Ian
Atnad, whose estranged daughter Arquien runs the Eden Group. This episode gets you registered as an excavator,
earns Ian's trust and ends when he grants you access to the closed areas, which is where
[Episode 15.2](ep15-2.md) begins.

- **Level:** Base Level 140 or higher.
- **Starts at:** the Eden Group headquarters in Morocc (`moc_para01`). Phantasmagorika Spokespersons in Prontera
  (`/navi prontera 121/77`), Morocc, Payon, Geffen and Alberta advertise the dig.

## Before you start

- **Base Level 140.**
- **Eden Group membership.** You must carry an {{ item(6219) }}, which you get by joining the Eden Group.
- For the delicacy step, bring **2 {{ item(517) }}**, **1 {{ item(513) }}** and **1 {{ item(11520) }}**.
  Mora Mandar drops from {{ mob(2132) }}. You also need about **10 {{ item(713) }}**.
- Most excavation NPCs refuse to talk to you if your bag is too full.

## Story path

### Chapter 1: Joining the Atnad Excavation Team

1. Enter the Eden Group building at Base Level 140+ with your Eden Group Mark. Secretary **Lime Evenor** greets
   you at the door, or you can find her at `/navi moc_para01 38/175`. Quest log: **Eden Group Leader**.
2. **Commander Arquien**, the Eden Group captain, in the far-left office on the second floor,
   `/navi moc_para01 133/170`. Talk through her explanation, choosing *"Please continue."* and *"Yes"*. Quest
   log: **Artnard Excavation Team 1**.
3. **Lime Evenor**, in the central room, `/navi moc_para01 174/165`, with Archaeologist Aures and Luke Lapez. Quest
   log: **Artnard Excavation Team 2**.
4. Travel to Phantasmagorika:
    - Go to the Juperos entrance on `yuno_fild07` (the Excavator Guide is at `/navi yuno_fild07 211/179`).
    - Inside Juperos, follow the Rekenber Guards east and then south to the hidden gate at
      `/navi juperos_01 242/91`.
    - Walk through `ver_eju` and the tunnel to arrive in `verus04`.
5. **Receptionist Nara**, `/navi verus04 182/168`. Choose *"I'm from the Eden Group."* You receive **300,000 Base /
   300,000 Job EXP**. Quest log: **Registered Excavator** (completed).

### Chapter 2: Proving yourself to Dr. Atnad

1. **Archaeologist Aures** (`/navi verus04 141/193`) or **Ian Atnad** (`/navi verus04 144/193`). Choose *"I came
   to help."* or *"Just here to help."*
2. **Ian Atnad** gives you **Core Collection**: bring **20 {{ item(6749) }}**. While the quest is active,
   {{ mob(3154) }}, {{ mob(3155) }} and {{ mob(3156) }} in the plaza further inside drop them at a 50% rate.
3. Bring the 20 devices to **Ian Atnad**. You receive **750,000 Base / 500,000 Job EXP**. Quest log: **Assisting
   the Excavation Team**.
4. **Archaeologist Aures** sends you to a young machinist. Quest log: **Young Blood?**
5. **Machinist Lloyd**, `/navi verus03 103/177`. Quest log: **Bottling the Energy**. Bring him **10
   {{ item(6756) }}**: click the glowing **Machine Remnants** on `verus03` (for example `/navi verus03 127/145`,
   `84/152`, `104/181`, `204/202`). Each one takes an {{ item(713) }} and gives one Cohesive Energy, then
   disappears for 30 seconds.
6. Bring the 10 Cohesive Energy to **Lloyd**. Quest log: **Good News!**
7. **Archaeologist Aures**. You receive a {{ item(6748) }}. Quest log: **Turning In Excavation Report**.
8. **Commander Louis**, `/navi verus04 179/165`. Choose *"Report excavation results."* You receive **300,000 Base
   / 300,000 Job EXP**. Quest log: **Artnard's Summon**.

### Chapter 3: The Last Exploration

1. **Ian Atnad** wants a "delicacy that is simple to eat yet juicy and refreshing". Quest log: **In Search of a
   Delicacy**.
2. **Luke Lapez**, `/navi verus03 107/177`. Quest log: **Count On Me**.
3. Bring **Luke Lapez** 2 {{ item(517) }}, 1 {{ item(11520) }} and 1 {{ item(513) }}. He makes a
   {{ item(11519) }}. Quest log: **Delicacy for Him**.
4. Give the Beef Toast to **Ian Atnad**. Quest log: **Last Exploration**.
5. Talk to **Ian Atnad** again and choose **"Converse"** (not "About the excavation project"). Choose *"What about
   the Last Exploration!"*, then *"I have no choice."*
6. Choose **"Converse"** once more. Ian grants your team access to the closed areas. Quest log: **Access
   Permitted** and **Explore Research facilities**.

The story continues directly in [Episode 15.2](ep15-2.md).

## Rewards

| Step | Reward |
|---|---|
| Register with Nara | 300,000 Base / 300,000 Job EXP |
| Core Collection (Ian) | 750,000 Base / 500,000 Job EXP |
| Excavation report (Louis) | 300,000 Base / 300,000 Job EXP |

## After the story

Once you are a registered excavator you can repeat these jobs, each on a 4-hour cooldown:

- **Passage Cleaning**: Manager Michelle (`/navi ver_eju 114/40`) or Commander Louis. Clear monsters in the tunnel
  for 1,500,000 Base / 1,000,000 Job EXP.
- **Eliminating Risks**: Commander Louis. Clear monsters in the plaza for 1,500,000 Base / 1,000,000 Job EXP.
- **Core Collection**: Ian Atnad (*"About the excavation project"*). 20 Operation Control Devices for 1,500,000
  Base / 1,000,000 Job EXP.
- **New Power Source**: Machinist Lloyd. 10 Cohesive Energy for 150,000 Base / 1,000,000 Job EXP.
- **Excavation report**: Archaeologist Aures hands out a new report after the Core Collection or New Power Source
  daily. Commander Louis pays 300,000 Base / 300,000 Job EXP for it.

Side quests in the same area:

- **To Phantasmagorika!**: Guide Elisha at `/navi yuno_fild07 216/157` and Leitner in the Rekenber building
  (`/navi lhz_in01 75/209`). It registers you for Elisha's lift straight to `verus04` and continues in
  15.2 as *Vestige*.
- **Police quests** (Police Chief Kesler, `/navi verus04 133/212`) and the **Wandering Bard** (`/navi verus03
  129/21`).
