# Episode 15.2: Memory Records

With access to the closed areas of Phantasmagorika, the Atnad Excavation Team finds **Memory Records**:
recordings left by the people who lived in the ruins. Ian Atnad and his daughter Arquien work through them
together, until Rekenber's own Chief Secretary arrives asking that their contents be kept quiet. The episode ends
with a meeting at Rekenber Corporation Headquarters in Lighthalzen.

- **Level:** Base Level 140 or higher (it continues straight on from Episode 15.1).
- **Starts at:** Phantasmagorika, `verus03`, the moment you finish [Episode 15.1](ep15-1.md).

## Before you start

- **Finish [Episode 15.1](ep15-1.md)**, through the **Access Permitted** / **Explore Research facilities** quest
  from Ian Atnad. That permit is what lets the Access Controllers on `verus03` let you through to `verus01` and
  `verus02`.
- Keep some bag space free. The record NPCs refuse to talk to you if your bag is too full.
- Plan for at least a day of real time. One step needs several Memory Record plays, and each kind of play has a
  4-hour cooldown.

## Story path

### Chapter 1: The Mysterious Piece

1. Go to the **Research facilities**: take the north-east exit of `verus03` at `/navi verus03 169/259` into
   `verus02`. Search a pile of **Strewn paper** (for example `/navi verus02 60/30`, `178/32`, `102/135`, `155/64`)
   and choose *"Check adequately."* You find a {{ item(6757) }}. Quest log: **Mysterious Piece**.
2. Show it to **Ian Atnad**, `/navi verus04 144/193`. **Keep the Memory Record.** Quest log: **Record Player**.
3. Go back to the Eden Group and talk to **Commander Arquien** (Nile), `/navi moc_para01 133/170`, twice, with the
   Memory Record in your bag. Quest log: **Piece of Memory Record**.
4. Collect **5 Memory Records** from the Strewn paper piles in `verus02`. Each pile gives one, then disappears for a
   minute.
5. **Commander Arquien** has moved to the excavation camp, `/navi verus04 172/149`. Give her the 5 Memory Records.
   Quest log: **Play Memory Record**.
6. Click the **Record player** next to her, `/navi verus04 170/150`, and listen.
7. **Commander Arquien**. Choose *"What just happened.."*. You receive **750,000 Base / 500,000 Job EXP**. Quest log:
   **Report Memory Record**.

### Chapter 2: The Laboratories

1. **Ian Atnad**, `/navi verus04 144/193`. Quest log: **Memory Records of the Laboratories**.
2. Go to the laboratories: take the north-west exit of `verus03` at `/navi verus03 52/254` into `verus01`, then
   ask **Verity** at `/navi verus01 123/181` to take you down to the underground bunker (*"Yes"*).
3. In `un_bunker`, search the glowing spots (Boxes, Under desks, Machine Remains, Thickets, Organized food trays,
   Document piles; for example `/navi un_bunker 382/335`, `87/167`, `316/243`, `50/292`, `245/360`) until you
   have **5 {{ item(6824) }}**. The exit back to `verus01` is at `/navi un_bunker 98/85`.
4. **Commander Arquien**, `/navi verus04 172/149`. She takes the 5 records.
5. Play them on the **Record player**, `/navi verus04 170/150`. You receive a {{ item(22691) }}.
6. **Commander Arquien**. Choose *"Show piece."* You receive **750,000 Base / 500,000 Job EXP**. Quest log:
   **Report Memory Record Content**. Her Memory Record dailies are now open.

### Chapter 3: Rekenber's Visit

1. **Play Memory Records for Arquien.** Talk to her and choose *"Research facilities Memory Records"*. Accept,
   collect 5 Memory Records from the Strewn paper in `verus02`, hand them in, and play them on the Record player.
   Each play gives **1,500,000 Base / 1,000,000 Job EXP** and starts a 4-hour cooldown (**One Memory a Day**).
   *"Laboratories Memory Records"* works the same way, with 5 Laboratory Memory Records from `un_bunker`, on its
   own 4-hour cooldown (**One Memory a Day 2**).
2. After **four Research facilities plays**, play **one more record of either kind**. At the end of that play
   Arquien notices a visitor at the excavation team's tent.
3. **Ian Atnad**, `/navi verus04 144/193`. Choose *"Is there something wrong?"* Rekenber's Chief Secretary asks you
   all to meet at Rekenber Corporation Headquarters.
4. Go to the Rekenber building in Lighthalzen. The **Rekenber Guard** at `/navi lhz_in01 277/234` lets members of
   the Atnad team through.
5. **Rekenber Chairman**, `/navi lhz_in01 270/257`. Choose *"What did you say?"* and *"What do you mean family's
   destruction?"*
6. **Ian Atnad** (`/navi lhz_in01 272/255`) or **Commander Arquien** (`/navi lhz_in01 272/251`). Choose *"Should
   the table be turned over?"*
7. **Chief Secretary** Tatio, `/navi lhz_in01 267/257`. You receive **4,500,000 Base / 3,000,000 Job EXP**, and
   the Phantasmagorika main quest ends.

!!! warning "Only Research facilities plays raise the counter"
    The visit in step 2 only happens after **four** *Research facilities* Memory Record plays. Laboratory plays do
    not count towards those four. Once you have done four Research plays, the next play of either kind triggers
    the visit.

## Rewards

| Step | Reward |
|---|---|
| First Memory Record (Arquien) | 750,000 Base / 500,000 Job EXP |
| Laboratory records (Arquien) | 750,000 Base / 500,000 Job EXP, {{ item(22691) }} |
| Each daily Memory Record play | 1,500,000 Base / 1,000,000 Job EXP |
| Meeting at Rekenber (Tatio) | 4,500,000 Base / 3,000,000 Job EXP |

Finishing the story opens `verus01`, `verus02` and the underground bunker, plus Arquien's two Memory Record
dailies.

## After the story

- **Memory Record dailies** (Commander Arquien): keep playing Research facilities and Laboratory records for EXP.
  A Laboratory play also gives you a Record Fragment if you don't already carry one.
- **Bunker memories**: the **Mysterious Device** at `/navi un_bunker 103/95` sends you into the bunker's past
  (`un_bk_q`) for its daily quests (*Help Her*, *Air Purifier*, *Grape Harvest*, *Subdue Hysterical Patients*,
  *Daily Necessities Transport*).
- **Vestige**: the third part of *To Phantasmagorika!*. It starts with Du at `/navi verus01 221/65` and leads
  Tamarin's party through the bunker to the [Last Room](../instances/last-room.md) instance (Base 150+). After
  that, Verity at `/navi verus01 123/181` can use a {{ item(6828) }} to take you straight to its entrance.
- **Krotzel's Request**: Krotzel at `/navi verus02 79/31`.
- The Episode 15.1 excavation dailies (Passage Cleaning, Eliminating Risks, Core Collection, New Power Source)
  stay available.
