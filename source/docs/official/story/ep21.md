# Episode 21: Age of Heroes

A dimensional rift opens on Scale Island, and Nyar uses it to send you, Lehar and Nillem back to a time before Lasgand gained his power. In the past you join the Gaebolg Resistance in Lunaforma, earn the trust of the Richard Mercenaries, the Walter fleet in Alberta and the Wigner merchants, and sneak into the Jormungandr cult's temple at Gimli. The cult's plan to bring back Jormungandr leads to a final battle. After that, Nyar brings you home to settle things with Lasgand.

- **Level:** Base Level 230 or higher.
- **Starts at:** Diver Fapa, next to the airship landing in `jor_tail`, `/navi jor_tail 233/41`.
- **Main hub:** Lunaforma (`jor_mbase` and its interior `mbase_in`), with Alberta (`jor_albe` and its interior `jalbe_in`) as the second town.

## Before you start

- **Episode 20 finished.** Diver Fapa only talks about the island once the last Episode 20 step (handing the heart fragment to Voglinde) is done.
- **Base Level 230.** Below that, Diver Fapa only tells you the quest is for level 230 and up.
- **A party.** [Gimli Infiltration](../instances/gimli-infiltration.md), [Mysterious Ghost Ship](../instances/mysterious-ghost-ship.md), [Final Battle](../instances/final-battle.md), [Secret Altar](../instances/secret-altar.md) and [Silent Sanctuary](../instances/silent-sanctuary.md) all need a party. The party leader creates each one.
- **Getting around.** Boats link the maps of the past:
    - **Walter Sailor** at `/navi jor_mbase 343/148` sails to Alberta. The **Walter Sailor** at `/navi jor_albe 201/200` sails back.
    - **Walter Sailor** at `/navi jor_mbase 161/343` sails to the Southern Raised Land (`jor_raise2`). The **Hidden Boat** at `/navi jor_raise2 100/26` sails back. The north edge of `jor_raise2` (`118/414`) leads to the Northern Raised Land (`jor_raise1`).
    - **Small Boat** at `/navi jor_mbase 121/26` goes to Scale Island (`jor_crk_p`). The **Small Boat** at `/navi jor_crk_p 75/57` comes back.
    - The [Warper](../../guide/warper.md) also lists Lunaforma, Alberta (Ep21) and the Raised Land fields.

## Story path

### Chapter 1: Through the rift

1. **Diver Fapa**, `/navi jor_tail 233/41`. Nillem, Aurelie and Lehar join you. Pick *I'll go now.* to sail to Scale Island (`jor_crk`). If you pick *I'll go later.*, talk to him again and choose *Go to Scale Island.*
   Quest: *Detected Rift on the North*.
2. Talk to **Nyar** at `/navi jor_crk 105/108`. Nyar opens a rift to the past and you land on the Scale Island of the past (`jor_crk_p`).
   Quest: *Rift on Scale Island*.
3. Talk to **Nyar** at `/navi jor_crk_p 105/108`.
   Quest: *First Meeting*.
4. Walk to `/navi jor_crk_p 100/95`. Ivan and the resistance soldiers find you, and you are moved to `luna_sf1`.
   Quest: *Lunaforma*.
5. Talk to **Ivan** (`/navi luna_sf1 258/151`). He takes you to Lunaforma (`jor_mbase 38/152`).

10. Walk to `/navi jor_mbase 313/106`. Go into the mercenary building (door at `jor_mbase 321/115`). A short scene plays at `mbase_in 289/124`.

12. Knock on Richard's door at `/navi mbase_in 113/120`. Nobody answers, so try the next door at `/navi mbase_in 113/114`. A stranger stops you there and takes you back to the office.
13. Step back into the office (`/navi mbase_in 289/124`), then fill out the form at the **Pile of Documents** (`/navi mbase_in 299/126`).
14. Talk to **Valdaris** (`/navi mbase_in 302/123`).
15. Talk to **Tan** (`/navi mbase_in 302/122`).

22. Sail to Alberta and talk to **Nillem** (`/navi jor_albe 192/209`).
    Quest: *Diligent Worker of Alberta*.

    - **Walter Sailor** at the market near the pier, `/navi jor_albe 148/235`.
    - **Walter Sailor** in the guild warehouse, `/navi jalbe_in 22/63`.

    Quest: *Small Cargo Transportation*.

When Nillem complains, pick *Stop Nillem.* If you pick *Complain to Yohan.* and then *I'll take a good rest.*, he takes the job back and you have to ask him for work and redo the deliveries. Then clean the four **Pile of Dirt** spots: `/navi jor_albe 151/210`, `/navi jor_albe 211/136`, `/navi jor_albe 167/139` and `/navi jor_albe 190/175`.
    Quest: *Thorough Port Cleaning*.
25. Report to **Yohan**.
    Quest: *Walter Officer Visit*.
26. Talk to **Iana Operta** in the guild building (`/navi jalbe_in 67/95`). Deliver her letters, in any order:
    - **Patissier Edel**, `/navi jalbe_in 126/54`.
    - **Professor Sanguin**, `/navi jalbe_in 29/28`.

    Quest: *Unexpected Letter Delivery*.
27. Report to **Iana Operta**. Maristella Walter is there too and suggests heading to Lunaforma.
    Quest: *News From Lunaforma*.

### Chapter 4: Ivan's soup kitchen

    Quest: *Ivan Wigner's Joyful Missionary Life*.

30. Have a meal with **Ivan** (`/navi jor_mbase 189/282`).
    Quest: *Ghostship Witness (1)*.
31. Talk to **Lyriq** (`/navi jor_mbase 185/311`) twice.
    Quests: *Ghostship Witness (2)* and *Ivan's Plan*.

33. Give the package to **Lalaila Wigner** (`/navi jalbe_in 56/138`).
    Quest: *Ivan's Regret*.
34. Open the **Dusty Box** on the bookcase in the office next door (`/navi jalbe_in 73/187`). Lalaila gives you a letter for Captain Maristella Walter.
    Quest: *The Receiver of the Letter*.
35. Give the letter to **Maristella Walter** (`/navi jalbe_in 68/46`).
    Quest: *Ownerless Weapon*.
36. Report to **Ivan** in Lunaforma (`/navi jor_mbase 233/277`).

### Chapter 5: Inside the Gimli temple

37. Go to the Northern Raised Land and step on `/navi jor_raise1 323/71`. You are moved to `luna_sf1`. Talk to **Ivan** (`/navi luna_sf1 49/268`).

39. Ask around the temple, in this order. Each spot moves you into a room in `luna_sf2`:
    - Step on `/navi jor_tmple1 110/190`, then talk to the **Believer** at `/navi luna_sf2 45/152`.
    - Step on `/navi jor_tmple1 186/228`, then talk to the **Believer** at `/navi luna_sf2 118/265`.
    - Step on `/navi jor_tmple1 238/268`, then talk to the **Believer** at `/navi luna_sf2 196/257`.
40. Go back to the meeting room (`jor_tmple1 186/72`) and talk to **Ivan** (`/navi luna_sf2 113/257`). The group decides to come back better prepared.
    Quest: *Recruiting Talent for Success*.
41. Talk to **Tris** in Lunaforma (`/navi jor_mbase 143/272`).

43. Talk to **Ivan** (`/navi jor_crk_p 69/85`).

    Quest: *2nd Floor Cult Infiltration*.
45. Step on `jor_raise1 323/71` again and talk to **Tan** (`/navi luna_sf1 51/267`).
46. Step on `/navi jor_tmple1 238/268` and talk to **Nadoyo** (`/navi luna_sf2 187/254`).
47. Talk to the **Guard** (`/navi luna_sf2 198/263`) to register. Talk to him again: the party leader picks *Finalize the registration.* to create [Gimli Infiltration](../instances/gimli-infiltration.md), then everyone picks *Can we head in now?* to enter. 61. Talk to three witnesses, in any order:
    - **Exhausted Soldier**, `/navi jor_mbase 189/249`. Quest: *Eyewitness 1*.
    - **Cult Member Escapee**, `/navi jor_mbase 218/298`. Quest: *Eyewitness 2*.
    - **Wounded Soldier**, `/navi jor_mbase 60/196`. Quest: *Eyewitness 3*.

    Quest when all three are done: *Gathering Eyewitness Complete!*
62. Report to **Tris** (`/navi jor_mbase 204/193`).
    Quest: *Find Tan and Nadoyo*.
63. Click the **Rock Crevice** at `/navi jor_raise1 199/190` and talk to **Nadoyo** (`/navi luna_sf1 253/257`).
    Quest: *Suspicion Location in the Temple*.
64. Click the **Decorative Door** at `/navi jor_raise1 128/326` and talk to the **Cult Priest** (`/navi luna_sf2 270/162`).
    Quest: *Serpent's Lair?*

66. Report to **Tris** (`/navi jor_mbase 204/193`). You are told to stay out of the coming battle.
    Quest: *Excluded from the Final Battle*.
67. In the central building, click the **Small Door** (`/navi mbase_in 85/141`) and talk to **Nyar** (`/navi mbase_in 64/184`).
    Quest: *I can't miss it!*
68. Click the **Decorative Door** at `jor_raise1 128/326` again, pick *Go in now.* and talk to **Nadoyo** (`/navi luna_sf2 254/162`).
    Quest: *Join the Final Battle*.
69. Go through the **Door** at `/navi luna_sf2 252/164`, walk to `/navi luna_sf2 260/45` and talk to **Heine** (`luna_sf2 260/46`).
    Quest: *Final Battle*.
70. Talk to **Heine** again to create and enter [Final Battle](../instances/final-battle.md) (the party leader creates it with *Hopi-hop spacial jump.*, then everyone picks *Begin the Final Battle*). At the end you are moved to `luna_sf2 252/147`.
    Quest: *Cat Led Adventures*.

### Chapter 9: Back to the present

71. Talk to **Nyar** (`/navi luna_sf2 254/150`). Nyar drags you to a hidden room. Talk to **Nyar** there (`/navi luna_sf2 258/257`).
    Quest: *Suspicious Book of Gimli*.
72. The party leader presses the **Suspicious Book** (`/navi luna_sf2 255/257`). This creates [Secret Altar](../instances/secret-altar.md) and a **Shining Door** appears.
    Quest: *Shining Door*.
73. Enter through the **Shining Door** (`/navi luna_sf2 250/257`).
    Quest: *Finally, A Real Mission!*
74. At the end of the altar you land at `jor_crk_p 108/105`.
    Quest: *Successfully Blocked the Source*.
75. Talk to **Nyar** (`/navi jor_crk_p 105/108`). Heine asks you to swap his tablet for a new one hidden near the Lunaforma dock.
    Quest: *Heine's Gift*.
76. Take the Small Boat to Lunaforma and search the **Bushes** at `/navi jor_mbase 129/43`. Your {{ item(1001627) }} is swapped for a {{ item(410308) }} and you are sent back to `jor_crk_p 79/69`.
    Quest: *Return to Scale Island*.
77. Talk to **Nyar** (`/navi jor_crk_p 105/108`) and pick *Return to the original time.* Back in `jor_crk`, step on `/navi jor_crk 137/138`. You are moved to `jor_twig`.
    Quest: *Time to Confirm Casualty*.
78. Talk to the **Nyar Clone** (`/navi jor_twig 122/164`).
    Quest: *Settling the Curse*. This has a 10 minute wait.
79. After the wait, talk to the **Nyar Clone** again.
    Quest: *Lasgand?*
80. Talk to the **Nyar Clone** to create (*Ok! Connect me to the 'Silent Sanctuary'!*) and enter (*Enter the 'Silent Sanctuary'!*) [Silent Sanctuary](../instances/silent-sanctuary.md). At the end you are back at `jor_twig 122/158`.
    Quest: *Butterfly Effect*.
81. Talk to the **Nyar Clone**. You are sent to `jor_crk`.
    Quest: *End of the Heroes Era*.
82. Talk to **Nyar** (`/navi jor_crk 105/108`). **Episode 21 is complete.**
