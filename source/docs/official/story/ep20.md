# Episode 20: Immortals

The Rgans hiding place is still sealed, so the search has to go wider: the Ancient Ice Gorge, the Rgans' Sacred Root under the Snake's Nest, and the drift ice of the Ice Sea. A white cat keeps turning up along the way, and Lasgand's plan to become immortal comes to a head in the Separated Sanctuary.

- **Level:** Base Level 215 or higher.
- **Starts at:** Horr, outside the Ice Castle, `/navi icecastle 27/126`.
- **Main hub:** the Ice Castle (`icecastle` and its interiors `icas_in` and `icas_in2`).

## Before you start

- **Episode 19 finished.** Every step of the Episode 19 story has to be done.
- **Base Level 215.** Horr only gives you the new exploration at level 215 or higher.
- **One run of Horr's hideout search.** After Episode 19, talk to **Horuru** in the dining hall (`/navi icas_in 32/184`) and agree to help. Then talk to **Horr** outside (`/navi icecastle 27/126`). He gives you 10 {{ item(1000842) }}. Bury them at the 10 **Marked places** in `jor_back2` (`222/123`, `49/175`, `308/99`, `113/235`, `279/307`) and `jor_back3` (`152/359`, `328/277`, `128/138`, `264/133`, `46/324`). Each try works about half the time, and Horr hands out spares one at a time. Report back to Horr to finish the run.
- **Rgan Transformation Scrolls.** Many NPCs in the Snake's Nest, the Sacred Root and the maze only talk to a Rgan. In human form they send you back to `jor_nest 38/263`. Bring several {{ item(101162) }}. Lunch at `/navi jor_back2 250/30` sells them.
- **A party.** [Canyon Exploration](../instances/canyon-exploration.md), [Drift Ice Zone](../instances/drift-ice-zone.md), [Nest of Twigs](../instances/nest-of-twigs.md) and [Separated Sanctuary](../instances/separated-sanctuary.md) need a party. A one-person party works.
- **Free weight.** Most reward NPCs check that you can carry 10 to 30 {{ item(1001217) }} first.

At `icecastle 27/126` only one NPC is shown. Once you have handed in the hideout search at least once and are Base Level 215, **Horr** stands there and starts Episode 20. You may need to step off the tile and walk back on after handing in.

!!! tip "Episode Skip NPC"
    The custom **Episode Skip NPC** in Prontera (`/navi prontera 164/163`) can complete Episode 20 for an {{ item(1001214) }} at Base Level 215. It gives 180 {{ item(1001217) }} and reputation but no EXP. It only accepts characters that used the same NPC to skip Episode 19.

## Story path

### Chapter 1: The Ancient Ice Gorge

1. **Horr**, `/navi icecastle 27/126`. Once the hideout search is handed in and you are level 215, ask him about the new search destination.
   Quest: *New Explore*.
2. Go to the adventurers' dorm in the castle (`/navi icas_in 258/196`). Everyone gathers there. Talk to **Leon** (`icas_in 262/202`).
   Quest: *Expansion of Exploration Scope*.
3. Travel through `jor_back3` → `jor_back4` (gate at `jor_back3 159/365`) → `jor_back5` (gate at `jor_back4 6/374`). Walk to `/navi jor_back5 353/73` and talk to **Lehar**. She takes you into the gorge. Talk to **Lehar** at `/navi jor_safty1 64/328`.
   Quest: *Indigenous Peoples of the Ancient Ice Gorge*.
4. Talk to **Lehar** at `/navi jor_safty1 195/327`, next to the enchanted devices.
   Quest: *Ancient Ice Gorge Exploration*.
5. Back in `jor_back5`, walk to `/navi jor_back5 353/352` and talk to **Miriam**. She takes you to the next part of the gorge. Talk to **Miriam** at `/navi jor_safty1 330/327`. You are sent back to the Ice Castle.
   Quest: *Evidence and return*.
6. Talk to **Leon** in the dorm (`/navi icas_in 262/202`).
   Quest: *Suspicious Sense*.

### Chapter 2: A spy in the castle

7. Walk to `/navi icas_in 259/216` and talk to **Horr** (`icas_in 259/219`). He gives you three places to check, in any order:
    - **Barracks** (door at `icecastle 186/222`): Horr at `/navi icas_in 113/41`. Quest: *Barracks Investigation*.
    - **Inn** (door at `icecastle 64/225`): Charr at `/navi icas_in 92/113`. Quest: *Investigate the Inn*.
    - **Back Alley** at `/navi icecastle 209/243`. Quest: *Exploring Back Alleys*.
8. Report to **Horr** (`/navi icas_in 259/219`). The answer you pick doesn't change anything.
   Quest: *The Identity of the Suspicious Device*.
9. Talk to **Vellgunde** (`/navi icas_in 260/205`).
   Quest: *Canyon Exploration*.
10. Make a one-person party and talk to **Lehar** at `/navi icecastle 201/171` to create and enter [Canyon Exploration](../instances/canyon-exploration.md). Follow Lehar and the cat through the canyon. At the end you are sent back to `icecastle 22/123`.
    Quest: *Return to the Ice Castle*.
11. Talk to **Miriam** in the dorm (`/navi icas_in 259/200`).
    Quest: *The story of the past*.
12. Talk to **Lazy** (`/navi icas_in 263/200`).
    Quests: *The Cold War and the Age of Intelligence* and *More expert than any Irwin* (this opens the dailies *Spy search* and *Intelligence information collection*).

### Chapter 3: The Sacred Root

13. In the Snake's Nest, walk to `/navi jor_nest 38/268` and talk to **Lazy** (`jor_nest 36/274`) three times. You don't need to be transformed yet.
    Quest: *Infiltrate the hideout of the Rgans*.
14. Transform and talk to **Horr** (`/navi jor_nest 40/274`). He opens the door to the snake god's root and you are moved to `jor_root1`.
15. Talk to the **Rgan Shaman** right in front of you (`/navi jor_root1 32/34`). He sees through your disguise and attacks.
    Hunt: 5 Superior Rgan Warlocks (in `jor_root1`) and 5 Church Warlocks (in `jor_root2`; `jor_root1 59/295` leads there).
16. In `jor_root2`, step on `/navi jor_root2 239/243` to reach the group's hiding spot in `jor_safty2`. Talk to **Lazy** (`/navi jor_safty2 77/362`).
17. Talk to **Miriam** (`/navi jor_safty2 81/359`).
18. Talk to **Horr** (`/navi jor_safty2 81/362`).
    Quest: *Sacred and Sacred*.
19. Go to the Sacred Root (gate at `jor_root2 25/291`) and talk to the **Rgan** at `/navi jor_sanct 162/86` twice. He wants 3 **Bamberries**.
20. Dig them up at the red **Callout** spots around `jor_root2`, for example `/navi jor_root2 43/63`, `90/50`, `118/104`, `143/79` and `234/95`. Each dig works about half the time, and a dug spot rests for a minute.
21. Bring the berries to the **Rgan** (`/navi jor_sanct 162/86`).
22. Talk to the **Rgan** at `/navi jor_sanct 185/128`. You receive a **Horsepower Recovery Device**.
23. Bring it to the **Rgan** at `/navi jor_sanct 42/105`.
    Quest: *Gaining the Trust of Rgans*.

### Chapter 4: Gaining the Rgans' trust

24. Finish five small tasks for the Rgans of the Sacred Root, in any order. The chapter moves on once all five are done.
    - *But think about it*: talk to **Gomiagand** (`/navi jor_sanct 215/84`). Answer *I think so too*, then *Tap Gamiravagand's shoulder*. Other answers end the talk and you have to start over.
    - *You are very angry*: talk to **Holesybigand** (`/navi jor_sanct 190/128`) and answer *I guess*, then *Pretend to be curious*. Then bring him 30 **Copo's Head Feathers** (from Copo in `jor_back5` and Armored Kopo in `jor_back6`).
    - *A Silent Answer*: talk to **Pamosugand** (`/navi jor_sanct 257/115`), then bring 20 **Large Gray Feathers** (from Grey Icewind in `jor_back5`) to **Dorily Logand** next to him (`/navi jor_sanct 258/117`).
    - *I've come this far*: talk to **Narsioramigand** (`/navi jor_sanct 271/156`), then find chores at the **Puddle** (`/navi jor_sanct 48/159`), the **Tower** (`/navi jor_sanct 95/152`), the **Dump** (`/navi jor_safty2 195/355`), the **Mountain of Junk** (`/navi jor_safty2 303/349`) and the **Warehouse** (`/navi jor_safty2 318/254`). You must be transformed.
    - *I'm probably a lunch box*: talk to **Eriligand** (`/navi jor_sanct 101/138`). Don't answer *No* or *I don't think so*. Those end the talk.

    Quest when all five are done: *Trust in Rgan*.
25. Talk to **Sarekgand** (`/navi jor_sanct 157/171`).
    Quest: *Where the maze ends*.
26. Talk to the **Supervisor** at the maze entrance (`/navi jor_sanct 22/283`).
    Hunt: 2 Jormungandr Guardian-P and 1 Jormungandr Guardian-Y. They live in `jor_maze` and `jor_root1`.
27. Report to the **Supervisor**.
28. In the maze (entrance at `jor_sanct 28/291`), step on `/navi jor_maze 15/47`. It moves you to a hidden room. Talk to **Miriam** (`/navi jor_safty2 190/38`).
29. Back in the maze, step on `/navi jor_maze 17/153` and talk to **Lehar** in the next hidden room (`/navi jor_safty2 310/43`).
30. Walk to `/navi jor_maze 140/68` and examine the **Shaky Wall** (`jor_maze 140/72`).
    Quest: *An Unexpected Reunion*.
31. Talk to the **Shaky Wall** again to create and enter [Drift Ice Zone](../instances/drift-ice-zone.md) (party leader creates). Follow the white cat. The exit warp takes you to `jor_back1 363/222`.
32. Talk to **Lehar** (`/navi jor_back1 365/226`).
    Quest: *Hideout Infiltration Report*.

### Chapter 5: Over the ice sea

33. Talk to **Lehar** in the Ice Castle (`/navi icas_in 226/255`). **Choice:** *It's okay, let's go now.* moves on right away. *Let's rest for a while.* means you talk to **Leon** (`/navi icas_in 233/252`) and pick *Let's go now* when you're ready.
    Quest: *Primordial Deep Sea*.
34. Outside, walk to `/navi icecastle 146/206` and talk to **Cororo** (`/navi icecastle 141/212`).
    Hunt: 5 Ice Gangu, and bring 3 {{ item(1000832) }}.
35. Bring the shells to **Cororo**.
    Hunt: 5 Shining Seaweed, and bring 3 {{ item(1000831) }}. Both monsters live in `jor_tail` and `jor_back1`.
36. Bring the stems to **Cororo**. He flies you to `jor_safty1`. Talk to **Cororo** there (`/navi jor_safty1 195/155`). He takes you to the drift ice (`jor_twice`).
37. Talk to **Cororo** at `/navi jor_twice 235/111`.
    Quest: *What is hidden between the ice*.
38. Walk north past your companions and talk to **Leon** at `/navi jor_twice 238/330`.
39. Talk to **Leon** at `/navi jor_twice 158/243`.
    Quest: *Branch's Nest*.
40. With a one-person party, talk to **Lehar** (`/navi jor_twice 164/244`) to create and enter [Nest of Twigs](../instances/nest-of-twigs.md). At the end you land at `jor_safty1 331/150`.
    Quest: *Beyond the Iceberg, Beyond the Ice Sea*.
41. Talk to **Aurelie** (`/navi jor_safty1 328/150`). She sends you back to the drift ice.
    Hunt: 5 Church Warlocks (in `jor_root2`).
42. Report to **Aurelie** at `/navi jor_twice 161/243`.
    Quest: *Deep Rooted Rgan*.

### Chapter 6: Rumors in the maze

43. Take the warp at `jor_twice 40/52` into the maze, walk to `/navi jor_maze 140/74`, transform and talk to **Lehar** (`jor_maze 142/70`).
    Quests: *Rumors Collection Between Rgan 1/2/3* and *The cat at the end of the maze*.
44. Listen to three pairs of Rgans, in any order:
    - **Hoton Lokengand** or **Sohem Garungand** at `/navi jor_maze 53/238`.
    - **Naaryugand** or **Misugozengand** at `/navi jor_maze 155/243`.
    - **Hanaungand** or **Kenguntlgand** at `/navi jor_maze 282/190`.
45. Talk to **Miriam** at `/navi jor_maze 150/33`.
46. Talk to **Miriam** at `/navi jor_maze 197/34`. She leads you to a hidden room in `jor_safty2`.
47. Talk to **Miriam** there (`/navi jor_safty2 300/148`). After the scene you are sent to `jor_back1`.
    Quest: *To the Ice Castle*.
48. Talk to **Lazy** at `/navi jor_back1 368/222`. The white cat (**Nyar**) next to him has an optional short talk.
49. Talk to **Leon** in the Ice Castle (`/navi icas_in 111/228`).
    Quest: *Finding Lehar...!*

### Chapter 7: The Undying

50. Click the **Marker** at `/navi jor_root1 32/75`. It takes you to `jor_safty2`. Talk to **Lehar** there (`/navi jor_safty2 75/258`).
    Quest: *Investigation, Investigation, Investigation*.
51. Follow Lehar's three marks in `jor_root1`. Click each first mark, then the mark it points to:
    - `/navi jor_root1 69/128`, then `/navi jor_root1 25/145`.
    - `/navi jor_root1 35/211`, then `/navi jor_root1 17/194`.
    - `/navi jor_root1 254/66`, then `/navi jor_root1 278/74`.

    Quest: *It's bullshit. What's the next place?*
52. Transform and click the **Mark** at `/navi jor_sanct 133/86`. You receive a {{ item(101162) }}.
    Quest: *Sacred Root Trend Investigation*.
53. Talk to the **Bishop** at `/navi jor_sanct 150/207`. You are moved to `jor_twig`.
    Quest: *Call of Nyar*.
54. Talk to the **White Cat** at `/navi jor_twig 187/198`.
55. Talk to the **White Cat** at `/navi jor_twig 180/123`.
56. Talk to the **White Cat** at `/navi jor_twig 142/153`.
57. Talk to **Leon** (`/navi jor_twig 138/157`).
    Quest: *Separate Sanctuary*.
58. Talk to **Aurelie** (`/navi jor_twig 138/152`) to create and enter [Separated Sanctuary](../instances/separated-sanctuary.md) (a party is required). Defeat Lasgand and talk to Aurelie inside. You receive the **Corrupted Heart Fragment of Ymir**.
    Quest: *The Immortal*.
59. Talk to **Aurelie** at `jor_twig 138/152` again. She sends you to the Ice Castle.
60. The door at `/navi icecastle 157/206` is now open. Go in and talk to **Voglinde** (`/navi icas_in2 253/118`). She takes the heart fragment. **Episode 20 is complete.**

**What the story unlocks**

- After step 40: the daily *Collect soft delicacies* from Toryoryo (`icas_in 243/66`).

## After the story

- **Side quest A** starts with the Iwin Courier at `/navi icecastle 109/241`. You need Base Level 215 and Episode 19 complete, but not this story. It leads to [Sticky Sea](../instances/sticky-sea.md) and opens more dailies (*Escargot!*, *Dangerous!*, *I hope they reconcile*).
- **Side quest B** starts with the Iwin Soldier at `/navi icecastle 46/164` once you have finished step 21.
- **Hideout search**: Horr's feeler daily from Episode 19 ends when you start Episode 20.
