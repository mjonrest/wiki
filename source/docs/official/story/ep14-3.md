# Episode 14.3: Dimensional Travel

Demon God Morocc has fled into the **Flame Basin** (`moro_vol`), a burning world inside the Dimensional Crack.
The Midgard Alliance, the Laphine of Eclage and the Dicastes have set up a joint camp there. In this episode you
follow the adventurer Tamarin into the Basin, help the camp's researchers, open the cave in the central hill
(`moro_cav`), and lead the final assault on the Temple of the Demon God.

- **Level:** Base Level 140 to start; Base Level 160 for the cave instances and the final chapter.
- **Starts at:** a pile of rags in Morocc, `/navi morocc 138/238`.

## Before you start

- **Base Level 140** to start Dimensional Travel. **Base Level 160** for Isle of Bios, Morse's Cave, Commander
  Hibba Agip's mission and the Temple of the Demon God.
- Bring **1 {{ item(528) }}** for the very first step.
- Chapter 3 is done in parties. Isle of Bios, Morse's Cave and the Temple of the Demon God are opened by a
  **party leader**.
- Several NPCs refuse to talk to you if your bag is too full. Free some weight and slots first.

## Story path

### Chapter 1: Dimensional Travel

1. **Piled Rags**, Morocc, `/navi morocc 138/238`. Choose *"Remove it."*, then *"Shake the rags."* Quest log:
   **Dimensional Travel**.
2. Talk to the rags again with a {{ item(528) }} and choose *"Give Monster's Feed."* The man underneath is
   **Tamarin**.
3. Keep talking. Choose *"Tell him the place."*, then *"Accompany him."*
4. **Tamarin**, at the oasis by the northwestern sphinx, `/navi moc_ruins 106/140`. Choose *"I'm listening."*,
   then *"Tell him you want to tag along."*
5. **Tamarin**, at the Dimensional Crack, `/navi dali 112/69`.
6. Find the **Sand Mound** on the second floor, `/navi dali02 90/62`, and choose *"Enter."* You arrive in the
   Flame Basin and Dimensional Travel completes.

!!! tip "Getting back to the Basin"
    Once you have reached the Basin, the **Sand Mound** at `/navi moro_vol 137/136` takes you back to `dali02`,
    and the dali02 mound sends you in again. Once you have spoken to Tamarin inside the Basin (the first step of
    chapter 2), the **Interdimensional Device** in Eclage (`/navi ecl_in01 38/98`) also takes you to the camp. The
    **Eclage Teleporter** at `/navi moro_vol 91/105` takes you back to Eclage.

### Chapter 2: Specimen Collection and Antidote

1. **Tamarin**, `/navi moro_vol 132/140`. Quest log: **Specimen Collection**.
2. **Barget Tiblack**, `/navi moro_vol 142/131`. He wants you to investigate **20 {{ mob(3026) }}** and bring
   **50 {{ item(6689) }}**, which drop from {{ mob(3027) }}.
3. Bring both to **Barget Tiblack**.
4. **Tamarin**, `/navi moro_vol 132/140`. Hunt **25 {{ mob(3028) }}** and collect **25 {{ item(6690) }}**.
5. Meet **Tamarin** by the big rock in the centre, `/navi moro_vol 198/179`.
6. **Barget Tiblack**, with the 25 stems still in your bag. He tells you about the noxious gas from the central
   rock. Quest log: **Antidote**.
7. **Instructor Rugen**, at the camp clinic, `/navi moro_vol 105/71`. Choose *"Talk to him."*, then *"Give Sonia
   stems."* He takes the 25 stems.
8. **Intoxicated Patient**, `/navi moro_vol 104/70`.
9. **Instructor Rugen** again. He gives you a report for Dr. Tiblack.
10. **Barget Tiblack**. You receive **1,500,000 Job EXP** and the Antidote quest completes.
11. **Tamarin** at the central rock, `/navi moro_vol 198/179`. Choose *"Enter."* to go into the cave
    (`moro_cav`).

!!! note "50 or 25 Sonias?"
    Barget's dialogue asks for 50 Sonias, but the quest only needs **25 Sonia kills and 25 Yummy Stems**.

### Chapter 3: Hunting Morocc

All three of these are party instances inside `moro_cav`, and each needs **Base Level 160**.

1. **[Isle of Bios](../instances/isle-of-bios.md)**: the **Yellow Seed** at `/navi moro_cav 50/64` (the Wandering
   Old Man at `/navi moro_cav 45/60` explains it). Clear it and talk to **Vrid** at the end. You receive a
   {{ item(6684) }}, and clearing it once unlocks Morse's Cave.
2. **[Morse's Cave](../instances/morse-s-cave.md)**: the party leader talks to the **Senior Tracker**
   (`/navi moro_cav 61/69`). Everyone enters through the **Red Flower** (`/navi moro_cav 57/69`). Quest log:
   **Pursuing Hiding Morocc**.
3. Come out and report to the **Senior Tracker**. If you defeated the {{ mob(3000) }} you also get a
   {{ item(6684) }}. Reporting is what unlocks the commander's mission, even if Morocc got away.
4. **Commander Hibba Agip**, at the Alliance camp, `/navi moro_vol 108/88`. Choose *"I'll do it."* Quest log:
   **Demon God Subjugation**.

### Chapter 4: Temple of the Demon God

1. With your party, the leader talks to **Guardian Nidhogg** at `/navi moro_cav 41/73` to open the
   **[Temple of the Demon God](../instances/temple-of-the-demon-god.md)**. Members enter through the **Yggdrasil
   Lookalike** tree at `/navi moro_cav 45/75`. Everyone who enters needs the Demon God Subjugation quest.
2. Defeat {{ mob(3097) }} inside the temple.
3. Talk to **Guardian Nidhogg** or the tree again. Quest log: **Fall of the False God**.
4. Report to **Commander Hibba Agip**, `/navi moro_vol 108/88`. On your first clear you receive an
   {{ item(22567) }}, a {{ item(6715) }} and **1,000,000 Base / 500,000 Job EXP**. Quest log: **Caged God**.
5. Bring the Darklord Soulpiece to **Guardian Nidhogg** at the Laphine camp, `/navi moro_vol 94/110`, and choose
   *"Give the Soul Shard."* She announces your victory, and **the whole server gets +10% Base, Job and quest EXP
   for about one hour**. If that bonus is already running, she asks you to come back later and keeps nothing.

## After the story

- **Temple of the Demon God, repeatable:** after the daily wait (**Caged God**, resets at 04:00 server time), Hibba Agip gives you the
  subjugation quest again. Each later clear pays an Expedition Award Box and 1,000,000 Base / 500,000 Job EXP.
- **Camp dailies** (each once a day; the cooldown resets at 04:00 server time):
    - **Instructor Igrid**, `/navi moro_vol 111/87` (Base 140+): kill {{ mob(3039) }}, {{ mob(3040) }} and
      {{ mob(3041) }} for 1,000,000 Base / 800,000 Job EXP.
    - **Adjutant Abidal**, `/navi moro_vol 110/90` (Base 160+, during Demon God Subjugation): kill
      {{ mob(3091) }} and {{ mob(3092) }} in the temple for an Expedition Award Box.
    - **Hishieh** (`/navi moro_vol 95/108`) and the **Magic Scholar** (`/navi moro_vol 98/107`): mana and orb
      tasks for players who finished the Eclage (Episode 14.2) storyline.
    - **Jeruth Hesranta**, `/navi moro_vol 136/84` (Base 140-160): Bradium box collection for players who did the
      Episode 13.3 Dicastes quests.
- **Flaming Basin and Doom Prayers**: a separate Base 150+ quest line starting at the **Base Investigator**,
  `/navi moro_vol 131/103`.

!!! note "Coming from Episode 14.2?"
    If you finished the Eclage storyline, talk to **Hishieh** and the **Magic Scholar** (Avant) at the Laphine camp
    once. That closes the 14.2 thread and unlocks their dailies.
