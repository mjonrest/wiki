# Official Content

Besides its custom systems, Miracle runs a large part of the official Renewal content. This section covers
what is switched on, read from the server's own script lists.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-castle: **[Instances](instances/index.md)**

    All {{ official_instance_count() }} official memorial dungeons, with entrance, level and cooldown.

-   :material-account-group: **[Eden Group](eden-group.md)**

    Eden Teleport Officers, starter gear and the level 100 to 140 mission boards.

-   :material-book-open-variant: **[Quests](quests.md)**

    Episode quest lines from 13 to 20, Illusion dungeons and the level 200 dungeons.

-   :material-cog: **[Official systems](systems.md)**

    Clans, item merge, mercenaries, marriage, the monster race and more.

</div>

## Switched off

These official parts are **not** loaded on Miracle:

- The official job change quests. Change jobs at the **Job Master** instead ({{ npc_where("Job Master") }}).
- The official War of Emperium castles. See [PvP & WoE](../content/pvp-woe.md) for Miracle's own WoE.
- The Izlude Arena, the God Item (Seals) quests and the airship routes.
