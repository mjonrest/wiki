# Eden Group

The Eden Group headquarters is `moc_para01`, the same map as the [Quest Room](../index.md#the-two-hubs).
Talk to any **Eden Teleport Officer** to go there. They stand in most towns:

## Starter quests and Eden gear

{{ official_npcs("npc/re/quests/eden/eden_quests.txt", only=["Instructor Boya", "Instructor Ur", "Administrator Michael", "Blacksmith Thorn", "Weapons Expert BK"]) }}

- **Instructor Boya** gives the beginner tasks for base levels 12 to 60.
- **Instructor Ur** gives the second set of tasks for base levels 60 to 99, sending you to the dispatched
  instructors around the world.
- **Administrator Michael** hands out the Eden Team uniform, hat, manteau, boots and Eden Dagger as you progress.
- **Blacksmith Thorn** trades the level III Eden weapons (dagger, staff, bow, revolver and more) and the
  Eden Team hat, manteau and boots.
