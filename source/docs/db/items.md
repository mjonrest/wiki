# Items

Tick **Also search descriptions** to find items by what their in-game description says, for example
`"perfect dodge"` or `ignore def`. Put words in quotes to match them together. The **How to get** filter lists
items by where they come from (Instance reward, Quest Shop, Cash Shop, Monster drop and so on).
