# Enchants

Every enchanter on Miracle: the enchant windows an NPC opens, the NPC enchanters that work through their own
dialogue (kind **NPC enchanter**, with the details on their guide page), and Laphine synthesis and upgrade items
that an NPC sells or gives. Search for the item you want to enchant, for example *Nebula Armor*. Open an entry to
see which items it works on and the enchants it adds, with costs and chances where the server's enchant table
has them. Miracle's own enchanters are explained on the [Enchants](../enchants/index.md) pages.
