# Vending Zone

**Prontera is a no-vending zone.** You cannot open a vending shop or a buying store anywhere in Prontera.
Trading happens in **Morroc** instead.

## Getting there

Open the [Warper](warper.md), pick **Special Areas** and then **Vending Zone (Morroc)** at the top of the list.
It drops you at `/navi morocc 156/93`.

## Autotrade shops

Shops and buying stores left on `@autotrade` in Prontera from before this change are not reopened after a server
restart. Open them again in Morroc.
