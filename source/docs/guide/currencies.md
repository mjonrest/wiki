# Currencies & Points

Miracle uses several point balances and coin items. Points are **shared by every character on your account**.
Use the [Coin Exchanger](../shops/coin-exchanger.md) to turn coins into points and back.

## Point balances

| Points | How to earn | Where to spend |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash Points** | Donations, or use a free {{ item(30014) }} (see [Free Cash Points](#free-cash-points)) | [iRO Enchanter](../enchants/iro-enchanter.md), [VIP](vip.md), Cash Shop, `@donate` |
| **Mission Points** | [Hunting Missions](../content/hunting-missions.md) (7 per mission), {{ item(30002) }} and other coins | Mission Shop, [Antiquity Gacha](../content/gacha.md) |
| **Instance Points** | Clearing [instances](../content/instances.md) (up to 1,200 a day), {{ item(30018) }} | [Instance Point Merchant](../shops/instance-point-merchant.md) |
| **Event Points** | [Automatic events](../content/events.md), {{ item(30017) }} | [Event Shop](../shops/event-shop.md), Mining tools, Hell Raid rounds, Antiquity Gacha |
| **Card Points** | Trading cards at the [Card Trader](../shops/card-trader.md) (1 point per card) | Card Point Shop |
| **Activity Points** | Being active in game: 1 point per minute | [Gacha Machine](../content/gacha.md) |
| **Ancient Tokens** | Recycling old weapons at the [Ancient Weapon Master](../shops/ancient-weapon-master.md) | Ancient weapon shop |

## Free Cash Points

Cash Points are never given straight to your account in game. You get them for free as a {{ item(30014) }}, which
adds 1,000 Cash Points when you use it. These are the places it comes from:

| Where | Chance | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| [World Boss](../content/world-boss.md) reward mail | 5% for every player who took part (one reward per computer) | 1 ~ 3 |
| [Daily Quests](../content/daily-quests.md) | One of the random rewards for each finished quest | 1 ~ 3 |
| [VIP daily reward](vip.md#daily-vip-reward) | One of the random rewards, every 18 hours | 1 |
| {{ item(30056) }} from [Hell Raid](../content/hell-raid.md#hell-scroll) | One of the 0.4% rare prizes | 1 |

The Cash Shop also sells {{ item(30012) }} to {{ item(30015) }} for the same number of Cash Points they give.

!!! info "Bound MVP Souls"
    Bound {{ item_name(30007) }} can be turned into normal, tradable ones 1:1 at the Coin Exchanger.
