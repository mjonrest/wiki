# VIP

Buy VIP from the **VIP Account** NPC in Prontera ({{ npc_where("VIP Account") }}).
It costs **500 Cash Points per day** and you choose how many days. New accounts get 7 days free with the
[freebies](new-players.md).

## Benefits

As listed by the VIP NPC:

- Character slots: 9 → 15
- Storage: 300 → 600 slots, plus the VIP storage at the Kafra
- **+50%** Base EXP, Job EXP and item drop rate
- No gemstone requirements
- Bigger and longer buffs from [Hera and the Healers](new-players.md#3-heal-and-buff-for-free)

## Daily VIP reward

While VIP is active, choose **Claim Reward** at the VIP NPC to get one random reward. You can claim again
18 hours later.
