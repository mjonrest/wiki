# New Players

## 1. Claim your freebies

Talk to the **Kafra Employee** in Prontera ({{ npc_where("Kafra Employee") }}) and pick **Claim Freebies**.
Each reward can be claimed **once per account and once per computer**.

=== "Freebies"

    - A random poring costume (top) and a random costume garment, account bound
    - 1,000,000 Zeny
    - **7 days of VIP**
    - 2× {{ item(6228) }}, 5× {{ item(6232) }}
    - 3× {{ item(30005) }}, 100× {{ item(30002) }}
    - Episode skip tickets: {{ item(1000282) }}, {{ item(1000287) }}, {{ item(1000288) }}

=== "Equipment Freebies"

    Pick one job (Knight, Crusader, Hunter, Wizard, Sage, Blacksmith, Alchemist, Rogue or Monk) and receive a full
    starter set, all account bound: a +12 enchanted weapon, +11 enchanted armor, garment and shoes, and two
    enchanted accessories. You can only claim one job's set.

=== "Limit Break Shadows"

    A +10 {{ item(1270127) }} and a +10 {{ item(1270128) }}.

## 2. Kafra services

The Prontera Kafra ({{ npc_where("Kafra Employee") }}) also offers:

| Service | What it does |
|---|---|
| Save Position | Saves you in Prontera. |
| VIP Storage | A second storage. Non-VIP players can still take items out, but not put them in. |
| Reset Skill/Stats | 5,000 Zeny for skills or stats, 9,000 Zeny for both. No limit. |
| Platinum Skill | Gives the quest skills of your job for free. |
| Renter | Gives you a cart, falcon, warg, Peco/Dragon or Mado depending on your job. |
| Promotional Code | Redeem promo codes from events and social media. |

## 3. Heal and buff for free

**Hera** ({{ npc_where("Hera") }}) heals you to full and gives Increase AGI and Blessing for 5 minutes.
You can also just type a chat message containing **heal** near her.
[VIP](vip.md) players get a much bigger package: status cures, AP, food buffs, Assumptio, Impositio,
Suffragium, Sacrament and your class Spirit, lasting 30 minutes.

Every other town has a standard **Healer** NPC as well.

## 4. Go to the Quest Room

The **Quest Area** NPC ({{ npc_where("Quest Area") }}) warps you to `moc_para01`, where most custom NPCs are.
Good first stops:

- [Paradise Merchant](../shops/paradise-merchant.md) for starter weapons and armor, paid in {{ item(30002) }}
- [Hunting Missions](../content/hunting-missions.md) to earn Mission Points and coins
- [Quest Shop](../shops/quest-shop.md) to see what your coins can buy

## 5. Skip the episode quests

If you are a 3rd class or higher, the [Episode Skip NPC](../content/episode-skip.md) completes Episodes 16 to 20
for you with a skip ticket. The freebies include the tickets for 16, 17 and 18.

## 6. Test your damage

The **Dummy Punching Bags** in south-east Prontera (around 220, 70) let you test your damage.
Type `@dummy` to warp straight to them.
