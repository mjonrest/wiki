# Hera (Healer & Helper)

**Location:** {{ npc_where("Hera") }} · she walks around the Prontera fountain

Hera heals and buffs you for free, and she can answer your questions about Miracle.

## Healing

Click Hera, or say a chat message with **heal** in it next to her (for example `Hera, heal me`). Healing only
works next to her in Prontera. She heals you to full
and gives Increase AGI and Blessing for 5 minutes. [VIP](vip.md) players get the bigger 30-minute package: status
cures, AP, food buffs, Assumptio, Impositio, Suffragium, Sacrament and your class Spirit.
See [New Players](new-players.md#3-heal-and-buff-for-free) for the details.

## Ask Hera a question

Stand near Hera in Prontera and say `Hera, <question>` in normal chat. There is no command to ask her from
somewhere else.

Examples: `Hera, when is hell raid?` · `Hera, where do I get MVP Souls?` · `Hera, is eddga alive?`

- She asks whether to answer **Only me** (private messages) or **Tell everyone** (she says it out loud to everyone
  around her). Only her answer is said in public, never your question.
- A typical answer takes a few seconds. If she needs longer, she says so; ask her anything again later and
  she gives you that answer first.
- One question at a time, **20 seconds** between questions and **30 questions a day** per account
  (characters on the same account share this). Questions can be up to 100 letters long.

### What she knows

Hera answers from this wiki, so she knows the same shops, quests, instances and systems you can read here.
She can also tell you a rough picture of what is going on right now:

| Topic | What she tells you |
|---|---|
| MVPs | For an MVP you name: alive, or roughly when it is back (within half an hour, about an hour, about N hours). Never an exact time. |
| [World Boss](../content/world-boss.md) | Awake, gate opening, fight on, just defeated, or how close the souls are to a summon |
| [Illusion of Moonlight](../special/boss-spawns.md) | How close the boss is (a long way off, getting closer, very close, ready, or out) |
| [Hell Raid](../content/hell-raid.md) and [events](../content/events.md) | Open now, running now, or the next start time |
| [WoE](../content/pvp-woe.md) | Whether it is on right now, and who owns the castles |
| Server | How many players are online, whether the weekend double rates are on, and the top 3 of this month's PvP and GvG ladders |
| Your own progress | Your instance cooldowns, instance points, daily quests, hunting mission, event / cash / PvP points, mining EXP and VIP time |

Ask "What's happening now?" for a short summary. Your own progress can lag a few minutes behind the game.

### What she won't do

- She never tells you anything about other players, and she cannot see who is online.
- She is not a GM. She cannot give items, refunds or events, and nothing she says is a promise from staff.
  For account problems, bugs or reports, contact a GM.
- She only talks about Miracle and the game.
