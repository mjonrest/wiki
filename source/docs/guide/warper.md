# Warper

The **Instant Warper** stands in every town (in Prontera at `/navi prontera 159/193`). It takes you to any town, field, dungeon or instance entrance, and its first option, **Last Warp**, sends you back to the last place you picked.

Maps from the newest content are tagged <span style="color:#00b000">**[New]**</span> in the menus.
